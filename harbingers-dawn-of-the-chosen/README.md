# Harbingers of the Ethereal Veil — Book 1: *Dawn of the Chosen*
### Fantasy-era novel by D.K. Shadow — the **first-written** work of the Reforged Universe

**Status:** unedited first draft, **incomplete** — Prologue + Chapters 1–10 (~52,000 words). The author began this, stopped ~halfway, and pivoted to *Project ForgePulse* (after the *Halo: Fall of Reach* E3 trailer). This is the high-fantasy era of the shared universe.

**Canon home:** the universe-level bible is shared with ForgePulse — see `../book-edit/01-story-bible/REFORGED-UNIVERSE-LORE.md` (§8–9 cover this era). This book answers to that canon.

**Register / epistemology:** high fantasy. Per **Clarke's Third Law**, the characters perceive advanced technology as magic/divinity — the Primordial Architects appear as gods, the Ethereal Codex as a sentient tome, and **the Dark Brethren are the Void** (the people of Meira have no way to know its true nature, so they name it for what it looks like).
