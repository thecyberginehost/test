



    -        Prologue: Dawn of the ChosenIn the beginning, there were the Primordial Architects. These divine entities wove the threads of existence, birthing realms of unimaginable splendor and complexity from the raw essence of reality. Their wisdom was timeless; their power, boundless. From their hands, all life flourished.Among their celestial creations was the Ethereal Codex. Alive and radiant, this divine artifact was crafted from the wisdom of the Architects, humming with a power that pulsed in harmony with all realms. Its leaves shimmeredwith the sacred knowledge of the universe, holding within its pages the balance of all existence.To safeguard this balance, the Architects selected the Harbingers, beings of great potential. Chosen to serve as guardians of the Codex, they were the mediators of the realms, the keepers of order, the peaceweavers. The Harbingers of the Veil, five in all, stood as the bulwark against chaos.However, in the eternal dance of light and shadow, darkness found its way. One of the Harbingers, seduced by the promise of omnipotent power, betrayed his sacred duty. His heart twisted with greed, he struck down his brethren, extinguishing their lives in his reckless pursuitto possess the Codex.But the Ethereal Codex, sentient and astute, perceived the rogue Harbinger'smalevolent intent. It slipped away, embarking on a journey across realms, striving to keep its power safe from his corrupted grasp.So, the Codex remained hidden for centuries. While the realms turned and life flourished, the Primordialconflictthat had unfolded was forgotten. The Codex stayed concealed, beyond the reach of those who sought its power for sinister motives.



       A cosmic shift occurred, stirring the Codex from its ancient slumber. The time was ripe to choose a new guardian, a beacon of balance, a new Harbinger.It was drawn to the humble village of Meira. Here, amidst the cadence of simple lives, the Codex resonated with the spirit of the village blacksmith, Amos. A soul of sincerity and untarnished strength, Amos found favor with the Codex.The tranquility of his modest existence was about to be shattered. Unbeknownst to Amos, he was chosen. The Ethereal Codex, the balance of realms, the fate of existence now rested with him. He was stepping onto a path that would awaken his dormant potential andalter the fate of all realms.And so, our tale begins...
