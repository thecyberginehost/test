Chapter 18 – An Uneasy Sacrifice
Date: December 17, 2888
Time: 1100, Galactic Standard Time (GTC)
Location: Obsidian Warship - Pillar of Anguish
In the gleaming corridors of the Obsidian Warship, The Pillar of Anguish, Eclipse leads her team through dark, narrow and immaculately clean corridors. Each surface reflects their quiet, calculated movements, illuminated by continuous soft lighting from seamless panels above. The advanced design of the ship dwarfs any technology her team has previously encountered, with interfaces humming silently and obsidian-colored walls shimmering like dark, still water.
“Stay sharp,” Eclipse commands in a low, controlled murmur. Her squad—composed of Tor and the rookie from Fireteam Valor, Royce—moves with silent precision, their specialized boots making scarcely a sound against the sleek metal floor.
As they approach a strategic intersection, Tor is handling the AdaptiScan device with deft adjustments. “The schematics our intel sent us show an interface hub just ahead,” he informs them quietly, pointing to a subtly marked panel near them.
Eclipse syncs the map displayed on Tor’s device to their tactical HUDs. “We need to tap into their network undetected. Tor, set it up. Royce, watch our six.”
Tor crouches beside a terminal that rises seamlessly from the floor, connecting the AdaptiScan with a soft click. It begins to transfer data with a low, almost inaudible whir.
Suddenly, the faint echo of footsteps signals an approaching threat. An Obsidian guard rounds the corner, his armor equipped with active camouflage that shifts and blends with the metallic hues of the corridor. He pauses, a slight tilt of his head indicating suspicion.
Royce freezes, but Eclipse is already in motion. She signals him to hold position and activates her suit’s stealth mode, cloaking her thermal signature and silencing her movements. More crucially, she engages her forearm armor plate—a specialized enhancement that tightens and reinforces her grip, courtesy of GRID. This covert operations technology is designed specifically for silent takedowns, allowing her to apply significantly more pressure without increased effort.
Eclipse glides up behind the guard, her approach silent and swift. As she reaches him, her arm snakes around his neck in a precise chokehold. The suit’s forearm armor activates with a quiet hum, tightening her grip. The guard’s muffled gasps are barely audible as the pressure increases and drops its weapon to the deck with a soft thump. It’s dark-skinned claws trying desperately to break Eclipse’s hold. Beneath her arm, subtle crunching sounds emerge as the stress on its trachea mounts. His struggles weaken, his camouflaged armor flickering in distress before it goes limp.
Eclipse lowers the guard to the floor gently, ensuring his body makes no sound upon contact. She then attaches a device to the alien’s armor that scrambles its communications and activates it’s camouflage capabilities, further ensuring their operation remains undetected.
“Clear,” she signals to Royce, her voice a mere whisper. Royce, visibly relieved, nods in understanding and releases a silent sigh of relief.
With the guard silently neutralized and their presence still concealed, Tor completes the data transfer. The AdaptiScan beeps softly, its task completed. “We’re all set,” he whispers, disconnecting the device, “That was easy enough.”
They continue to navigate through the corridors of the Pillar of Anguish, a ghostly presence in the veins of an enemy ship, their movements a testament to the precision and silence of true covert operatives.
Navigating the eerily quiet corridors of the Obsidian Warship, the stark emptiness amplifies every sound, transforming the soft hum of the ship’s life support systems into a deafening presence. Lieutenant Argus leads Fireteam Valor with heightened alertness, acutely aware of the mission's critical and perilous nature in the enemy warship.
"Stay low and keep your eyes on the shadows," Argus directs in a hushed whisper, barely audible over the commlink. The corridors, bathed in an unsettling blue light, cast long, distorted shadows that challenge their perceptions and deepen the ominous atmosphere.
Wraith, with his fluid, graceful movements that belie his formidable presence, keeps pace just behind Argus. His sharp eyes scan for any signs of the few roaming guards mentioned in the AdaptiScan report. "This silence doesn't sit right with me," he comments quietly, a hint of suspicion tinting his voice. "An Obsidian ship this size should be swarming with patrols."
As they approach a crucial junction leading to the reactor room, Argus signals for a cautious halt. The schematic on his HUD pings their location, a blinking dot amidst the sprawling digital blueprint of the warship. They need to turn left, but first, they ensure the path is clear.
Suddenly, a soft distortion in the air indicates a guard's presence—a barely visible figure with active camouflage against the metallic walls of the ship. Without a word, Wraith signals to Argus, who acknowledges with a nod. Both men press back against the wall, blending into the shadows as the guard passes by, oblivious to their presence.
Once the coast is clear, they quickly duck into a nearby storage room to reassess their strategy. The cramped space, filled with crates and technical equipment, provides cover and a momentary respite from their stealthy progression.
Argus reviews the HUD once more before turning to Wraith, his face set in a serious expression. "We need to get as many of these Obsidians back here before we detonate the nuke. It’s not just about taking out the ship; it's about ensuring we don't have to fight them outside, as well."
Wraith nods, his mind racing through potential tactics. "What if we trigger a ship-wide alert? Make them think there’s a system failure, or an incoming attack? They'll return to their posts to defend the ship."
Sergeant Kaine, who had been quietly monitoring the ship's network from his portable device, chimes in with a vital piece of information. "Captain Harris mentioned in his last report that these ships have a self-repair mechanism. We could simulate an alert indicating their self-repair system is down,” he paused, “Or let them know we’re here. I’ll have their sensors show us in a few other locations in the ship, not in proximity of our real location."
He pauses, then delves deeper into the technical aspect. "The ship’s network includes a database structure nearly identical to ours, so the exploit should work as long as the AdaptiScan can properly translate the code input and correlate it to their language and query sequences."
Kaine navigates through his device, pulling up a holographic interface that projects the network's schematic. "I’ve identified a legacy service port that's still active—it’s vulnerable.
His fingers hover over the controls as he prepares the digital payload, ready to exploit the vulnerability."
"Once I execute, there’s no turning back," Kaine warns, his tone steady, signaling his readiness and expertise. "Everyone ready?"
Argus gives a firm nod, signaling the team's readiness. "Do it," he instructs firmly.
Kaine presses the execute button, sending the malicious code into the ship’s network. Instantly, alarms blare throughout the Obsidian warship, the sound echoing ominously through the corridors as the ship’s lighting shifts to a pulsing red, casting everything in a sinister hue.
"Let’s move," Argus directs, stepping out of the storage room. "We hit the reactor room, now."
As they advance towards the reactor, the ship vibrates with the footsteps of Obsidian soldiers scrambling in response to the simulated emergency. Argus and Wraith use the chaos to their advantage, staying hidden as much as possible.
Reaching the reactor room, they confront a massive door. Wraith sets a small explosive device at its base for a silent breach. With a muted thump, a hole through the door appears, just large enough for them to file through, one at a time.
Inside, the reactor room unfolds like a cathedral of technology, dominated by towering consoles and pulsating energy cores. Argus approaches what he believes to be the main control panel, expertly navigating the holographic interface to sync their operation with Eclipse's team. His actions are precise, a result of his extensive training and experience. Argus thought it was surprisingly easy to guess his way through the interface.
As Argus finalizes the synchronization with Eclipse's team data from the control room, Kaine delivers some reassuring news. "I've installed a rootkit. We’ll be able to have intermittent access now. Obsidian cybersecurity protocols are almost non-existent outside of their periodic system updates," he reports confidently, monitoring the network activity on his device.
Kaine adds another strategic move, "And I’ve disabled their power-routing subsystem for dynamic camouflage. It’s typically used to shield critical areas during alerts. With this offline, our approach to the reactor, and Eclipse's team's movement toward the data control room should encounter minimal security interference," he paused, a sly smirk forming under his helmet’s faceplate, “I’ve also put a few random blips on their sensors to throw them off. They think we are on multiple locations on this ship.”
Appreciating the update, Argus instructs, "Good work, Kaine.” 
Argus then opens an encrypted channel to Eclipse, "Your path to regroup at the reactor room should be clear. Meet you there.”
With the path now set, Argus turns his attention back to the task at hand within the reactor room. Besides overloading the reactor to create a diversion, the critical part of their mission was to place a tactical nuclear device at a strategic location within the reactor core to ensure the destruction of the Obsidian warship. He retrieves the compact, heavily shielded device from the backside of his armor mount, its surface cold and unyielding. Positioning it carefully within the core's access point, he enables the remote activation setting, ensuring they are able to activate the tactical nuke outside of the blast radius.
“We’re on a tight clock now," Argus announces, double-checking the device’s secure placement and activation status.
As they prepare to exit the reactor room, a sudden burst of energy pulses through the area, not from their sabotage, but from an unnoticed Obsidian guard who had evaded Kaine’s network manipulations. The guard opens fire with a plasma rifle, the bright streaks of energy cutting through the dimly lit corridor where Argus and Wraith are momentarily exposed.
"Contact, front!" Wraith shouts, diving for cover behind a bulkhead. Argus rolls to the opposite side, drawing his weapon. The Obsidian's camouflage flickers as he moves, revealing his position intermittently—a deadly game of hide and seek in the ship’s pulsating red emergency lighting.
Kaine, reacting quickly, tries to flank the guard, moving stealthily along the perimeter of the room. "Cover me! I'll try to flank the son of a bitch!"
Wraith provides suppressing fire, his shots strategically aimed to keep the guard pinned. Argus peeks from his cover, firing controlled bursts toward the guard, trying to coordinate his shots with Wraith’s to maximize their chances of hitting the target.
The Obsidian guard, skilled but outnumbered, maneuvers to maintain his advantage. However, the confined space of the corridor limits it’s options, and Kaine’s stealthy approach goes undetected. In a swift, calculated move, Kaine reaches a vantage point and takes a clean shot, neutralizing the guard with a precise hit to the base of its neck as the Obsidian instantly collapses in a heap.
With the immediate threat down, Argus signals the all-clear. "Guard’s down. Let’s move out before they send reinforcements. Eclipse, we’re on our way to the rendezvous point."
Their navigation through the reactor room becomes a tense rush through the now-alerted ship. They navigate the corridors, moving quickly but cautiously, aware that the entire ship could be on them at any moment. As they converge with Eclipse’s team, the combined forces prepare for a swift extraction, knowing that the successful placement of the nuke and the ensuing chaos will soon obliterate the warship.
Their mission nearing completion, the teams rely on each other's strengths in the final moments. The strategic sabotage and cyber manipulation by Kaine, coupled with the tactical expertise of Argus and Wraith, have set the stage for a critical blow against the Obsidian fleet. 
As they attempt to exit the reactor room, the situation abruptly intensifies. A group of Obsidian guards, wielding red energy swords and advanced weaponry, appears seemingly from nowhere, effectively blocking their path. Their weapons, humming with lethal energy, are poised and ready, but none are as imposing as the figure who steps forward from their ranks. 
Distinguished by his dark charcoal armor adorned with a subtly intricate symbol—an emblem of two crossed energy swords beneath a crescent moon—the Obsidian carries himself with the unmistakable bearing of a warrior bound by deep codes of honor. He strides toward Argus, his presence commanding yet devoid of immediate hostility.
"You there, Lieutenant Argus, I presume," the Obsidian says, his English articulated with precision, his tone conveying a measured respect. The team is momentarily taken aback, not just by his fluency but by his composed acknowledgment of their ranks.
Harlan instinctively trains his weapon on the commanding Obsidian, but a discreet hand signal from Argus stays his intent to fire. Meanwhile, Eclipse's voice comes through the comms, tinged with urgency yet controlled, "I see you're in a hot spot, gents. We're on our way to flank. Keep them busy. We're still undetected."
"I am High Commander Drakken Thik'shal of the Blackguard," the Obsidian introduces himself, his voice steady and clear. "In your tongue, we might be considered akin to your Praetorians. I have made it my duty to learn about those who now stand as our adversaries."
Argus, keeping his composure under the weight of his personal grief and professional duty, responds with a measured tone, "High Commander, your forces attacked our planet and have already killed millions. Now you want to talk instead of fight?” Argus grunted, getting increasingly agitated, “What is your purpose?”
Drakken’s expression, though hidden behind his helmet, shifts subtly—a flicker of regret passes through his stance. "Lieutenant, the warriors that struck your planet are not my choice of soldier. They follow orders from my superiors, whose strategies I do not control. War is complex, even in the heart of Obsidian. We are often driven by the hands of those far from the frontlines. I regret our strategy, but in this time of war, I do not regret your loss. My position does not afford me the luxury of offering true sympathies."
“Noted.” Argus pauses, looking to his left and right, ensuring his men are ready to fight at a moment’s notice, “But what are we doing talking instead of fighting?
Drakken lets out a low, guttural chuckle, “You are the most interesting of all the Humans I have had the honor of studying before this fight. I only wanted to speak to a worthy adversary on common ground. Perhaps in another life, we would not be standing on opposing sides. But today, we are soldiers in conflicting armies, and we do what we must. And sometimes Argus, a true warrior must find another way to fight battles, to avert further loss of life. A more, diplomatic way. I believe that is your English word for coming to an agreement without the use of physical force.”
Their conversation is interrupted as Eclipse breaks through the encrypted communications channel, "We are in position, Argus. Just give us the signal, and we’ll engage the bastards."
Argus gives a slight nod, a decision hanging in a delicate balance, his finger on the pulse of a situation that could either lead to a possible significant diplomatic breakthrough or escalate into a fierce confrontation. Drakken Thik'shal, a warrior of the Obsidians, stands before him not just as an enemy but as a possible key to understanding and perhaps influencing the broader conflict dynamics. The choices made in the next moments could define the path forward.
Argus stands before Drakken Thik'shal, the weight of his grief and duty pressing heavily upon him as the High Commander speaks. His mind races, analyzing Drakken's words, searching for sincerity, for the possibility of a genuine understanding that might avert further bloodshed. Yet, a creeping suspicion gnaws at his resolve—the possibility that this encounter, however honorable it appears, might be nothing more than a calculated delay, a tactic to gain the upper hand.
As Drakken's voice echoes in the dimly lit corridor of the reactor room, Argus's eyes narrow. The loss of his family, the unprovoked attacks on his people—these memories fuel a hardened resolve. The idea of dialogue as a precursor to deceit solidifies in his mind. They are soldiers, each bound by duty, but the battlefield has no place for misplaced trust.
With a sudden shift in demeanor, Argus doesn’t speak, he reaches subtly to his combat belt and swiftly grabs a grenade, the action unnoticed by the Obsidians. Argus, grenade in hand, puts both arms behind his back as if he were in the At Ease position, “Avert loss of life through diplomacy, you say? We are well passed that. And I seriously doubt your leaders will let that happen, especially after this unprovoked assault on Human life. Your kind has killed not only the helpless civilians of our planet, but my wife and daughter as well,” he pauses as pulls the pin from a grenade, “There’s no way in hell any of us will let that stand.”
Argus slowly releases the fuse lever into his hand, so it doesn’t drop to the ground. He starts a countdown from five seconds in his head as the grenade cooks,
“And there’s no way in hell, any of us will let you live!” Argus yells as he swiftly lobs the grenade with three seconds left until detonation. The grenade arches overhead towards Drakken and his Blackguard.
Drakken reacts instantly, his warrior reflexes honed by years of conflict. With a swift movement of his armored hand, he bats the grenade away. It clatters harmlessly to a distant corner of the room, where it explodes with a muffled thud, causing minimal damage. 
Taking cover behind a nearby console, Drakken let’s out a brief one-sided smirk and whispers to himself, "Very well, Argus. I suppose I would do the same in your position,” his voice carries a mix of resignation and respect, acknowledging the inevitability of their clash. 
"Then let us proceed as warriors," he adds, drawing his energy sword, which glows with a menacingly red aura in the dim light of the reactor room.
As the battle ensues, the sound of energy weapons and rifle bursts fill the reactor room. Argus and his team take strategic cover, exchanging fire with the Blackguard. The air crackles with the intensity of the firefight, each side maneuvering for an advantage.
Eclipse and her team, guided by the integrated navigational icon Argus had relayed, maneuvered stealthily behind the Obsidian forces. The element of surprise  on their side. Tor, equipped with his specialized blowback charge gloves, calculates the perfect moment to strike. As he rounds a corner, he spots one of the Blackguard, isolated from his group. Tor raises his fists, the gloves whirring to life with a high-pitched hum.
With a swift punch, Tor releases the charge. The energy bursts forward with a deafening boom, striking the unsuspecting Blackguard squarely in the back. The force of the blow sends him flying forward, its armor cracking under the intense pressure as the Obsidian crumples to the ground.
The sudden attack throws the Blackguard into disarray, their formation breaking as they realize they are flanked. Argus seizes the opportunity, signaling his team to advance. They move with precision, each member covering the other as they push forward.
Drakken Thik'shal, recognizing the shift in the tide, issues commands to his men, trying to reorganize them into a defensive stance. Yet, the surprise and coordination of Fireteam Valor and their allies prove overwhelming.
As the intense standoff within the reactor room escalates, Argus commands his team to maintain cover behind the industrial equipment, assessing the Blackguard's now formidable shield wall that deflects their assault with apparent ease. Realizing the need for a tactical pivot, Argus turns to Kaine, hoping for a breakthrough from their tech specialist.

"There's got to be something here..." he mutters under his breath. Moments later, his eyes light up as he discovers an automated defense system originally intended to protect the reactor from internal threats—a failsafe mechanism not unlike those used in high-security Terran facilities.
"I've found something," Kaine announces, his voice a mix of relief and urgency. "There’s an internal defense grid, designed to protect the reactor. It's not connected directly to their main network, but I can access it through a backdoor in the maintenance subsystem."

Caught completely off-guard by the sudden activation of the ship's own defenses, the Blackguard scattered in disarray. Their shield wall falters as they move to evade the rapid-fire plasma bursts now targeting them. The precision and intensity of the ship’s turrets introduce chaos into their previously orderly ranks.

"Kaine, good work! Keep that system on them!" Argus shouts, seizing the opportunity to counterattack.
On the other side, Eclipse reacts swiftly, "Argus, we’re engaging now." Her team, having quietly maneuvered to a flanking position during the chaos, now advances with precision. The combined distraction and their tactical positioning allow them to effectively disrupt the Blackguard from the rear.
Tor, utilizing the chaos to his advantage, prepares his blowback charge gloves once again. This time, as he rounds on a group of disoriented Blackguard, he unleashes the kinetic blast directly into their midst, knocking several to the ground and causing chaos in their ranks.
The battle intensifies as Fireteam Valor presses the advantage, moving out from their cover to engage the scattered enemies. Harlan, reloading with another drum of ammunition, lays down a suppressive fire to cover the advance of his teammates.

Drakken, shouts further commands in his native language, attempting to rally his warriors. 
As the firefight continues, Argus remains acutely aware of their primary objective—the placement of the tactical nuke within the reactor. Despite the immediate need to engage the enemy, he keeps a vigilant eye on the mission clock, ensuring that their escape route remains secure for a timely exfiltration after delivering the decisive blow to the Obsidian warship.
