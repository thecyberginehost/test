# Cut from Book 1 (archived, not deleted)

These chapters were the original **warship-boarding finale** of Book 1
(*Project ForgePulse* — *Shadows of War*). They have been **cut** in favor
of ending the book on **Chapter 16 — "Shadows Over New Eridanus"**
(Lieutenant Charles's nuke last stand) plus the **Obsidian-POV Epilogue**.

## Why they were cut
- **No ship-board finale.** The book now ends on Charles's last stand; nobody
  boards or fights aboard the warship.
- **One nuclear device, not two.** Charles detonates the only nuke (denial of
  the weapon + maximum kills). With no second warhead, the plan to destroy the
  warship is gone — so 17 (commandeer transport, fly into the warship) and 18
  (the fight aboard, Royce's sacrifice, warship destroyed) no longer fit canon.
- **The Pillar of Anguish survives** and is left looming — it is excavating
  beneath New Eridanus for the corrupted artifact. This is now a deliberate
  series hook, paid off later, not resolved here.

## Files
- `17-A_Dish_Best_Served_Cold.docx` / `.txt` — commandeering the transport,
  flying into the warship.
- `18-An_Uneasy_Sacrifice.docx` / `.txt` — the fight aboard the *Pillar of
  Anguish*; Drakken parlay; Royce manually detonates the nuke; warship destroyed.

## Salvage notes (material worth reusing elsewhere)
- **Drakken's parlay** (Ch18) is the strongest alien dialogue in the draft —
  a model for Drakken's voice; reusable in a later Drakken/Obsidian scene.
- **Royce's sacrifice arc** (rookie → hero) was paid off here. With Ch18 cut,
  Royce now **survives Book 1** and his payoff needs a new home in a later book
  (see Discrepancy Report).
- **Kaine vs. Xa'Nar** cyberspace duel (Ch18) — reusable hacking set-piece.
