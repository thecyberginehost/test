Chapter 10 – Deployment Over New Eridanus
Date: December 16, 2888
Time: 1350 Galactic Standard Time (GTC)
Location: New Eridanus

As the STRIKE Transports reach the upper atmosphere, the view from their windows reveals a scene of utter devastation. The planet below is engulfed in chaos, a tapestry of destruction that stretches as far as the eye can see. Massive explosions dot the landscape, sending mushroom clouds billowing into the atmosphere, a stark testament to the ferocity of the invasion. The sky itself seems angry, painted in hues of red and orange, reflecting the apocalyptic state of the planet. Even the alien warships are so large, they seemingly dwarf Trident’s major cities.
The turbulence inside the transports is intense, the vehicles shaking violently as they pierce through the disturbed atmosphere. The Praetorians, strapped tightly in their seats, can only watch in horror as enemy warships descend through the clouds, their ominous silhouettes growing larger and more menacing. One massive ship already hovers over New Eridanus, casting a dark shadow over the city, and from what Lieutenant Argus could see, there was another over Titan City.
As the transports begin their plummet towards the destination, the alien forces detect their presence. Suddenly, the sky is alive with the deadly dance of energy projectiles aimed at the descending transports. The STRIKE Transports' automated systems spring into action, executing evasive maneuvers with robotic precision. Over the transport’s communications channel, a calm, artificial voice announces, "Punching it in three, two, one." With a sudden and forceful boom of acceleration, the transports dive even faster, the G-forces pressing the soldiers into their seats as they desperately try to evade the relentless barrage.
The scene outside the windows is a blur of motion, streaks of light and explosions filling their field of view, a deadly fireworks display in the sky. Then, in a heart-stopping moment, Fireteam Shadowhawk’s transport, despite its evasive efforts, is hit by a massive anti-air projectile. A blinding flash of light and a deafening explosion fill the sky as the hull of the transport is torn open.
The Praetorians in the neighboring transports witness a sight that chills them to the bone. They see their comrades from Shadowhawk being sucked out into the open sky, engulfed in flames as they fall towards the surface. An entire team of elite soldiers lost before even setting foot on the battlefield.
The loss sends a wave of shock and grief through the Praetorians, but it also hardens their resolve. They understand the stakes of their mission more than ever. The transports continue their rapid descent, navigating through the chaos with a desperate urgency. Each soldier prepares for the imminent landing, their minds focused on the task at hand, but their hearts heavy with the loss of their fellow Praetorians.
As they break through into the lower atmosphere, the transports stabilize slightly. The enemy's presence looms large, and the battle for New Eridanus – and for their very survival – is about to begin.
Inside Fireteam Valor’s STRIKE Transport, the atmosphere is thick with tension and fear. The grim spectacle of Fireteam Shadowhawk’s transport being destroyed has left its mark, casting a shadow over even the most seasoned soldiers. The rookie, visibly shaken by the horrific scene, voices his fear, his words trembling, "Holy shit! Did you see that? They... they didn't even have a chance...we’re going to get blasted out of the sky and we won’t even have a chance to fi…"
Before he could continue, Harlan, sitting across from him, reacts swiftly. With a sharp, decisive movement, he reaches across and smacks the rookie’s helmet firmly. "Get ahold of yourself!" Harlan's voice is a mix of stern command and fiery determination. "You're a goddamn Praetorian. We live. We fight. We die. That's it. Focus."
The rookie, stunned by the suddenness of Harlan's action, nods quickly, his eyes hardening as he swallows his fear. The rest of the team, witnessing this exchange, silently acknowledge Harlan's words. A hard reminder of their duty and the reality of their profession.
The cabin of the transport is now filled with a renewed sense of resolve. Each Praetorian checks their gear, mentally preparing for the battle that awaits them. The fear remains, a natural response to the unknown and the dangerous, but it is now tempered by the unyielding spirit of the Praetorian creed.
Fireteam Valor, united in their purpose, readies themselves for the imminent engagement. They are Praetorians, the elite of the elite, and they are prepared to face whatever challenges lie ahead with courage and determination.
As Fireteam Valor's STRIKE Transport plummets through the atmosphere at a blistering twelve-hundred kilometers per hour, the intense velocity pushes them past the OGDF dropships and Razor Wings below. Skimming dangerously close to the surface, they fly low, possibly evading the enemy's radar. But above them, the sky is an inferno, lit up with relentless enemy fire trying to bring them down.
In the cabin, the Praetorians brace against the tumultuous descent, their bodies tense and eyes fixed on the maelstrom outside. The transport shakes violently under the strain, the sound of anti-aircraft fire reverberating through the metal hull.
Suddenly, a catastrophic explosion rips through the air. Fireteam Honor’s transport, leading the formation, is struck by a direct hit. The impact is devastating. A blinding flash and a deafening boom fill the sky as the transport bursts into a ball of fire and debris. The aftermath is horrifying – fragments of the transport and its passengers are scattered across the sky, some colliding with the other transports in a gruesome rain of metal and flesh.
Inside Fireteam Valor's transport, the scene is one of shock and horror. The rookie, pale-faced and wide-eyed, stares at the carnage. Others wince or turn away, the brutality of what they've just witnessed etching itself into their memories.
Argus, seeing the despair and fear in his team's eyes, knows he must act to rally their spirits. He stands up, his voice cutting through the chaos. "Listen up!" he shouts. "This is what we trained for. This is why we fight! Down there is where they die!” Argus paused as he simultaneously reached above his head to pull down the emergency deployment lever, just in case they are hit with an anti-aircraft projectile, “We live! We fight! We die!”
His eyes burn with a fierce determination under his visor as he continues, "Those bastards think they can take our planet and our families. We kill them all. No goddamn mercy! Shoot anything that isn’t human! Do you get me?”
“Sir, yes, sir!” Each member of Fireteam Valor screamed in unison.
His words resonate in the cabin, igniting a fire in the hearts of his men. Faces harden, grips tighten on weapons, and a steely resolve replaces the fear. They are Praetorians – they will waiver, no matter the odds.
The transport continues its rapid descent, cutting through the sky, a lone bullet heading towards its target. The enemy fire intensifies, a relentless attempt to stop them, but the Praetorians are undeterred. They are ready to face whatever awaits them on the ground, united by a common purpose and the unyielding will to fight.
As Fireteam Valor’s STRIKE Transport nears its destination, the soldiers inside spot enemy vessels – similar in design to their own transports – hurtling towards the surface. It's a clear sign: enemy soldiers are deploying, and an immediate firefight upon landing is inevitable.
A massive concussion blast rips through the sky, catching the last two transports in its wake. The force of the explosion knocks them violently off course. Inside, the Praetorians are thrown against their restraints, the world outside a blur of spinning chaos. The transports' systems flicker and fail, their normal functions crippled by the blast.
Argus, amidst the disarray, makes a split-second decision. "Manual override!" he yells. "Opening the drop hatches, now!" The soldiers scramble to comply, their hands working frantically over the controls. With a groan of stressed metal, the hatches swing open, exposing them to the open sky.
"Activate graviton arrestors, full downward thrust on my mark!" Argus commands, his voice cutting through the roar of the wind. "Brace for high impact, and goddammit, watch for the enemy when we hit the ground. They’re waiting for us!"
The boom of the manual release echoes through the transports as the Praetorians are dropped from the sky. The graviton arrestors engage with full force, creating a jarring, yet controlled fall. They plummet towards the surface, falling from a height of four-hundred meters high. Although the graviton arrestors are working overtime to ensure the Praetorians stay controlled, the landing promises to be anything but gentle.
As they fall, enemy fire erupts from below. Alien soldiers, having spotted the descending Praetorians, open fire, their weapons spewing deadly energy projectiles into the sky. Two Praetorians from FireFireteam Ironclad are hit mid-fall by a large energy blast, their bodies erupting in fiery explosions, fragments scattering into the wind. Tessa, too, is struck in her armor’s upper left breastplate, piercing her armor and wounding her; a searing pain shoots through her body as she's knocked unconscious by the concussion of the projectile that hit her. As Tessa’s battle armor sensors detect her unconsciousness, the suit’s automated guidance system activates. The guidance system barely keeps Tessa’s graviton arrestors from flailing on the deadly plunge to the planet’s surface.
They hit the ground hard, the impact sending shockwaves through their bodies, the force of the hard drop sending kinetic energy outward from their graviton arrestors with a resonating boom. Before they can fully recover, they're under a hail of enemy fire. Argus, with battle-hardened reflexes, unclips his weapon from his breastplate holster and immediately zeroes in on an enemy soldier to his right. He fires a burst of rounds, hitting the alien squarely in the chest. The impact sends a spray of dark, viscous blood into the air, the alien's body crumpling to the ground in a heap.
Vega, immediately rushes to Tessa's side, her movements swift and purposeful amidst the chaos. As she kneels beside Tessa, she quickly checks her helmet display, which shows Tessa's vital signs. Although weak, Tessa is still alive.
Vega's hands work quickly, removing the damaged portion of Tessa's breastplate armor to assess the wound. Beneath the armor, she finds a deep gash, the edges of the wound jagged and raw. Blood seeps from the injury, staining Tessa's combat suit. Vega knows she needs to act fast.
As Vega reaches for her medical kit, she notices that Tessa's suit's auto-aid system is damaged, rendering it useless in this critical moment. Without hesitation, Vega begins to manually administer first aid. She realizes that she does not have time to clean the wound properly. She provides first-aid as best she can under the circumstances, applying pressure to stem the bleeding, and administers a temporary sealant to cover the injury.
In the midst of her life-saving efforts, an alien sniper from a building fifty meters away has them in their sights. Suddenly, a shot rings out. Vega's body jerks violently as a projectile pierces through her backplate, exiting cleanly through the center of her chest. 
Rourke, reacting instinctively, zeroes in on the sniper's position. With a vengeful precision, he fires his weapon, eliminating the alien threat with a well-placed shot. "Vega!" he yells, his voice a mix of anger and sorrow.
Rourke is momentarily frozen by the shock of losing Vega. Vega lies motionless, her death a reminder of the dangers the Praetorians face. Rourke's quick action had prevented any further casualties, but the loss of Vega weighs heavily on the him. Rourke’s foresight had not been active during that moment. He blames himself for Vega’s death.
To his left, Tessa, gasps for air as she regains consciousness. Her adrenaline is so high, she doesn’t notice her wound or her damaged battle armor. She comes to her senses and unclips her weapon. An alien notices her stumbling to her feet and erratically fires, missing all shots. Tessa engages the alien with ruthless efficiency. Her shots tear through the alien's arm, severing it cleanly. The alien screams in agony as its limb hits the ground, its weapon clutched in its severed hand, still erratically firing. Tessa doesn't hesitate, sending another round through the alien's skull, silencing it instantly.
Nearby, Rourke targets an enemy taking cover behind fallen debris. He fires, his rounds ripping through the cover and piercing the alien's head, a mist of blood and brain matter painting the wreckage.
The rookie, amid the chaos, manages to pull out a timed grenade. With a shaky yet determined hand, he lobs it towards a group of three enemy soldiers taking cover in a crater, twenty meters away. The grenade lands in their midst, and for a brief moment, there's a tense pause before it detonates with a deafening roar. The explosion is gruesome – the enemies are torn apart, their bodies disintegrating in a shower of blood and gore.
The air is thick with the smell of burnt flesh and the unfamiliar stench of alien blood. The ground is littered with the remnants of the fallen enemy, a brutal testament to the Praetorians' efficiency.
In the sky, enemy aircraft swoop down in strafing runs, their weapons blazing. The Praetorians scatter, firing upwards, their bullets piercing the hulls of the enemy ships. One of the aircraft spirals out of control, crashing to the ground in a fiery explosion that sends debris flying in all directions.
The battle is relentless, the Praetorians fighting with a grim determination, each aware that their survival and the success of their mission hang by a thread. They move as one, lethal unit trained for exactly this kind of hellish combat. They are fighting not just for their lives, but for the citizens still trapped in New Eridanus, and all those who call it home.
As Argus swiftly assesses the chaotic battlefield, he realizes the necessity of a strategic fallback. "Fireteam Valor, on me! We're going to regroup. Covering fire on my mark!" he commands. The team, well-versed in combat tactics, begins a coordinated bounding withdrawal to a nearby structure.
The building, a remnant of the city's once-thriving district, stands partially ravaged by the earlier alien assault. It’s exterior, marked with blast scars and blackened by fire, provides a stark contrast to the sleek and advanced designs of the surrounding architecture. Windows are shattered, and the facade is crumbling in certain areas, but the structure's core appears sound enough to offer temporary refuge.
During their retreat, the Praetorians engage the enemy with lethal efficiency. Harlan, spotting an alien soldier emerging from the rubble, quickly raises his rifle and fires. The shot lands squarely in the alien's torso, sending it reeling backward with a guttural cry, its dark blood splattering against the debris.
Tessa, finally realizing the pain right under her left collarbone as the adrenaline subsides, takes aim at another enemy soldier attempting to flank them, her arms shaking as she lifts her weapon. She squeezes the trigger, and her bullets tear through the alien's leg, causing it to collapse with a shriek of agony. She finishes it with a precise shot to the head, silencing it permanently.
The rookie spots two more enemy combatants advancing. He steadies his aim and fires in quick succession. The first alien is hit in the shoulder, spinning it around before a follow-up shot finds its head. The second alien is struck in the chest, the impact lifting it off its feet before it crashes to the ground lifelessly.
As the team reaches the building, they swiftly clear the entrance, ensuring no enemy remains inside. The interior is a shadow of its former self. Charred furniture, broken glass, and debris litter the floor, a ghostly reminder of the people who once occupied this space. The air is thick with the smell of smoke and the lingering tang of alien blood.
Inside, Lieutenant Charles quickly organizes the remaining Ironclad members to secure the south side. They set up defensive positions, using broken furniture and wall fragments as makeshift cover.
On the north side, Argus and Fireteam Valor establish their stronghold. They keep their weapons trained on the shattered windows and the broken doorway, ready for any enemy incursion.
Despite their reduced numbers, the Praetorians' resolve remains unbroken. They are a dwindling force, but each is a formidable fighter in their own right. The team members check their ammunition and prepare for the next confrontation. They are battered, but not beaten. Their spirits fueled by the urgency of their mission and the memory of their fallen comrades.
In this improvised fortress, amidst the ruins of a city under siege, they wait, ready to defend their position and counterattack when the opportunity arises. The battle for New Eridanus is far from over, and the Praetorians are determined to fight to the bitter end.
As Fireteam Valor settles into their makeshift stronghold, the radio crackles to life. "Valor Six, this is Pegasus Two, over," comes the pilot's voice, tense but clear over the comms.
Argus quickly grabs the radio. "Pegasus Two, this is Valor Six, go ahead, over."
The pilot's voice returns with urgency, "ETA to your position is ten minutes. Is there a secure landing zone? Over."
Argus glances out a shattered window at the war-torn landscape. "Negative, Pegasus Two. No secure zone available. Be advised, you'll be coming in hot, but we'll ensure it's as secure as possible, over."
His eyes scan the surrounding area and settle on a building adjacent to their position. It appears structurally sound, perhaps the best option they have for a landing zone under the circumstances. "Pegasus Two, adjust your approach for a landing on the building adjacent to our position. It's the best shot we have. Have Razor Wings on standby for cover fire, over."
"Roger that, Valor Six. Adjusting approach and coordinating with the Razor Wings. Pegasus Two out."
As the teams maintain a vigilant 360-degree perimeter defense around the building, occasional enemy combatants approach their position. Each time, the Praetorians respond with swift and deadly precision. A soldier from FireFireteam Ironclad, positioned by a broken window, spots an alien soldier attempting to sneak up from the rubble. He takes careful aim and fires, the shot landing in the alien's head, dropping it instantly.
Meanwhile, Argus and Charles meet inside to quickly strategize. The sound of sporadic gunfire and the occasional alien shriek from outside punctuate their planning.
"Charles, I need you to take Ironclad up as high as possible in this building. Provide overhead cover and keep an eye out for any more incoming hostiles," Argus instructs.

Their footsteps echo in the hollow, damaged corridors, offices and restrooms. A stark reminder of the once-civilian nature of their battleground.
Outside, Fireteam Valor, led by Argus, tactically maneuvers through the debris and chaos towards the adjacent building. They move in a tight formation, covering each other's movements, their eyes constantly scanning for threats. The sounds of battle rage around them, but their focus is singular – secure that rooftop for the incoming Pegasus Transports. The fate of the civilians and the success of their mission depends on it.
As Fireteam Valor quietly and swiftly moves towards the adjacent building, the ambience is eerily calm outside. The only sounds punctuating the silence are the distant, precise shots from Ironclad's sniper, Specialist Brooks. His role is crucial, providing overwatch and taking out enemies from a distance as the rest of his team provides additional overhead security.

Argus acknowledges the intel with a curt "Copy that, Ironclad Three. Keep us covered."

The two hostiles guarding the door are taken down in a matter of seconds, their bodies hitting the floor with heavy thuds.

Argus says, his voice reflecting both concern for his family and the determination to carry out their mission.
