# Prologue: Rise of the Obsidian Empire

In the vast cosmic expanse, the Obsidian Empire had flourished for millennia, its strength and influence reaching the farthest corners of known space. Born from diverse planetary colonies, each with its unique strengths and challenges, the empire found its guiding light under the visionary leadership of Highlord Vrakar.

Vrakar, a being of unparalleled wisdom and ambition, faced immense challenges in uniting the warring factions of the Obsidian worlds. Yet, through diplomacy and might, he accomplished what many deemed impossible, weaving a tapestry of unity from the myriad of diverse threads.

At the heart of the empire's power stood the Tribunal Guard, a select group of warriors personally chosen by Highlord Vrakar. These five individuals, each hailing from different Obsidian races, were more than just guards; they were symbols of the empire's unity, each member exemplifying the pinnacle of their race's capabilities. Their very presence was a testament to the unified strength of the Obsidian Empire, and they served as both protectors and ambassadors.

However, among the ranks, there were those whose names invoked awe and terror in equal measure. The Dra'kor Vhel'Tan - the Darkness Undying. These two unmatched warriors held a reputation that eclipsed even the most elite units of the empire. Taking orders directly from Highlord Vrakar, their efficiency, ruthlessness, and honor-bound code made them legends within the Obsidian Armada. They were the embodiment of fear for both foe and ally, their missions always successful, their actions always decisive.

The Obsidian Blackguard were the empire's elite warriors, bound by an unwavering honor that dictated their every move. Just below them stood the Titan Vanguard, formidable fighters in their own right. The road to becoming a Blackguard was laden with peril - a test that few survived. Aspiring members faced a lethal selection process; to fail meant certain death. It was a testament to their indomitable spirit, a journey where only the absolute best could ascend to the ranks of the Blackguard.

As the Obsidian Empire expanded, its stories of valor, unity, and unyielding power echoed throughout the cosmos. Tales of the Tribunal Guard's might, the merciless precision of the Dra'kor Vhel'Tan, and the honor-bound ferocity of the Blackguard inspired reverence and dread. The titan Vanguard's stalwart warriors, standing at the precipice of greatness or doom, became beacons of hope for the young and ambitious, illustrating that sacrifice in the empire's name could lead to unparalleled honor.

Yet, beneath the tales of valor, a complex web of politics and power dynamics unfolded. Highlord Vrakar's vision was not just of conquest, but of a united empire that stood as a beacon of strength and collaboration. His leadership was a delicate dance of ensuring the empire's many races felt represented and valued, all while keeping the ever-present threats of rebellion and external aggression at bay.

The empire's strength was not just in its vast armadas, unparalleled warriors, or far-reaching colonies. It was in its unity. A unity forged in the fires of adversity, under the watchful gaze of Highlord Vrakar, and protected by the empire's most formidable: from the Tribunal Guard to the enigmatic Dra'kor Vhel'Tan.

And so, as stars burned bright and galaxies whirled in the endless dance of the cosmos, the Obsidian Empire emerged as a testament to ambition and unity. Its stories, both of light and shadow, became the stuff of legends. Yet, every legend carries within it the seeds of truth, and as the empire grew, so did the challenges it would face. But one truth remained unshaken: in the heart of darkness, unity was their undying light, a beacon that would either guide them to greater heights or be the very flame that consumed them.

The empire's fate rested in the hands of its leaders, its warriors, and its people. And as the tides of time flowed, the Obsidian Empire stood, a monolith against the vast backdrop of space, waiting for the next chapter in its epic saga to unfold.

Amidst the grandeur of the Obsidian Empire, its ever-watchful eyes had long been set on a burgeoning species: the humans. For eons, the Obsidians had silently observed their evolution, their technological advances, and their forays into the cosmic realm. From a distance, they studied, learned, and assessed, always vigilant, always ready.

The humans, in their boundless curiosity and ambition, ventured further into space, colonizing planets and moons without fully understanding the significance of their actions. What they saw as uninhabited, ripe for expansion, the Obsidians recognized as sacred grounds, repositories of their ancient artifacts and relics, some holding powers beyond comprehension.

Highlord Vrakar's normally stoic demeanor darkened with rage when reports came in of the humans' latest colonization efforts. These weren't just any planets – these were sanctified lands, holding relics from a time long before the humans even learned to harness fire. Every step the humans took on these worlds was a desecration, every artifact they discovered an affront to the Obsidians' revered ancestors.

Planet Trident, with its azure skies and vast oceans, was particularly significant. Deep beneath its surface lay a relic of unimaginable power, a testament to the Obsidian Empire's ancient might. Reports indicated that the humans had found it, oblivious to the immense power they had stumbled upon.

In the grand chamber of the Obsidian Citadel, Highlord Vrakar summoned High Commander Drakken Thik'Shal, the formidable leader of the Obsidian Blackguard. Their discussion was intense, the weight of decisions heavy in the air. The time for observation was over; now was the time for action.

"High Commander," Vrakar's voice echoed, deep and resonant. "The humans tread on sacred ground, ignorant of the power they wield. We cannot let our relics, our very heritage, be sullied by their touch. Planet Trident shall fall first. We reclaim what is ours."

Drakken, his form imposing even in the grandeur of the chamber, met Vrakar's gaze with an unwavering stare. "My lord," he began, choosing his words with precision, "the humans, for all their advancements, remain blind to the sanctity of what they unearth. Do we seek to educate them, or is our intention more... final?"

Vrakar's gaze intensified, a deep fire burning behind his eyes. "Educate? They've had their chance to understand, to respect. Now, they will learn of the consequences. Their ignorance will be met with our might. I shall send in the Dra'Kor Vhel'Tan to initiate first contact."

A brief silence enveloped the chamber, the gravity of the impending decision weighing on both leaders.

Drakken nodded, understanding the depth of Vrakar's resolution. "As you command, Highlord. The Obsidian Blackguard shall mobilize. We will ensure that the humans are made aware of the price for their ignorance. I'm sure the humans will know the meaning of their desecration once the Dra'Kor Vhel'Tan take hold."

Vrakar, appreciating the loyalty and commitment of his commander, inclined his head slightly. "See to it. And let the universe bear witness to the might of the Obsidians. This is not just about Planet Trident; this is about sending a message to all who dare desecrate our sacred relics."

With a final salute, Drakken exited the chamber, determination evident in every step. As the massive doors closed behind him, he could feel the weight of the task ahead. But there was also a sense of honor and exhilaration. The Blackguard were not just warriors; they were the embodiment of the Obsidian Empire's pride and strength. And now, they were to be unleashed.

Drakken wasted no time, summoning his captains and commanders. As they gathered, he could see the anticipation in their eyes, the readiness for what was to come. With a voice full of conviction, he declared, "Prepare for war. The Obsidians will make way to Trident, and we will let no human survive." The room resonated with a chorus of affirmations, the Blackguard eager to prove their valor once more.

The Obsidian Empire's throne room was a masterpiece of dark architecture, its towering spires cutting through the cosmos, a testament to its imperial might. As Highlord Vrakar's gaze lingered over the vast expanse, he was well aware of the monumental decision he was about to enforce. One final command was needed to set his plans in motion. With a discreet gesture, he summoned the embodiment of the Obsidian Empire's might.

The massive doors, carved with the stories of a thousand battles, slowly parted, revealing the formidable figures of the Dra'Kor Vhel'Tan. Every being in the empire had heard whispers of these twin brothers, their very names evoking an uneasy blend of reverence and dread. Lythar and Rythos, bound by blood and battle, are legends incarnate.

Lythar, the elder by a fleeting moment, was a vision of malevolence. His obsidian armor, was meticulously crafted to inspire fear. Crimson lines flowed like veins across its surface, pulsating in rhythm with his heartbeat. His helmet, smooth and reflective, concealed his eyes but not the intent they held. Rythos, while bearing similar intimidating armor, had nuances that set him apart: sharper edges, and a slightly darker hue that seemed to absorb the light around him. His presence was that of a storm about to break, unpredictable and devastating.

Without requiring an introduction, Vrakar addressed them, "Lythar, Rythos, a new dawn awaits the Obsidian Empire. Our conquest of Planet Trident is crucial, and its destiny intertwines with yours."

Lythar responded, his voice a deep rumble from beneath the helmet, "We stand ready, Highlord."

Vrakar continued, his tone firm, "Your mission is clear. Penetrate the defenses of Planet Trident covertly. Disarm their core defenses and eliminate any who pose a threat. Gather as much intelligence as you can on their defense space station: Liberation. Leave no witnesses. We must catch them unaware, and the advantage of surprise is ours to wield."

Rythos, with an air of barely-contained fury, inquired, "Once the Obsidian Armada emerges from the shadows of hyperspace?"

"Your presence on Trident concludes," Vrakar declared. "Return to me directly. Your task is not to engage in prolonged battles but to clear our path. You will be the rising tide that will either lead to our defeat or our victory."

The deadly twins looked at each other through their dark crimson helmets, both understood what had to be done. Both warriors ready to fight. Both warriors ready to die.
