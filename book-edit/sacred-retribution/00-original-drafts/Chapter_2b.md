# Prologue – Part 2

As High Commander Drakken's ship, Vengeance Incarnate, sliced through hyperspace, a palpable tension permeated its pristine halls. Every corner of the ship resonated with the might and majesty of the Blackguard Elite. Within its heart, High Commander Drakken moved with Larkar Zhul beside him, their forms radiant against the ship's ethereal glow.

Larkar, ever the eloquent provocateur, began, "Drakken, this venture against the humans, it's a testament to your ambition, isn't it? A fresh challenge after countless victories."

Drakken met Larkar's gaze evenly, his voice serene. "Indeed, Larkar. It is our duty as the Blackguard Elite to face and conquer new frontiers. Humanity is but the next step in our grand design."

Larkar responded gracefully, though the undertone of challenge was unmistakable, "Your vision has always been expansive, High Commander. One might wonder, however, if ambition might overshadow prudence on occasion."

Drakken's reply was smooth, betraying no hint of irritation. "Ambition and prudence are not mutually exclusive, Larkar. I've found they often walk hand in hand."

A gentle chime echoed through the ship, heralding their approach to Trident. The stage was set, and beneath the formal courtesies, the rivalry between the two Obsidians remained as fierce as ever.

Larkar's eyes gleamed with mischief, sensing an opportunity to further needle Drakken. "Ah, but High Commander, it's often said that even the grandest design can falter if its architect lacks...clarity."

Drakken stopped in his tracks, facing Larkar with an icy calm. "Larkar, my path has always been clear, and it leads our people to unparalleled greatness. If you question my leadership, invoke the Luk'Sha O'ka. Challenge me. Prove you can lead better."

The weight of the silence that followed was immense. The crew aboard the Vengeance Incarnate felt it, an electric charge hanging in the air.

For the uninitiated, the Luk'Sha O'ka was not a mere challenge—it was a sacred ritual. It was the purest form of combat in Obsidian culture, where disputes were resolved, and hierarchies established. The battle was to the death, and only one would emerge victorious, taking up the mantle of leadership if they could best the current leader. It was the most honorable way to determine if a leader still had the right to lead, or if their time had come to an end.

Larkar's eyes remained locked onto Drakken's, searching for a sign of weakness. Finally, he replied, voice dripping with insincere sweetness, "All in due time, High Commander. We have more pressing matters to attend to, such as the human stronghold."

The hint of a smirk tugged at Drakken's lips. "Indeed. Focus on the task at hand, Larkar. But remember, my offer stands."

With that, the two continued forward, the galaxy unaware of the tempest brewing between these titans of the Obsidian Blackguard Elite.

Drakken and Larkar peeled off. Larkar to the Vor'Ruk Naleth and Drakken to the bridge of the Vengeance Incarnate. High Commander Drakken's voice, both calm and commanding, echoed through the bridge of the Vengeance Incarnate. "Pause the AetherVail tether, let us linger unseen within its cascading veils." A strange, shivering pause enveloped the ship, suspending it in a quiet space between the chaotic currents of hyperspace and the tangible reality beyond.

The vessel, along with twelve others, each veiled in advanced stealth technologies, hovered in this interstitial space, nearly invisible and indistinct. Ahead, the faint outline of Station Liberation lingered like a distant star, unaware of the impending threat concealed in the cosmic in-between.

Behind them, a multitude of Obsidian vessels waited, cloaked in the undulating colors of the AetherVail, prepared to surge forward at Drakken's command. The initial task was clear: dismantle the guardianship of Station Liberation to lay bare the world of Trident to their ensuing onslaught.

Below, in the "Vor'Ruk Naleth" – Warbreath Chamber – Drakken's Blackguard prepared, encased in their uniquely embellished armors, each piece telling a silent tale of battles fought and won. This space, meant for introspection before inevitable conflict, murmured with the subtle shift and creak of armor, the calm breaths of warriors readying their minds for what lay ahead.

Larkar, a seasoned warrior with eyes reflecting deep pools of cosmic violet, addressed his companions, not as subordinates, but as equals in battle. "Comrades, we stand on the brink of a new confrontation. How do you suppose the humans will react to our sudden shadow?"

Vaera Kint, armor flowing like a dark, silent river around her, smirked, "They will tremble, Larkar, their resolve dissolving like dust to the void. We will be their unspoken fear, their silent demise."

A tower of strength, Gravus Lo'Nok, nodded thoughtfully. "We are the dark that consumes light, unyielding as the void. But we must remember: even the weakest being will fight fiercely when backed into a corner."

The agile Serkis Jin'thal added, "Rightly spoken, Gravus. Empires rise and fall, but it is often the forgotten lessons of history that prove most lethal. We will wield our strength with somber responsibility."

The final voice, Ta'lia Nok'shal, as youthful as she was wise, gently spoke, "We shall be their silent undoing, our unity their unbreakable chain. In victory, we honor those who have walked this path before us. Vor'kara, my kin."

She laid a hand over her heart, a symbolic gesture, signaling respect and unity among them. Each warrior followed, their voices uniting in a whispered chorus, "Vor'kara."

Vor'kara, a term sacred to the Obsidian people, translated to 'Sacred Bonds' in human tongue. Yet its meaning ran deeper in the native dialect of the Obsidian, signifying a profound link, an unspoken understanding and mutual respect between warriors bound by common purpose and shared history.

High above in the quasi-space where the Vengeance Incarnate lingered, Drakken, his gaze fixed on the distant, fragile glow of the station, silently spoke ancient words reserved for moments that tread upon the precipice of destiny, "In the veiled shadow, dawn finds its breath." His claw-like fingers tightened, ready to unleash a battle that would carve its scars upon the canvas of history.

With a deep, resonating inhalation, Drakken seized the fragile moment, eyes ablaze with a fire kindled by eons of ambition and conflict. "Into the maelstrom, we reveal ourselves!" He declared, arm extending forward as the veil of hyperspace dissipated, revealing the Vengeance Incarnate and its accompanying warships, stark and ominous against the expanse of star-speckled darkness.

The abrupt materialization of the Obsidian fleet, a looming, ethereal spectacle, paralyzed the crew aboard the Liberation's bridge with dread. Eyes widened, voices hitched in throats, as they beheld the dark titans of war materializing before them. In the shadow of the impending apocalypse, the entirety of the Liberation could only tremble, their sentinel defenses glaringly futile against the overwhelming darkness.

Drakken, expression unmoved, issued the next command with an implacable resolve. "Neutralize their defenses, let them feel the weight of our arrival." Simultaneously, the warships unleashed a cataclysmic salvo, advanced projectiles and arcs of vivid, searing energy beams cascading through the void. The Liberation's automated defenses were eradicated, reduced to mere stardust within mere seconds, their once formidable power extinguished beneath the Obsidian's onslaught.

In the next heartbeat, boarding ships propelled through the chaos, laden with the legions of Obsidian warriors, those whose fate was to first meet the defenders of the Liberation. Among them, the Titan Vanguard, behemoths in battle, stoic and relentless, forged their path through the bleak expanse towards the human bastion. Within their vessels, a hallowed, unwavering chant began to swell, each syllable a testament to their boundless determination.

"Tor'Vesh, Kara'Dun sarith; Vaerie Luth'ara, Kara'Nok exta," they intoned, voices interweaving into a rich tapestry of devotion and impending doom. "Through shadow, we claim light; in oblivion, we carve existence."

Their creed, a haunting melody amidst the chaos, underscored the approaching clash, a symphony heralding both ruin and creation, resonating through the cold abyss between the two warring entities.

And then, a sound - metallic, final, reverberated through the comms, a collision of worlds as the Obsidian boarding ships latched onto the Liberation, forging the first tangible link between the invaders and the beleaguered defenders.

Drakken, his demeanor as serene and inexorable as the void itself, inclined his head slightly, an acknowledgment of the contact and a silent tribute to those who now embarked upon the dance of war. And thus, in the boundless theatre of the cosmos, another tragic yet epic tale of conflict began to unfurl. The pulsating energy of impending battle flickered across High Commander Drakken's intense, otherworldly eyes, reaching into the core of his martial spirit, stirring ancient and potent desires born from epochs of relentless conflict. With a subtle, yet immeasurably potent gesture, he turned to his 1st Helm, Zorath Ke'Ulan, who stood steadfast, a vigilant sentinel amidst the celestial chaos unfurling beyond the ship's colossal viewports.

"Vor'kara," Drakken whispered, the words a sacred pact, an unspoken promise woven through ages of blood and shadow, his gaze locked with Zorath's, acknowledging the depth of responsibility now bestowed upon him.

With solemn, undeterred resolve, Zorath responded, his voice a tranquil echo amidst the storm, "Vor'kara En'thar," paying homage not only to his superior but to the hallowed path they now tread together, beneath the watchful eyes of the cosmos.

With swift, purposeful strides, Drakken transitioned towards the Vor'Ruk Naleth, his warrior's sanctum, where his elite, the Blackguard, awaited, each soul a tempest of battle-hardened resolve and unyielding commitment. Upon his arrival, the air grew taut, charged with an electric, anticipatory energy, the essence of legacies forged and yet to be wrought beneath the indifferent celestial canvas.

"Vor'kara, my Highborn," he announced, each word a testament to their shared purpose, their unified devotion beneath the endless cosmic sprawl. As his eyes navigated through each warrior, acknowledging the singularity and depth of their presence, he intoned their names, a roll call of warriors destined to carve their path through the annals of time and space: "Larkar Zhul, Vaera Kint, Gravus Lo'Nok, Serkis Jin'Thal, Ta'lia Nok'Shal."

A palpable silence lingered momentarily, before he continued, his voice resonating with a primordial, indomitable force, "We are ready for battle. First contact has been made by the Titan Vanguard. 1st Helm has the ship, and now, we shall showcase to our Obsidian brethren aboard the Liberation the essence of those truly born for battle."

He paused, his eyes surveying the warriors before him, each entity an embodiment of countless, cataclysmic conflicts, myriad destinies entwined through epochs of chaos and conquest.

"Today," Drakken concluded, "we etch our legacy amidst the stars, an indomitable torrent amidst the cosmic order. In our wake, we shall leave both devastation and reverence, for in our path, we forge both destiny and doom."

With this, he gestured forward, the gateway before them irising open, revealing the looming, ethereal abyss beyond, their pathway towards destiny, towards the cataclysmic confluence of aspirations and obliteration, where their Vor'Kara, their sacred bonds, would be both tested and exalted amongst the coming battle.

The Blackguard, aboard their sleek and formidable boarding craft, carved through the celestial void, bound for the embattled Liberation. Upon their approach, through the advanced displays of their ship, a chaotic dance of death and defiance unraveled before them. Through the transparisteel windows of the Liberation, the unforgiving clash between Obsidian cannon fodder, the formidable Titan Vanguard, and desperate human defenders painted a tumultuous tableau against the backdrop of the cosmos.

Larkar Zhul, his eyes unyielding, beheld the frenzied confrontation with discerning severity. "Even for the Titan Vanguard, this chaos... It is a despicable display of battle prowess," he asserted, a palpable disdain etching through his words.

Drakken's response emanated, a hissing reprimand veiled within a whisper, yet teeming with an unspoken ferocity, "This is why we, the Blackguard, enter the fray, Larkar. To rectify the failings of others, to demonstrate the true art of combat. Each Obsidian must execute their duty with precision; where they falter, we shall prevail. Our path now leads into the heart of this 'Liberation', into the maelstrom to demonstrate the potency of true warriors."

The boarding craft collided with the Liberation, a melding of technologies amidst the chaotic symphony of battle. A moment, stretched seemingly into eternity, encapsulated their transition into the fray, as they descended upon the turmoil below, their movements a meticulous and ethereal dance amidst the chaos.

Their ingress into the vessel seemed an ethereal passage through time, their forms weaving and gliding through incoming fire, deflecting torrents of desperate resistance with their crimson blades, which sang a mournful dirge amidst the disarray. A symphony of methodical, calculated destruction ensued as, one by one, they dismantled the human resistances with an unsettling, yet awe-inducing, grace.

Upon encountering a fortified barricade, steadfastly guarded by beleaguered human defenders, Gravus Lo'Nok, his form a silhouette of foreboding dominance against the flickering battle-lit corridor, approached. With an effortless, yet immensely potent sweep of his sword, the barricade was sundered, cascading into a disarray of shrapnel and despair, its defenders silenced amidst the unrelenting, inexorable advance of the Blackguard.

The Obsidian warriors pressed onward, an unstoppable tide of vengeance and purpose, each step a proclamation, an unwavering declaration amidst the boundless, indifferent void, "We are the Blackguard, the harbinger of destinies, forger of the vor'kara."

And so, through shadow and ruin, they forged onward, beneath the watchful eyes of eternity.

In the nerve center of the Vengeance Incarnate, a complex web of intricate technology and dim, ambient lighting, 1st Helm attentively monitored the "hyper-cipher" radio channel, intercepting an urgent, desperate signal from the Liberation attempting to breach through to Trident. With a cunning glance towards the communications officer, 1st Helm gestured to let the message flow just a little longer, allowing a semblance of hope to linger in the human commander's voice. "This is Commander Drexel calling from Liberation. We are under attack! We will not survive up here long..." came the strained communication. A sly, mischievous laugh slipped from 1st Helm as he then coolly ordered, "Cut them off." The transmission choked into silence mid-sentence, "Get Trident's Defenses..." and then, nothing.

Transitioning back to the chaotic warfare unfolding within the confines of Liberation, the battle raged on, a tumultuous tempest of violence and strategy. The Blackguard, formidable and stoic, allowed the Titan Vanguard to spearhead the push towards the pivotal central server room. The sight of the revered Blackguard filled the Titan Vanguard with a feral, zealous wrath; they plummeted into the fray; each Obsidian casualty countered with a haunting, taunting laugh intended to crumble the human resolve. But the Blackguard maintained a calculated restraint, their true combat prowess masked in strategic patience. "I sense your unyielding itch for battle, my Highborn," Drakken confided, his voice a steady undercurrent amidst the chaos, "But let the Titan Vanguard press on. Our true might will reveal itself in due time. Let us not hoard all the enjoyment." Nods of accord spread among the elite warriors.

As the Titan Vanguard and the accompanying forces forged onward, they were met with staunch resistance; a choke point, meticulously defended by humans, seemingly ready to make their last stand before the central server room, stood before them. Their faces, strained yet resolute, told stories of defiance and an unwavering will to protect their last bastion of hope.

The cacophony of battle engulfed the Liberation, a space station now synonymous with a desperate struggle for survival. The Titan Vanguard, wielding their pulsating purple energy swords, demonstrated a venerable, savage elegance on the battlefield, weaving a deadly dance that was both awe-inspiring and terrifying. Their prowess was acknowledged even by the stern-eyed Blackguard, who watched as the Vanguard clashed against a resilient, unyielding human defense.

Yet, despite their combat mastery, the Vanguard found themselves hindered, unable to pierce through the heavily fortified human barricade. Bullets, explosives, and tumultuous waves of aggressive counterattacks from the humans painted the metallic halls with chaos, flickering lights casting eerie shadows over the fallen of both sides.

Drakken, his dark eyes ablaze with impatience and an unstated urgency, spoke, his voice a melding of calm and commanding resolve, "Blackguard, my Highborn. Now is the time. Show the Vanguard, and our enemies, the apex of our might. SEIZE WHAT IS OURS!"

Responding, the Blackguard, with unified, fluid motion, unsheathed their red energy swords. The weapons bathed them in a sinister, blood-like glow, an unspoken harbinger of doom for those who stood against them. They advanced, a wall of deadly certainty, blades masterfully parrying a barrage of bullets.

Within this chaos, a human soldier, his eyes reflecting a desperate courage, attempted to halt Larkar's advance. His weapon, emptying rapidly in a frenzied burst, failed to find its target as each projectile was effortlessly deflected by Larkar's blade. In an answer to this failed assassination, Larkar, with an unsettling calm and precise lethality, launched his sword through the air. The crimson blade sailed, unwavering, embedding itself into the soldier's skull.

Approaching the now-lifeless human, Larkar exuded a sinister grace, his laughter echoing darkly amidst the chaos, a mockery of the futile human resistance. Extracting his sword from the remains of his foe, he lifted his gaze to witness the human forces retreating, barricading themselves within the central server room. The subsequent, resounding boom of the sealed door punctuated the corridor, a temporary pause in the desperate symphony of battle that ensued.
