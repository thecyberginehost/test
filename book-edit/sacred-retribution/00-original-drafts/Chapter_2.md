# Prologue: On the Brink of Dominion

The Obsidian homeworld, Karthak Prime, was a realm of darkness and grandeur, its skies perpetually painted with hues of deep purples and swirling blues. On this planet, ancient rites had shaped the destinies of countless generations. And atop a towering spire of obsidian, Highlord Vrakar stood, surveying the expanses of his dominion.

Beneath him, a sea of black-armored warriors—the Obsidian Blackguard—marched in formation, their red energy swords crackling, humming a deadly symphony that resonated with their unity and discipline. Vrakar's sharp, angular face was a study of determination. The conquests ahead were not just for resources or dominance, they were a sacred undertaking.

Beside him, High Commander Drakken Thik'shal, leader of the Blackguard Elite, looked on with an equal measure of pride and anticipation. "The humans," Drakken murmured, his voice a gravelly whisper, "do they know what awaits them?"

Vrakar's cold eyes met Drakken's. "They occupy lands and realms that hold the echoes of the Ancestors. They desecrate these places with their very presence. The Scouring Rite begins with them."

Drakken nodded slowly, understanding the gravity of their holy mission. But even as Vrakar spoke of sacredness and divine purpose, Drakken's thoughts were also on the thrill of the battle, the challenge the humans might present, and the glory that awaited the Obsidian Blackguard.

The winds of Karthak Prime howled, carrying whispers of wars to come, as two leaders, bound by purpose and ambition, set their sights on a galaxy unprepared for the storm about to be unleashed.

The sprawling launch grounds of Karthak Prime resonated with the determined march of thousands. As the Obsidian Blackguard stood at attention before the colossal flagship, "Ebon Titan," the air was thick with tension and anticipation.

From their elevated vantage point, the Elite watched the rest of the armada prepare. The Titan Vanguard soldiers made their way past, their silver armor glinting under Karthak's muted light, each piece inscribed with deep crimson runes. They carried their purple energy swords with a sense of pride and purpose. Though not as menacing as the Blackguard, their reputation in battle was well-respected.

As the Vanguard passed the Elite, silent exchanges took place. Nods of respect and acknowledgments of shared purpose were exchanged, a bond of warriors about to embark on a journey of conquest and purpose. Though the two groups were distinct, in this moment, the line that separated them blurred, united by a common objective.

Then came the troops often referred to by the Blackguard as the "cannon fodder." Their mismatched armors lacked the refinement and elegance of the Elite and the Vanguard. They were a sea of eager, yet inexperienced warriors, drawn from the far corners of Karthak Prime. In one-on-one combat, they might not stand a chance against a formidable opponent, but in large groups, their sheer numbers could overwhelm.

High Commander Drakken Thik'shal watched them all with a practiced eye. To him, each was a piece on a chessboard, every soldier with a role to play. "It is not just about strength," he whispered to himself, thinking of the impending battles. "But strategy, positioning, and the will to see our mission to the end."

He turned to his Blackguard, his most trusted. "Prepare to board the Ebon Titan. We have been summoned by Highlord Vrakar. The sacred conquest awaits."

With disciplined synchrony, the Blackguard moved, the weight of their purpose echoing in every step. The vast armada of Karthak Prime was on the move, and the galaxy would soon feel its might.

Inside the Ebon Titan, a vast chamber lit by an eerie, glowing ambiance awaited the Blackguard Elite. The room, known as the Conclave Hall, was filled with an air of expectation. Each Obsidian warrior stood at attention, their sleek armor reflecting the dim, pulsating light.

At the chamber's epicenter stood Highlord Vrakar, an imposing figure even amongst the towering Blackguard. His eyes, burning with an intensity matched only by his ambition, surveyed the room, taking in every warrior, every piece of the machinery that surrounded them.

"Warriors of the Obsidian Blackguard, Titan Vanguard, and soldiers of the armada," he began, his voice echoing throughout the chamber, "we are on a mission of reclamation and purification. The human-infested world of Trident has for too long been a blemish in our domain. Its very existence is a sacrilege to what it once stood for. We are here to correct that."

A holographic display of the human space station "Liberation" materialized in front of Vrakar. "This will be our first point of contact," he continued. "The Blackguard Elites, under the command of High Commander Drakken Thik'shal, will spearhead the assault."

The display zoomed in on the intricate details of the station's defenses. "Your task is to cripple their defenses and create an opening for our boarding parties. We will send in our overwhelming ground forces, supported by the Titan Vanguard, to test their defenses. This will give us a clear picture of what we are up against."

High Commander Drakken stepped forward, acknowledging Vrakar with a nod. "Once the initial defenses have been breached," Vrakar continued, "Drakken and a select group of the Blackguard will infiltrate the inner chambers of the space station. Their mission is clear: find the central server system and disable all planetary defenses."

Vrakar's gaze became even more intense, his voice lowering to a deadly whisper, "And leave no human alive. The Liberation station must become a silent tomb, a message to the universe of the price of defiance."

A murmur of assent spread through the room. Every warrior was ready, eager to fulfill their duty. They had trained for moments like these, and they would not fail.

"As we embark on this quest," Vrakar concluded, "remember our cause. We are not just reclaiming a planet. We are reclaiming our destiny."
