## Planned canon beats (Obsidian saga)

### "The family's last stand" — Drakken's discovery (mirror of human Ch11 "Tragedy")
- **Off-page deaths, discovered — never shown.** The killings of Argus's wife & daughter are NOT depicted directly on either side. Human side: **Argus** finds the aftermath (+ his critically injured son). Obsidian side: **Drakken** discovers the same aftermath through enemy eyes.
- **Never named as Argus's family — but the reader realizes it.** The text never states whose home it is. Instead, **descriptive callbacks** (the home as established in **Ch1**, specific objects, the wife & daughter, the boy's signs) make the *reader* think *"oh shit — this is Argus's family,"* while **Drakken never knows** (✅ locked: tragic irony). Works on two levels: standalone = "a brave human family"; cross-saga = the gut-punch.
- **The killer = a DISGRACED Titan Vanguard** (✅ author ruling — NOT the Dra'Kor Vhel'Tan). One of an *honorable* unit who fell to butchering civilians — to Drakken, *worse* than a known brute: a betrayal of the honor the Vanguard embodies.
- **Why he's "barely alive": the family did it to him.** Unarmed civilians — a *soldier's* family (itself a recognition clue) — fought an elite warrior so fiercely they mortally wounded him before falling. That ferocity is what Drakken praises.
- **Drakken's reaction:** honors the dead humans (warrior's respect for how hard they fought); **denies the dying Vanguard any honor** — a scowl, no Vor'kara, left to bleed out in contempt. ⭐ A root of his "newfound respect for humans" → the **Lok'Sha O'Ka with Argus** → the **alliance pull** (the very thing the Void works to prevent).
- **Junior survives (canon — he's the Harbinger / Lyrana's peace partner).** Drakken finds **no third body**, only signs a boy fought and is gone, and assumes he crawled off to die — **he's simply wrong.** Junior is gravely wounded and out of sight; **Argus finds him alive afterward** (human Ch11). Working sequence: Drakken sweeps through first → Argus arrives after.
- ⚠️ **CRAFT DEPENDENCY (human side):** for the reader-recognition to fire, human **Ch1 (the family home) and Ch11 (Tragedy)** must plant **concrete, memorable details** (the house, a specific object, the daughter's something, the wife, the boy's room) that this Obsidian scene then echoes **without naming.** Lock those matched details during the human voice pass.
