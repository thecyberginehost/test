# Sacred Retribution — the Obsidian-POV Saga

This is the **Obsidian-perspective saga** (mirror of the human **Project ForgePulse**). Per canon structure, it tells the **same war from the Obsidian side**, and runs the inverse bookend format: *alien prologue → alien chapters → human epilogue.*

## `00-original-drafts/`
The author's **earliest raw drafts (~2023), Obsidian POV** — the only Obsidian-side material that exists. They cover the **opening of the war: the assault on Station Liberation**, which is the direct mirror of the human side's **Ch8 "As Liberation Falls."** These are **unedited and pre-evolution.** They are **NOT canon where they conflict with the story bible** — the bible (`../01-story-bible/`) remains the single source of truth. When we do the voice pass, we normalize against the bible.
