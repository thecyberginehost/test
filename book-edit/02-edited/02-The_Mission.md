Chapter 2: The Mission
Date: December 15, 2888
Time: 0845 Galactic Standard Time (GTC)
Location: GFC Base: Gladius – War Room
The atmosphere in the war room was tense, charged with anticipation as Fireteam Valor settled into their seats. The air thickened with the arrival of Lieutenant Lysander, leader of Fireteam Honor, the only other STRIKE team stationed on Trident. Mark Argus, seeing his old bunkmate from the GFC's Officer Candidate School, felt a mix of camaraderie and seriousness. The rest of Fireteam Honor followed, exchanging brief, respectful greetings with their counterparts. Despite the shared history and camaraderie, there was an unspoken understanding of the gravity of the situation that had brought them together.
The room's dynamic shifted as Captain Orion entered, his presence commanding immediate attention. "Officer on Deck!" he bellowed, and every member in the room snapped to attention, a unified display of discipline and respect.
What happened next was unexpected. Commander Drexel, the officer commanding Trident’s Orbital Defenses, emerged from behind the podium. His presence signified the critical nature of their mission. The Commander, usually stationed on the Liberation, Trident’s central planetary station, seldom made personal appearances for briefings.
As he stood before them, Argus and Lysander exchanged a glance. Both were acutely aware of the vulnerabilities of Trident's defense system. The Liberation was the linchpin, controlling most of the planet’s automated orbital defenses and communications. In their strategic minds, it was a rudimentary setup, dangerously close to putting all their eggs in one basket. If Liberation fell, Trident would be left almost defenseless, save for the manual and automated weapons systems scattered across the planet.
The room fell silent, awaiting the Commander's briefing. The air was heavy with the weight of unasked questions and unspoken concerns. What threat loomed over Trident? Why the sudden recall? The answers, Mark knew, would shape the future of Trident and its inhabitants. As Drexel cleared his throat to begin, every Praetorian in the room braced themselves for what was to come.
Commander Drexel, standing solemnly at the podium in his GFC Battle Dress uniform, exuded a sense of urgency. The Praetorians, seated attentively, could sense the gravity of the situation from his attire alone. The Battle Dress uniform was reserved for serious fleet operations, and its presence was a clear indicator of the severity of their current predicament.
After a contemplative draw from his cigar, Drexel began the briefing. "We're facing a series of anomalies across Trident," he said, his voice steady but tinged with concern. "Communications failures, telecom disruptions, and our planetary defenses are progressively going offline. Orbital imagery of the stations is either blurred or distorted in some way. We can’t see a thing."
He then activated the holographic display, showing a map of Trident dotted with red markers indicating the compromised sites. "We've deployed multiple platoons of the Orbital Ground Defense Force to investigate these outages. The OGDF, as you know, is our primary orbit-to-ground force for rapid recon and reaction assignments."
The room was silent, every member acutely aware of the seriousness of sending in the OGDF — they were the first line of response to any ground-based threats or anomalies. They were not just any infantry unit; they were Special Forces.
“However,” Commander Drexel stood at the front of the room, his expression grim, "We've lost contact with all deployed OGDF teams," he announced. "Only one transmission was received — a disrupted message from Lieutenant Graham, the platoon leader of Echo Platoon that was deployed to the Huckleberry Automated Defense Platform."
The room fell silent as Drexel played the recording. Static filled the air, punctuated by the sounds of gunfire and screams.
"This is Echo six! We’re under attack! I don’t recognize them... they’re not Human! They’re cutting us down,” He paused as an explosion rung out, a faint shriek could be heard in the background, “ Echo seven is down! Gunner, watch your six! You’ve got one right beh…" Lieutenant Graham's voice was filled with panic and fear. The transmission ended abruptly after the sound of another explosion filled the air, leaving a chilling silence.
Lieutenant Argus exchanged a grave look with Lieutenant Lysander. The desperation in the message was palpable — a stark departure from the usual disciplined communications.
Commander Drexel let the message sink in before continuing. "Fireteam Valor, your mission is to secure the Huckleberry Automated Defense Platform and gather intelligence on this threat. Fireteam Honor will ensure the security of the Liberation. All but one of our OGDF platoons were dropped to the communications and telecom sites."
The gravity of their task was clear. An unknown enemy, system failures, and now a whole unit out of contact. Argus felt a familiar tension, the weight of responsibility pressing down on him.
"Lieutenants, assemble your teams and prepare for deployment. We’re counting on you," Drexel concluded.
Before he could leave, the Commander added, "This operation is under the direct oversight of GRID." He gestured to a figure standing to the side.
The figure stepped forward and removed his helmet, revealing a firm, determined expression. His gaze was precise, unwavering. It was Wraith, a GRID operative.
Captain Orion stepped forward, addressing the room. "This is Wraith. He's been assigned by GRID to assist us. He was once a Praetorian, served with me back when I was a Lieutenant. He's one of us." Orion's voice carried a hint of camaraderie, an unspoken trust between old comrades.
"I hope you still remember how to drop, Wraith," he added with a slight smile.
Wraith's response was calm yet carried an edge of confidence. "It's like riding a bike, Captain. You never really forget."
Wraith stood confidently before both Fireteam Valor and Fireteam Honor, his demeanor professional yet open. "If anyone has questions, I'll answer to the best of my ability, within the bounds of what you have the clearance to know."
Lieutenant Argus, eyeing Wraith with a mix of curiosity and caution, spoke up. "Are you a Mindreaver?"
"All GRID Operatives are Mindreavers," Wraith replied succinctly.
Argus nodded, a hint of respect in his tone. "That will be a huge asset. Another Mindreaver on the team..." He glanced towards Sergeant Rourke.
Rourke, overhearing the conversation, chimed in with a wry smile. "Probably not as fortified as our GRID counterpart here, but good enough for GFC work!"
While the others continued their discussion, Lieutenants Argus and Lysander stepped aside for a brief sidebar.
"I don't trust GRID as far as I can throw them," Argus murmured, his voice low.
Lysander nodded in agreement. "But if Orion is vouching for this one, I won't question it. He doesn't give his word lightly."
Argus glanced back at Wraith. "Let's hope this decision doesn't backfire on us. You remember what those bastards did when we were fighting on Planet Crete, don’t you?"
“I’m trying to forget it,” Lysander said, his face turning grim.
Returning to the group, Argus's expression was neutral, but his mind was active with thoughts. 'A GRID operative and a Mindreaver... This mission just got a lot more interesting,' he thought.
Captain Orion, sensing the underlying tension, spoke up. "Teams, I know bringing in GRID might not sit well with everyone. But Wraith is here for a reason. We need every advantage we can get. Trust isn't given lightly in our line of work, but Wraith has earned it."
The room's atmosphere, charged with a mix of skepticism and anticipation, reflected the complexity of the mission ahead. With the addition of Wraith, the teams were entering uncharted territory, but they were ready. They were Praetorians, after all, trained to face the unknown head-on, with resilience and adaptability as their guiding principles.
As Wraith stood before the teams, outlining the nuances of the mission, a quiet conversation began among the members of Fireteam Valor.
Specialist Royce, the team's rookie, leaned towards Sergeant Kaine. "What the hell is a Mindreaver?" he whispered.
The question, louder than intended, caught the ears of nearby members. Staff Sergeant Rourke, chuckled from his position. "Royce, you trying out for the choir or what? Pretty sure the Liberation can hear you from here."
Specialist Royce rolled his eyes, responding with a hint of sarcasm, "Very funny."
At the podium, Captain Orion overheard the exchange. A slight smirk crossed his face as he remarked, "Specialist Royce, glad to see you're embracing the subtleties of covert ops.”
Lieutenant Argus, hearing the banter, turned sharply. "Rourke, Royce, that's enough," he said authoritatively. The room fell silent, all eyes turning back to the front.
"Specialist Royce, during a brief, shut your hole," Argus said sternly.
Royce met his lieutenant's gaze, a flash of apprehension crossing his face before regaining his composure, “Yes, sir.”
Argus, noticing the quick recovery, nodded subtly. The rookie was green, but he wasn’t showing fear — a promising sign.
As the briefing continued, each member of Fireteam Valor, from the experienced Rourke and Kaine to the new recruit Royce, understood the gravity of their mission. Wraith's presence as a Mindreaver and GRID operative added a layer of complexity, and potential, to their operation.
As the meeting adjourned, Argus felt a surge of resolve. This was more than a mission; it was a fight for survival. The unknown enemy had brought the battle to their doorstep, and he was ready to face it head-on.
Commander Drexel's final words resonated with them as they left the room. "Drones dispatched to the affected locations have only returned static. You're going in blind, and intel will have to be gathered on the fly."
Argus exchanged a look with Lysander. They both understood the implications — this mission was unlike any other they had faced. The uncertainty of what lay ahead was daunting, but they were Praetorians, trained for the impossible.
"And one more thing, Praetorians," Captain Orion added firmly. "Whatever the hell is out there... kill them all. We’ll sort out the details later. Our priority is to prevent this threat from reaching our cities. We can't have another incident, like what happened on Crete."
Crete. The memory sent a shiver down Argus's spine. They had learned hard lessons there — lessons that would not be forgotten. Underestimating the enemy was not an option.
As Captain Orion concluded his directive, he added with urgency, “Hurry up. Deployment in thirty minutes.” The room, already tense, buzzed with a new intensity as the Praetorians processed the tight timeline.
Now, as the Praetorians filed out of the briefing room, they did so with a heightened sense of urgency. The task ahead was formidable, but they were the Galactic Forge Coalition's best, trained to adapt and overcome even the most challenging circumstances. Each STRIKE Team filed into a different arms room.
Lieutenant Argus, as he prepared for the mission, couldn't shake off the uneasy feeling the situation on Trident evoked. It was reminiscent of Crete, yet there was something different, something more sinister at play this time. The disjointed transmission from Echo 6 echoed in his mind, reminding him of the unknown dangers that awaited them.
Amid their hurried preparations, Specialist Tessa Byrne sought to lighten the mood with a bit of humor. "Thirty minutes to gear up? Guess I'll have time to have a cup of coffee," she quipped, a wry smile on her face.
Lieutenant Argus, always appreciative of his team's efforts to ease the tension, responded with a brief, amused grin. "Just make sure it’s a French Press, Tessa. We'll need the coffee made right, this time."
Staff Sergeant Rourke added his dry humor to the mix, “Make sure if you have Vega make the coffee, leave her the directions with pictures, you know she can’t read.”
Corporal Elara Vega, the fireteam’s medic, shot back playfully, “Keep it up, Rourke, and I’ll make your next physical assessment hell. Remember who’s grading the STRIKE teams this year, me!”
The team's camaraderie was a small yet crucial comfort as they reached the armor station. The mechanical bots worked efficiently, fitting each member with the advanced Praetorian armor. The dark hue and the distinctive STRIKE Team emblem on the right breastplate marked them as the elite of the elite, a fact that was not lost on any of them.
The STRIKE Team emblem, engraved on their advanced armor, featured a sleek, angular shield overlaid with two crossed swords, symbolizing defense and combat readiness, and was encircled by a ring of micro-circuitry patterns, representing their technological prowess and unity. The emblem's dark tones with metallic accents complemented the armor's color, distinguishing the STRIKE Team as an elite unit within the Praetorian ranks.
Specialist Milo Harlan, the team's heavy weapons expert, selected his preferred armament, the formidable SHR-3663R Portable Chain Gun, affectionately known as 'The Shredder'. Its reputation for precision and power was well-earned, especially in the hands of a skilled Praetorian.
Tessa, the team's sniper, meticulously prepared her gear, her focus unwavering. “Whatever’s waiting for us out there, it won’t stand a chance,” she said confidently, checking the calibration on her sniper rifle.
As they continued their preparations, the team members ensured that every piece of equipment was battle ready. The light-hearted banter amidst the serious atmosphere served as a reminder of their shared experiences and unspoken bond.
Lieutenant Argus, observing the unfamiliar weapons Wraith carried, couldn’t resist showing his curiosity. Wraith, ever perceptive, noticed his gaze.
"These weapons are of my own design," Wraith began, a trace of pride in his tone. "GRID grants me access to advanced technology and arms, within a reasonable budget, of course."
Argus raised an eyebrow, his interest piqued. "A sword and a pistol? That's an unconventional combo for a former Praetorian."
Off to the side, Specialist Royce, the rookie on the team, was discreetly eavesdropping. Despite the typical Praetorian skepticism towards GRID, he couldn’t hide his fascination.
Wraith, aware of Royce's attention, spoke louder for the benefit of the young Specialist. "The sword here, my Reactor Blade, is something special. Forged on Veridian Prime at GRID HQ, it combines Trident's indestructible metal with an alloy from a classified asteroid. And, infused with the latest GFC Warship reactor engine energy, it has this distinct glow."
He then displayed his pistol, "Sting," with a sense of ownership. "Sting here shoots smart explosive rounds that detonate on impact, but only when it’s safe – all thanks to synchronization with my enhanced Neural Interface, another perk from GRID. It decides whether or not to detonate based on the target's nature, using a smart AI system embedded into all GRID Operatives armor, a light version of TitanAI.”
Royce, now openly admiring the weapons, was visibly impressed. Wraith's gear was a reminder of GRID's advanced technological edge. “What’s TitanAI? I’ve never heard of that,” he asked.
“Well, seeing as you have the clearance to public knowledge,” he sarcastically said, “TitanAI is an AI integrated vulnerability management and enemy targeting database that has the capability to scan anything in my sector of fire, determine whether or not there is a threat or security vulnerability, and instantly communicate this information with my Neural Interface. That determines whether or not Sting’s rounds will explode on impact,” he paused as he took a sip from his armor’s water system, “TitanAI is my favorite piece of software created by my favorite GFC and GRID contractor. Can you guess who that is?”
Rourke, listening in decides to answer, “Cyber AI Engine. Everyone knows that, except for the rookie, apparently.”
Argus nodded, acknowledging the strategic advantage of such unique armaments. "Well, if GRID's tech can give us an upper hand, I'm all for it."
Wraith, noticing the team's interest in his armor, began to elaborate. "This isn't my design, but I made sure GRID got me something that wasn’t off the shelf." He tapped on the sleek surface of his suit. "It's as unique as the weapons I carry."
"The armor is crafted from the same material as my Reactor Blade, including its power source," he continued. "It enhances my reaction time by five times the normal rate. Plus, it’s equipped with rear-boot thrusters. They can propel me forward by 50 meters in an instant, hitting speeds up to eighty kilometers per hour, though it's just for a short burst, then it slows to fifty kilometers per hour, or until I stop. It's designed for rapid engagement and retreat, perfect for what we might be in for on the field."
Wraith paused, a hint of excitement in his voice. "And it hasn't seen combat yet, so you'll witness its debut performance."
Specialist Royce, always curious and slightly out of turn, couldn’t help but comment. "Looks like it can’t take a hit, it’s so thin."
Without a word, Wraith turned his pistol, Sting, on himself. He deactivated the detonation function just in case, and pulled the trigger. The round hit the armor but left not a single scratch. He turned back to the team with a sarcastic grin. "As resilient as it is sleek, Specialist."
Lieutenant Argus, watching the demonstration, was visibly impressed. His own battle armor would have likely been damaged, if not penetrated, by such a close-range shot. "That’s some impressive tech, Wraith."
The room filled with a new sense of respect and awe for the GRID operative's gear. Wraith's presence and his state-of-the-art equipment added an unexpected but welcome edge to the team's capabilities. As they finalized their preparations, each member of Fireteam Valor felt a renewed confidence, knowing they had an asset like Wraith on their side for the challenges ahead.
The team proceeded with their preparations; each member focused on their gear but acutely aware of the new dynamics Wraith brought to their mission. His presence, coupled with his cutting-edge armaments, added a layer of intrigue and potential to their already formidable capabilities.
Lieutenant Argus observed his team, feeling a surge of pride. This was more than a group of soldiers; they were a tightly knit family, prepared to face any challenge together. Now with a GRID Operative on their side, he almost felt they could be unstoppable.
Rourke glanced at Argus, noting the readiness of their team. “Looks like we’re set. Let’s show whatever the hell is out there what the Praetorians are made of.”
Argus nodded in agreement; his expression resolute. “We’re the shield of the entire GFC. Let’s move out.”
In the launch-bay’s holding area, Lieutenant Argus and Lieutenant Lysander stood ready, only the formidable three-meter advanced steel double doors separating them from the launch pad. The anticipation was palpable as both teams awaited the signal to deploy. Above the doors, a light system stood ready to indicate their clearance: red for standby, green for go.
Both teams, donned in their Praetorian armor, were a sight to behold. The armor was a marvel of military engineering, a far cry from standard issue and even from the OGDF armor. It boasted enhanced mobility systems, integrated communication and sensor arrays, and reinforced plating capable of withstanding extreme combat, and even self-aid capabilities. While there were minute areas on the armor that were exposed, due to mobility purposes, its defensive capabilities were second to none, designed to provide maximum protection without sacrificing agility.
The armor also boasted augmented strength and agility capabilities, allowing its wearer to perform at peak human efficiency. Its design was sleek yet intimidating, a perfect balance of form and function, representing the pinnacle of GFC military innovation.
Using their armor’s advanced communication system, Lieutenants Argus and Lysander coordinated with their teams. Fireteam Valor and Fireteam Honor were the epitome of readiness, a blend of fierce determination and disciplined training.
“Sir, this is Valor Six. Fireteam Valor is green, over,” Argus reported over the secure channel to Captain Orion.
Following his lead, Lysander confirmed, “Sir, Honor Six here. Fireteam Honor is green, over.”
Captain Orion’s voice crackled through the comm, “STRIKE Teams, the light is green!”
Simultaneously, the indicator light switched from red to green, and the massive steel doors began to part, their movement sending a reverberating rumble through the bay. As the doors opened, revealing the vastness of Trident’s mountainous terrain and beyond, the teams sprang into action.
Fireteam Honor dashed towards their space-bound transport, destined for the Liberation with Commander Drexel. Meanwhile, Fireteam Valor made for their specialized dropship, the STRIKE Transport. This rapid response vessel was unique to their operations, capable of carrying a maximum of four members per ship. There were two such ships at their disposal, allowing for swift and efficient deployment.
The STRIKE Transport, designed for extreme velocity descents, was a technological marvel. Equipped with pulse thrusters for rapid atmospheric exit and high-speed atmospheric re-entry, it could reach any destination on Trident within ninety seconds. Advanced inertial dampeners and a cutting-edge gravitational modulation system ensured the passengers' safety despite the intense speeds and rapid acceleration.
As the teams boarded their respective transports, the Praetorians in their advanced armor were a formidable sight. The armor was not just a defensive tool; it was a symbol of their elite status. It featured state-of-the-art technology, including a fully integrated HUD (Heads-Up Display), advanced life support systems, and a networked communications array that allowed for seamless, encrypted data and voice transmission.
With the teams loaded into their transports, the doors of the STRIKE Transports closed with a definitive hiss, sealing them inside. The engines hummed to life, a powerful yet controlled sound that resonated with the promise of swift action.
As the transports lifted off, heading towards their respective destinations, the Praetorians inside readied themselves for what lay ahead. The mission was clear, the stakes high, and the unknown vast. But one thing was certain: the Praetorians of Fireteam Valor and Fireteam Honor were prepared to face whatever challenges awaited them.
The STRIKE Transport, significantly faster than the one carrying Fireteam Honor and Commander Drexel, sliced through the atmosphere with a velocity that underscored the urgency of their mission.
Inside the transport, the soldiers were strapped in, each one mentally bracing for the rapid descent. The craft's advanced technology was the only thing standing between them and the ravages of extreme speed. Without the inertial dampeners and the gravitational modulation system, their bodies would have been subjected to unbearable G-forces, likely resulting in severe physical trauma, loss of consciousness, or death. These systems were crucial, counteracting the intense strain and keeping the team combat-ready upon arrival.
“Sixty seconds to target!” Lieutenant Argus alerted the team.
As the transport reached the upper atmospheric coordinates, its onboard computer system emitted a sharp beep of confirmation. With a controlled burn of its thrusters, the craft rapidly descended towards Trident's surface. The transition from the calm blackness of space to the blazing friction of reentry was almost instantaneous. The exterior of the transport glowed with heat, yet inside, the Fireteam Valor remained focused and unshaken.
As the STRIKE Transport carrying Fireteam Valor hurtled towards Trident, it approached the critical altitude of one hundred meters above the target. "Prepare for The Boom," Lieutenant Argus announced, his voice tinged with anticipation. The team members braced themselves, ready for the signature maneuver that was about to commence.
Wraith, standing among the team, looked out the window at the rapidly approaching ground. A hint of exhilaration in his voice, he said, "You don't know how much I've missed this part!"
Lieutenant Argus, focused and alert, held onto a strap as the transport hurtled downwards. He glanced at his team, ensuring everyone was prepared. "Get ready for forced ejection in five, four, three, two, one," he counted down firmly.
As he hit "one," the transport's reverse burst thrusters roared to life, dramatically slowing the vehicle's plummet. The bottom doors swung open, and the team members were propelled out feet-first towards the ground below.
As the transport thrusters dissipated, the team activated their suit's Graviton Arrestors – a marvel of engineering designed to counteract the pull of gravity. The Arrestors created a controlled, repelling force field, allowing each member to plummet feet-first towards the ground without the risk of bodily impact.
Wraith, equipped with GRID's advanced technology, activated his own descent mechanism. His suit deployed a series of micro-thrusters, strategically placed to provide a smoother, more controlled descent. The micro-thrusters worked in perfect harmony, adjusting his speed and trajectory with precision, ensuring an even more graceful plummet to the ground than the rest of the team. It was an elegant solution, showcasing GRID's cutting-edge advancements in personal combat gear.
The team, now just moments from landing, was ready to transition into combat mode seamlessly. The operation was underway, and with Fireteam Valor's skill and Wraith's unique capabilities, they were a formidable force ready to face the unknown challenges that awaited them on the surface.
Excitement and adrenaline surged through the team as they dropped from the transport, each member perfectly in sync. Hitting the ground with a Boom, creating a small crater for each team member upon impact in the shape of the Graviton Arrestor’s protective field, they were immediately battle-ready, weapons drawn, eyes scanning the surroundings, and a tactical formation adapting to the terrain. Surprisingly, there were no enemies in sight – only the reality of devastation.
