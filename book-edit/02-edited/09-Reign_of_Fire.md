Chapter 9 – Reign of Fire
Date: December 16th, 2888
Time: 1030 Galactic Standard Time (GTC)
Location: GFC Base - Gladius
In the war room aboard the Gladius, a somber mood prevails as the members of Fireteam Honor and Fireteam Valor are engrossed in the tactical displays. They watch with a mix of awe and concern as Admiral Greenwich's fleet, though dwindling, valiantly engages the enemy, taking down numerous hostile ships in the process.
The radio suddenly crackles to life, cutting through the tense atmosphere. "Gladius Six, this is Shadowhawk Six," Lieutenant Barkley's voice comes through, heavy with the weight of recent events. "The Liberation is lost. We had to scuttle it to avoid enemy capture. Right before we hit Trident's atmosphere, we spotted a lone enemy vessel using invisibility tech. Its destination is unknown."
Captain Orion, maintaining his composed demeanor, responds, "Shadowhawk Six, understood. What's your current status?"
"We're planetside. Had a rough landing. Air brakes failed, and we had to perform a waterborne landing roughly six kilometers southeast of New Eridanus. Additionally, we have KIAs. I will brief you in person, sir," Barkley reports, his voice reflecting the gravity of their losses.
"Copy that, Shadowhawk Six. Can you provide your exact coordinates for extraction?" Orion inquires, ready to coordinate the rescue.
"Wait one," Barkley replies, a brief pause ensuing as he accesses the automated navigation system. "Coordinates marked on your display now.”
"Received, Shadowhawk Six. We're arranging transport for your extraction now. Stay sharp and hold your position," Orion instructs, his voice resolute.
"Roger that, Gladius Six. Shadowhawk Six out."
The radio goes silent, leaving a heavy air in the room. The team members exchange solemn glances, each acutely aware of the dire situation and the sacrifices already made.
Orion turns to the Crew Chief of the Pegasus Transport ship – Chief Sussex. " We need to organize a rapid extraction for Shadowhawk Six. They're vulnerable out there, and time is not on our side. We can't afford to lose more lives."
“Already have my team getting the birds ready to fly,” Chief Sussex says with predetermined sense of tone.
“Good. Fly hard, Chief.” 
The Chief turns around, takes his helmet out from under his arm and firmly places it on his head, and runs out of the War Room.
Captain Orion arises with a renewed sense of urgency, opens multiple communications channels to coordinate with the transport units and plots the fastest route to Lieutenant Barkley's coordinates. “Less work for the Chief when he winds up his transport,” he thinks to himself.
As the situation in the war room unfolds, the sound of engines coming to life signals the commencement of a crucial mission. The Pegasus class dropship, a robust and agile craft, begins to hum with activity above them. The war room, positioned directly below the launch and landing pads, subtly vibrates with the resonance of its engines.
Concurrently, the distinct, high-pitched whine of a Razor Wing fighter cuts through the ambient noise. Its role is unmistakable: to escort the Pegasus dropship.
Amidst this backdrop, Harlan, standing by a console, reaches into his pocket and retrieves his tobacco pouch. In an uncharacteristic display of tension, he takes an unusually large pinch of Epsilon Wintergreen Longcut and places it in his mouth. This action, more pronounced than usual, is a clear indicator of the heightened stakes, even for a veteran like Harlan.
Rourke, noticing Harlan's action, comments quietly to Tessa, "I’ve never seen Harlan so uptight."
Captain Orion, observing these small but telling reactions, addresses the room with a steady and confident voice. "The Pegasus Transport is ready, and the Razor Wing is set for escort. It's imperative we bring back Fireteam Shadowhawk and Fireteam Ironclad safely. They hold critical intelligence from The Surgeon and updates on Admiral Greenwich's engagement above the planet. Our strategy moving forward hinges on this information."
He pauses to ensure his message is absorbed, then adds, "Valor and Honor, prepare for the next mission brief as soon as the team’s land. Review your protocols and be ready to act swiftly based on the new intelligence. Precision and readiness are key in this upcoming operation."
With this, the war room becomes a hub of focused activity. Communications with the pilots of the Pegasus Transport and the Razor Wing are streamlined and efficient, ensuring coordinated and successful operations.
Outside, the engines of another Pegasus dropship powers up, emitting a deep, resolute thrum that mirrors the determination of each team inside. A second Razor Wing, poised and lethal, gets ready for takeoff, its engines providing a sharp counterpoint to the dropship’s steady rumble.
In these moments, the war room and the aircraft above are synchronized in a singular, vital mission – the safe extraction of Shadowhawk and Ironclad. This operation showcases the intricate dance of teamwork and precision that characterizes military endeavors, highlighting the unwavering spirit and commitment of those who serve.
The members of Fireteam Valor and Fireteam Honor, caught in the tension of awaiting their next orders, find a momentary escape in the camaraderie that often unites soldiers in such times.
Rourke, seizing the moment to lighten the mood, looks at Harlan with a playful smirk. "Harlan, I can smell that candy-ass Epsilon Wintergreen shit from here." he jokes, his laughter echoing in the room.
Reaching into his pocket, Rourke produces a bag of Red Man chewing tobacco and tosses it to Harlan. "Try some Red Man, Harlan.”
Catching the pouch, Harlan raises an eyebrow in surprise. "Red Man? I thought Red Man died with Old Earth."
Rourke grins, "Let’s just say, I know a guy in logistics who knows a guy. Some seed vault on Old Earth had a stash of tobacco seeds. They're growing it in agricultural stations now. Rare as hell, but nothing beats the original, eh?"
Harlan chuckles, the tension in the room easing slightly. "Thanks, Rourke. Didn't know you were such a connoisseur. Let me switch this crap out!" His retort is light, the camaraderie evident. 
Harlan casually leans over to the garbage nearest Tessa, spitting out the wintergreen with a practiced ease before reaching for the bag of Red Man.
Tessa, observing this, wrinkles her nose in mock disgust and quips, "Really, Harlan? Could you at least pretend to be civilized? You're making me reconsider my life choices just by watching you. Should have joined STARCOM Fleet, instead!"
Her comment draws a fresh round of chuckles from the team, and Harlan, with a grin, retorts, "Tessa, in the field, manners are the first casualty. You should know that by now."
He opens the bag of Red Man, carefully selecting a pinch. "But I'll try to keep it classy just for you," he adds, winking at Tessa as he places the chew in his mouth, ever-so delicately, a tiny dribble of Red Man saliva peeking through the left side of his mouth.
Rourke laughs, "There you go, Harlan. Embracing the old ways. Tessa, you'll get used to it. It's just another day,” he pauses and glances over at Harlan, “Dammit Harlan, don’t take all of it!”
Harlan lets out a chuckle, “Don’t worry, I only took half!” He exclaimed, throwing the bag of Red Man back to Rourke.
After Rourke catches the Red Man, he shifts his attention to Royce, "Hey, rook," Rourke calls out, a mischievous glint in his eye. "I've only ever seen you chew gum. How about letting your balls drop for a change?" He saunters over to Royce, holding the bag of Red Man open just enough for him to get a good whiff.
Royce, trying to maintain his composure under Rourke's teasing gaze, retorts smartly, "I'm just trying to keep my habits as refined as my company, Rourke." Despite his words, he tentatively reaches into the bag, thinking about the mistake he is going to make.
As Royce attempts to partake in the ritual of chewing tobacco under Rourke's playful scrutiny. He cautiously takes the smallest pinch of Red Man, an amount so minuscule it's almost comical.
As he tentatively places the chew in his mouth, trying to appear nonchalant, it quickly becomes apparent that he's out of his depth. Within moments, his face twists in discomfort, and he accidentally swallows the tobacco. He starts coughing violently, the unexpected reaction causing him to spit out the remnants onto the floor.
The room erupts into laughter at the sight, and Harlan, unable to contain his amusement, quips, "Christ rook, I never took you for a swallower!" His comment only adds to the hilarity of the situation.
Royce, still coughing and trying to rid his mouth of the unpleasant taste, manages to retort between coughs, "Screw you, Harlan!" His response, though sharp, is delivered with a half-smile, acknowledging the joke at his expense.
Tessa, amidst her laughter, adds her own playful remark, "Well, looks like Royce is fitting right in. Not sure if that's a good thing or a bad thing."
The laughter and banter serve as a much-needed release for the team, temporarily lifting the weight of the situation they are in. It's a reminder of the importance of camaraderie and light-heartedness, even in the face of danger.
As the light-hearted banter continues in the war room at Gladius, Argus finds himself momentarily lost in laughter. However, his mind can't help but drift to thoughts of his family: Julia, Kat, and Junior. The concern for their safety amidst the escalating situation grows too strong to ignore. Seizing a moment, he steps just outside the war room, close enough to return quickly if needed, but far enough to have a private conversation.
He dials his home number, his heart racing with a mix of apprehension and longing. The phone rings, and after a few heartbeats, Julia's voice comes through, laced with worry. "Mark, what’s going on? The news is talking about aliens and a battle above the planet."
Argus takes a deep breath, steadying his voice. "Julia, listen carefully. I need you to remember the combination to the safe. I'll be back as soon as I can, but you need to be prepared. I’m not sure how long I’ll be on mission."
Julia's voice trembles slightly, "Mark, are we in danger?"
"Remember our emergency plan," Argus says firmly. "If it comes down to it, use the Nightstalker pistols. If you're within twenty feet of a target, use your pointer finger along the side of the muzzle protectors. The laser sight is there if you need it. Show Kat how to use them, just in case." He pauses, knowing what he is about to tell them is classified, but his love for family urges him to speak candidly, “Junior should already know how to use it. And Julia, there’s an incoming alien invasion. They will be planetside today. I’m going to be home as soon as I possibly can.”
From just inside the war room, Captain Orion overhears bits of Argus's conversation. He doesn't interrupt, but a look of respect and understanding crosses his face, knowing the difficult balance between duty and family. He knows the situation is dire. Despite hearing classified information being leaked to his Lieutenant’s family, the captain understood. He would have done the same, had his family been stationed on Trident with him. Fortunately for Captain Orion, his family is in the safe confines of their estate on Veridian Prime.
Argus then addresses his children, his voice filled with warmth and concern. "Junior, Kat, I love you both so much. Junior, I need you to be strong and look after Mom and Kat. Kat, help your brother and listen to Mom, okay?"
"Dad, the news said aliens... There are lights in the sky. I'm scared," Junior's voice is small and filled with fear.
"Listen to me, both of you. Be brave, help Mom, and look out for each other. I'll be home as soon as I can. You're strong, and I'm proud of you," Argus reassures them, his heart aching with each word.
"Mark, please be safe. We'll be waiting," Julia whispers.
"I will. Stay safe, lock the doors, and remember, I love you all," Argus responds, ending the call with a heavy heart.
Taking a moment to compose himself, Argus steps back into the war room, his expression stoic but his eyes betraying deep concern. The team, focused on the upcoming tasks, doesn't notice the subtle change in him. Argus rejoins them, ready to face the challenges ahead, his resolve to protect his family fueling his determination.
As the laughter dies down and the team starts to regain their composure, they refocus on the task at hand. 
Fifteen minutes pass in the war room at Gladius, each minute stretching out like an hour under the weight of anticipation. The distant but unmistakable sounds of the Pegasus dropships and their escorting Razor Wings landing above them finally break the silence. The arrival of Fireteam Ironclad and Fireteam Shadowhawk signals a shift in the atmosphere. It's time for focused action and sharp attention.
Captain Orion, a figure of authority and calm, steps out to meet the newly arrived teams on the launch pad. The heavy thud of their boots on the metal surface echoes in the corridor as he escorts them down to the war room. Along the way, Orion offers sincere condolences for their fallen comrades, his words imbued with respect and empathy.
"If Admiral Greenwich chose you for his personal guard, then truly, you are all 'None Better'," Orion states, his voice firm yet filled with a warm sense of conviction. His words serve as a reminder of their skill and dedication, offering a measure of comfort amidst the losses they've endured.
Lieutenant Barkley and Lieutenant Charles from Ironclad and Shadowhawk walk alongside Captain Orion, their faces etched with the fatigue and intensity of their recent ordeal. They share details of the brutal encounter with the aliens and the tragic loss of Commander Drexel. "It was hell up there," Barkley says, his voice low. "We had to make a tactical retreat to the escape pods and order a nuclear strike on The Liberation. It was the only way."
Charles adds, his tone laced with a mixture of sorrow and determination, "These aliens... they're unlike anything we've faced. We need to be prepared for their brutality. As they enter the war room, Fireteam’s Valor and Honor rise to greet their fellow Praetorianse. Handshakes and solemn nods are exchanged, a silent language of warriors acknowledging each other's experiences and losses.
"Welcome back," Rourke says, his usual joviality tempered with seriousness. "We heard about The Liberation. Tough break."
Harlan, standing beside Tessa, adds quietly, "You did what had to be done. You can’t predict what happens when you’re in the shit."
Tessa, her gaze sweeping over the weary faces of Fireteam Ironclad and Fireteam Shadowhawk, says, "You look like hell. Let’s make them pay.”
The room settles into a focused hush, all eyes turning towards Captain Orion as he steps forward to commence the briefing. The faces around him, a blend of stoicism and resolve, are a testament to the unyielding spirit of the Praetorian teams. They stand united, ready to face the next challenge, their determination fueled by the losses they've endured and the unbreakable bond they share as defenders of their planet and their loved ones.
Captain Orion stands before the assembled Praetorians, his demeanor embodying both the gravity of the situation and the unyielding resolve of a seasoned leader. "Men, women, Praetorians," he begins, his voice cutting through the tense atmosphere, "you're all now under my command. As of a few minutes ago, Admiral Greenwich's communications went completely dark. That only means one thing. The fight is coming to the surface."
The room reacts instantly; every Praetorian sits up even straighter, their eyes reflecting a mix of anger and determination. There's fear too, an acknowledgment of the unknown, but it's overshadowed by an unwavering resolve to face whatever comes.
Orion continues, delving into the tactical details. "The aliens have destroyed star outposts Echo, Fox, November, Sierra and Zulu, on the outskirts of our star system. No traces left. Then they systematically disabled our long-range communications and automated planetary defense platforms. Fortunately, Admiral Greenwich managed to save a few orbital titan cannons before his fleet was overwhelmed."
He pauses, letting the information sink in. "Make no mistake, we still don't know exactly what we're up against. All we know is that they're far more advanced than us, but they can bleed, and they can die! Evacuation protocols have already been successful in Epsilon City, Titan City and some outlying villages. New Eridanus and Crete city is about to be evacuated within the next 20 minutes. As a precaution, all evacuees are being transported to Atlantis Underwater Contingency Station, save for those in Crete City," he paused and looked at each of his Praetorians, “The civilians of Crete City will be immediately escorted to the emergency shuttles to get off planet.”
He looks around the room, his eyes meeting those of his soldiers. "OGDF forces and our Razor Wing squadrons are being armed to the teeth. I suggest you all refit. Look at the soldiers to your left and right. This may be the last time you see each other alive. The enemy may overrun the planet. We might all die before we can escort the civilians to safety. Remember, men, you’re our elite. The GFC’s might. We will all fight and die if we have to!"
At his words, a palpable energy surges through the room. Each Praetorian, fueled by a blend of duty and adrenaline, joins in a unifying battle chant. It's a powerful, motivating sound, echoing the resilience and fighting spirit of those prepared to defend their home against all odds. The chant, a hallmark of the Praetorian ethos, reverberates through the war room, a sound that is more than just noise – it's a declaration of their readiness to face the imminent battle, a testament to their courage and unbreakable will.
Captain Orion's voice booms across the war room, "MEN! Praetorian Ethos! Go!" In unison, every member of the Praetorian teams stands at attention, their voices rising together in a powerful recitation of their creed. Captain Orion discreetly activates the intercom system of Gladius, broadcasting their unified chant to every soldier waiting to fight, infusing them with the same unyielding spirit.
The Praetorians, their voices resonant and clear, recite:
We are the sword.
We are the shield.
We are the fury of the Galactic Forge Coalition and the guardians of STARCOM Fleet.
In silence, we strike.
In direct contact, we overwhelm and conquer.
We are the first to fight and the last to live.
We are unbreakable, unstoppable.
We live. We fight. We die.
We are Praetorians!


“We live. We fight. We die.” Captain Orion whispered to himself. 
As the final words of the ethos echo through the base, a palpable sense of determination and unity fills the air. The ethos, more than just words, is a manifestation of their collective spirit – a reminder of their duty, their resilience, and their commitment to defend humanity against any threat.
Captain Orion, looking around at the assembled soldiers, sees the impact of their words. The ethos has not only solidified the resolve of the Praetorians but has also inspired the rest of the soldiers on Gladius, reminding them of the importance of their fight and the strength they possess as a united front.
The war room, now filled with a renewed sense of purpose and conviction, is ready to face whatever challenges lie ahead. The Praetorians, standing shoulder to shoulder, are a formidable force, prepared to give their all in the defense of their planet and their people.
As Captain Orion's command to refit and prepare for deployment echoes in the war room, the very foundation of the Gladius suddenly shudders with an ominous ferocity. It starts as a low rumble, almost imperceptible, but rapidly crescendos into a violent shaking that grips the entire structure. Dust cascades from the ceiling like a gritty, gray waterfall, coating everything in a fine layer. The ground beneath them quivers and heaves, creating an unsettling sensation of the earth moving under their feet.
The walls groan under the strain, and shelves lining the room rattle violently. Equipment and personal items that once sat securely are now jostled from their perches, clattering to the floor in a cacophony of noise. Glass objects shatter upon impact, adding to the growing disarray. The once orderly war room now resembles a scene of chaos, reflecting the turmoil unfolding outside.
Through the windows, the Praetorians witness a harrowing sight. The sky, streaked with menacing shades of red and orange, is alive with the fire of the enemy's onslaught. Explosive projectiles rain down from above, each impact sending shockwaves that are felt even within the sturdy confines of the base. In the distance, New Eridanus is engulfed in a maelstrom of destruction, its once serene skyline now marred by plumes of smoke and devastating explosions.
The severity of the situation hits with full force. Argus, momentarily paralyzed by shock, stares out the window, his heart racing with dread. Thoughts of his family, their safety now uncertain, flood his mind. The physical shaking of the room mirrors the turmoil inside him, a tumultuous blend of fear and determination.
Captain Orion, maintaining his composure amidst the chaos, raises his voice to cut through the din. "Men! Fraggo! We take the fight to New Eridanus, now! You have ten minutes to gear up and get your asses to the launch pad. We deploy in STRIKE Transports. We are evacuating the citizens and killing every one of those alien bastards on the way out! Now move it out! Double time!”
Spurred into action by Orion's commands, the Praetorians quickly gather their bearings amidst the trembling room. They rush to the arms room, each step, deliberate and urgent. The base continues to quiver with each new impact from the ongoing assault.
In the arms room of Gladius, the Praetorian teams engage in a flurry of activity, each Praetorian focused on their crucial role in the impending mission. The room is alive with the sounds of preparation: the clanking of ammunition, the securing of gear, and the purposeful movement of seasoned soldiers.
Harlan immediately heads to the ammunition racks. He stocks up on specialized ammo, ensuring his Shredder is ready for the intense combat ahead. His eyes then scan the explosives section, selecting an array of grenades and demolition charges, tools essential for both offense and defense in the unpredictable situations they might encounter. Before leaving the arms room, Harlan grabs an extra can of his favored Epsilon Wintergreen Longcut, a small comfort in the face of uncertainty.
Each team member is equally focused on their preparations, aware that hydration and sustenance are key in prolonged engagements. They attach hydration packs to their battle armor's integrated water containment systems, ensuring easy access to water without the need to remove their helmets. Liquid food packs are swiftly loaded into their suit's dispensary systems, providing them with nutrient-rich sustenance during the mission.
Ammunition is a top priority. Every soldier grabs as much as they can carry, mindful of the uncertainty of when they might be able to refit. The variety of ammunition reflects the diverse weaponry of the team, ranging from standard rifle rounds to specialized munitions.
As the Praetorians continue to equip themselves, there's a sense of urgency mixed with an unspoken understanding of the gravity of their mission. They exchange brief nods and words of encouragement, a nonverbal language that speaks volumes of their camaraderie and mutual respect.
Finally, the teams are ready. Loaded with equipment and mentally prepared for the battle, they head to the launch pad where the STRIKE Transports await. The atmosphere is charged with a blend of determination and anticipation as they board the transports, ready to confront the unknown challenges that await them in New Eridanus.
The launch pad at Gladius base buzzes with intense activity as the massive metal doors creak open, revealing the prepped and ready STRIKE Transports. The imposing sight of these transports, coupled with the urgency of the mission, adds to the palpable sense of determination among the Praetorian teams.
Captain Orion, his voice clear and authoritative, directs each team to their designated transport. "Valor, Honor, Ironclad, Shadowhawk, you each have your transport. Let's move out efficiently and with purpose," he commands.
To the right of the Praetorian's STRIKE Transports, three platoons of the Orbital Ground Defense Force are boarding Pegasus Dropships. The distinctive sound of Razor Wings whirring up provides a backdrop of readiness and power.
Orion quickly pulls the Praetorian lieutenants - Lysander, Argus, Charles, and Barkley - aside and introduces them to the OGDF lieutenants. "These are your OGDF counterparts," he states. "Coordinate with them once you're on the ground. Your first task is to secure a landing zone for the Pegasus Dropships. You'll have Razor Wing support."

With the briefing concluded, the teams board their respective ships. The air is thick with anticipation and focus as the STRIKE Transports and Razor Wings prepare for takeoff.
The Pegasus Dropships and Razor Wings are the first to stir into action. Their engines ignite, sending a chorus of powerful roars and high-pitched whines slicing through the air. One by one, the Pegasus Dropships, each carrying a platoon of OGDF soldiers, lift off gracefully yet with a sense of urgency, their bulky forms surprisingly agile as they ascend into the sky. Accompanying them, the Razor Wings ascend with sleek, predatory grace, their engines emitting a piercing whir as they take their positions, ready to escort and protect the dropships.

Each member of the team finds their way to their designated drop seats, the familiar process executed with practiced efficiency. The interior of the transport is a hive of activity as the Praetorians strap themselves in, checking their gear one last time and securing themselves for the imminent launch.

The rapid ascent is a rush of adrenaline, the sheer force pressing each soldier into their seats. Outside, the sky transforms from a serene blue to the darker shades of the upper atmosphere, a visual marker of their transition from the known to the unknown.
As they breach the atmosphere's edge, the transports align for their drop, the Pegasus Dropships and Razor Wings already ahead, cutting through the sky towards the battle zone. The moment is charged with a potent mix of fear and determination, each Praetorian acutely aware of what awaits them below.
