Chapter 13 – The Discovery
Date: December 16, 2888
Time: 1907 Galactic Standard Time (GTC)
Location: GFC Base: Gladius – GRID Section 47 – 2,000 meters underground
The room that housed both Dr. Voss and the enigmatic artifact was a sealed chamber, designed to contain and analyze extraterrestrial objects. It was stark, with the clean lines and minimalist design characteristic of advanced research facilities. The lighting was subdued yet precise, casting an ethereal glow on the artifact at the room's core. Surrounded by an array of sophisticated equipment, the space felt more like a sanctum, a world apart from the war raging outside.
At the center of this meticulous setup stood the artifact: an object of indefinable materials, its surface a complex tapestry of textures that seemed both ancient and impossibly advanced. The artifact was roughly cylindrical, with undulating surfaces that suggested an organic inspiration, yet its composition was clearly the product of a technology far beyond Human understanding. With almost every interaction from Dr. Voss, the artifact came alive, its surface illuminating with an array of symbols that resembled hieroglyphics - a language of light that defied translation. These symbols pulsed with an otherworldly energy, hinting at knowledge and power locked within.
Dr. Voss's assistant in this endeavor was a semi-sentient artificial intelligence, codename Athena. Athena, a marvel of GRID's technological prowess, was an ever-learning system designed to adapt and evolve in response to new stimuli. Pre-programmed to follow Dr. Voss's directives, Athena assisted in the analysis of the artifact, taking meticulous notes, running simulations, and attempting to decode the luminescent symbols that defied Human understanding. Despite Athena's advanced capabilities, the artifact's language remained elusive, a cipher without a key.
Athena's physical manifestation in the lab was subtle, designed for efficiency over form. Its interface was projected holographically when needed, displaying data and analyses in floating, holographic screens that Dr. Voss could manipulate with gestures. The AI's voice, when it communicated, was neutral, designed to be calming yet devoid of unnecessary emotion, a constant companion in the quest for understanding.
In the meticulously arranged research chamber deep within Gladius, Dr. Elena Voss stood before the artifact, her gaze locked onto its complex, enigmatic surface. She reached out, her fingers brushing against it in a deliberate pattern she had deduced from weeks of study. This action, a calculated hypothesis based on the artifact's previous responses, was meant to probe deeper into its mysteries.
As she initiated contact, the room's ambiance shifted, the air around the artifact shimmering with anticipation. "Activating experimental sequence ninety-nine," Dr. Voss announced, her voice steady despite the adrenaline coursing through her.
Athena projected a holographic display next to her, its screens flickering with data. "Analysis suggests a potential energy spike, Dr. Voss. Please proceed with caution," Athena advised, its voice embodying the balance between concern and curiosity.
Dr. Voss, her focus unwavering, responded, "This is different, Athena. I'm not looking for energy spikes. I believe the artifact senses and reacts to specific Human neural patterns when touched. We're not just unlocking it; we're communicating."
At that moment, the artifact activated, not with its previously observed beam of light, but by enveloping Dr. Voss in an aura of swirling symbols, mathematics, equations, and scripts of an unknown language. These symbols, aglow with a soft luminescence, danced around her, creating an immersive display of alien knowledge.
"It appears you are correct, Dr. Voss. The artifact is interacting with you on a level we have not seen. The patterns—they are complex, but not random. There is purpose," Athena observed, its tone betraying a hint of wonder.
Dr. Voss, engulfed in the artifact's light, smiled amidst the swirling equations. "This is it, Athena. It's not just showing us; it's teaching, sharing... I can almost feel the knowledge," she whispered, her voice filled with awe.
Suddenly, the display intensified. The equations and symbols accelerated, coalescing into two streams that funneled directly into Dr. Voss's eyes. Overwhelmed by the sudden influx of alien information, she convulsed, unable to maintain her stance.
Athena, detecting the physiological distress, immediately sent an alert to Wraith, who was occupied in a nearby lab with classified GRID research involving quantum encryption barriers. He rushed into the room just in time to catch Dr. Voss as she collapsed, her body wracked by the artifact's unfathomable transfer of knowledge.
When Dr. Voss regained consciousness, gasping for air with her eyes wide open, she was met with the concerned faces of Athena's holographic projection and Wraith at her side. "I know what we're supposed to do," she said, her voice a mix of determination and wonder. The artifact's message, now a part of her, had unveiled a path forward, a revelation that promised to alter the course of their conflict with the Obsidians.
Wraith, his concern evident, gently asked, "Are you alright, Doctor?"
Breathing steadily now, she managed a weak smile, "I've never felt this good. It's like my mind is alive with possibilities and knowledge I have to sort out. I need time to understand all of this."
His curiosity piqued, Wraith probed further, "What did you mean when you said you know what we're supposed to do?"
Dr. Voss, her eyes reflecting a universe of newfound knowledge, began to speak, her words tumbling out in a torrent. "The information, it came in so fast, like a deluge, and I'm still sifting through it, trying to understand. But one thing is clear—the artifact," she paused, searching for the right words, "there's not just this one. There are exactly fifty, and another one is here, on this planet. We're meant to be its," she paused, trying to find the words, “Harbingers. Yes that’s it. Harbingers.”
Wraith, still holding Elena steady, asked, "Who's 'we'? And what does that mean?" trying to grasp the full scope of her revelation.
Elena, her breathing steadying, locked eyes with him. “Humanity. Specifically, only five Humans if I’m correct in my data sorting. Five harbingers. From the feeling I’m getting, to be a Harbinger is to be a guardian of sorts," she stated with newfound clarity. The revelation not only marked a significant turning point in her research but also hinted at a destiny intertwining humanity with these ancient celestial relics.
She shook her head slightly, the enormity of the revelation still settling in her mind. "What that means in its entirety, I'm not sure, yet. I'm still trying to piece together the influx of thoughts, the connections. It's like trying to follow a dozen threads at once."
Athena, ever the diligent assistant, offered her computational prowess. "Dr. Voss, if you require assistance in organizing your thoughts, I am equipped to handle multiple streams of data simultaneously. It might be beneficial to vocalize your insights for external analysis."
Dr. Voss, grateful for the offer, nodded. "Yes, Athena, I could use your help. There's so much to unpack here, and we need to understand what being Harbingers means." Together, they embarked on a journey to decipher the profound secrets entrusted to Dr. Voss by the artifact, a mission that would undoubtedly shape the destiny of humanity and its role in the universe.
At a moment’s notice, Elena had an astonishing revelation, “The next artifact is underground. Beneath New Eridanus. We need to find it before the Obsidians do."
Wraith, absorbing the gravity of her revelation, simply acknowledged, "Noted."
Understanding the immediate need to act, Wraith made his way to the base's rapid transport system, a cutting-edge Hyper-Velocity Lift. This marvel of engineering, capable of covering the two-thousand meters from the ground floor to the surface in mere seconds, used a combination of magnetic levitation and vacuum propulsion to eliminate air resistance, providing a swift and smooth ascent.
The lift, a seamless blend of GRID and the GFC technology, initiated its rapid ascent. Wraith's thoughts raced as he was propelled upwards, the weight of the mission pressing down on him.
Upon arrival at the surface level, Wraith met with The Surgeon and Captain Orion. The atmosphere was charged with urgency, their focus sharpened by the knowledge of what lay beneath New Eridanus.
"Before you call GRIDCOM, we already have a strategic asset perfect for artifact extraction. I noticed Lieutenant Strickland and his platoon moving out with the Praetorians to New Eridanus. Recall them."
Wraith's confusion was evident. "Why them, and not Praetorians?"
The Surgeon, with a firmness that brooked no argument, stated, "Call GRIDCOM now. It's not my place to inform you. You simply do not have the proper clearance for further information."
Bewildered, Wraith couldn't grasp the situation. Not having the proper clearance? As a GRID Operative, he was privy to nearly all levels of classified information. What did Lieutenant Strickland and his OGDF platoon possess that the Praetorians did not?
Captain Orion, sensing Wraith's confusion, added, "In all fairness, Fireteam Valor fought with Strickland and his platoon on planet Crete against the insurgents." He detailed the platoon's brutal tactics and the uncanny luck that seemed to favor them, even more so than their Praetorian counterparts. "Possibly the only OGDF platoon to outshine Praetorians," he concluded.
Wraith was escorted to the secure communications room.
Wraith, standing in the communications suite of Gladius, initiated a secure call to GRIDCOM, his mind racing with the implications of Dr. Voss's revelation and the urgent need to act on it. As the line connected, encrypting the communication to prevent any potential eavesdropping, Wraith relayed the situation with a precision that belied the urgency of the message.
"The Surgeon recommends recalling Viper Platoon to Gladius for an immediate briefing on a new mission concerning the artifact discovery," Wraith reported, his voice steady.
The voice on the other end acknowledged the urgency, "Understood. We'll implement the recall of Viper Platoon under the guise of being an armed escort for civilians currently with Fireteam Ironclad. A Pegasus transport is already en-route to facilitate this maneuver, ensuring the Praetorians suspect nothing out of the ordinary."
Wraith, though accustomed to following orders without question, found himself compelled to ask, "May I inquire why Viper Platoon is specifically requested for this mission, and not Praetorians?"
It was not typical of Wraith to question directives, especially given his background as a former Praetorian—a testament to the soft spot he retained for his comrades-in-arms. The voice offered a succinct explanation, "GRID has a longstanding, undisclosed relationship with Lieutenant Strickland of Viper Platoon. That's the extent of what can be shared until you're granted further clearance."
The response left Wraith perplexed. His role as a GRID Operative had always provided him with access to a wide berth of classified information, yet here he was, faced with a directive shrouded in mystery.
Captain Orion's earlier words echoed in his mind, recounting Viper Platoon's notorious reputation for brutality and uncanny fortune on the battlefield, particularly during the conflict on Planet Crete. It seemed they possessed an edge, even over the esteemed Praetorians, making them uniquely suited for the challenges of extracting the newly discovered artifact.
In the war room, a strategic hub throbbing with the pulse of impending actions, Wraith reported to Captain Orion and The Surgeon, "Viper Platoon is a go." The weight of the situation pressed heavily in the room, a tangible echo of the urgency that propelled their mission forward.
Captain Orion, without a moment's hesitation, approached the communications array near the podium. With practiced precision, he dialed in the secure frequency to Ironclad Six, ensuring the line was encrypted against any potential eavesdropping. "Ironclad Six, this is Gladius Six. Ensure Viper Platoon escorts the civilians back to Gladius as planned. It's imperative they arrive without delay."
Turning back to the room, Wraith leaned in, his voice low but firm, " I trust you will keep this information classified. None of what we are briefing to Viper Platoon will leave the room."
Orion met Wraith's gaze, a flicker of understanding passing between them. "You have my word," he replied with a nod. "The importance of discretion cannot be overstated. This operation hinges on our ability to move undetected and unanticipated. The walls have ears, but they won't hear anything from this room."
The gravity of the conversation underscored the clandestine nature of their endeavor. The success of their mission not only depended on the swift recall and briefing of Viper Platoon but also on the absolute secrecy surrounding their objectives.
With the directive clearly understood, Orion's next step was to ensure the seamless execution of the recall. The Pegasus transport's arrival and Viper Platoon's redeployment were critical moves in a larger strategy, a chess game played against an unseen opponent whose moves were unpredictable but invariably hostile.
The Surgeon, usually a figure enveloped in his own machinations and dark methodologies, remained silent, his thoughts inscrutable but undoubtedly aligned with the mission's success. His earlier interventions, often walking a fine line of ethical quandaries, now played a pivotal role in the gathering storm they faced.
