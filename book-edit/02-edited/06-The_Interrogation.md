Chapter 6 – The Interrogation
Date: December 15, 2888
Time: 2330 Galactic Standard Time (GTC)
Location: GFC Base: Gladius

In the dimly lit interrogation chamber, The Surgeon stood as a figure of calculated dread. As an amplified Mindreaver, he had an arsenal of techniques at his disposal, many of which skirted the edges of legality and morality. His tools, specifically designed for GRID and shrouded in secrecy, were as effective as they were controversial:
Mind Fracture Device: This ominous apparatus, resembling a helmet, was placed over the alien's head. It sent sporadic, jarring pulses into the brain, designed to fracture the subject's mental defenses, making them more susceptible to divulging information.
Echo Chamber: This device created a soundproof field around the alien, amplifying its own cries and screams internally. The psychological effect of hearing its own distress without respite served to break down its resolve.
Psyche Shredder: A small, handheld tool that emitted a focused beam causing acute neurological pain. It was used to target specific parts of the alien's body, eliciting pain responses without leaving physical marks.
Phantom Limb Inducer: This device created the illusion of extreme pain in limbs or appendages, a sensation that felt real to the subject but caused no actual physical harm.
Reverie Extractor: A sophisticated device that tapped into the alien's sensory inputs, flooding its mind with disorienting and distressing hallucinations, blurring the line between reality and nightmare.
Temporal Lobe Agitator: By stimulating the temporal lobes, this tool induced intense feelings of déjà vu and disorientation, making it difficult for the alien to concentrate or resist questioning.
Fear Amplifier: Emitting low-frequency vibrations, this device triggered primal fear responses in the brain, leaving the alien in a heightened state of anxiety and vulnerability.
Memory Prodder: This tool, when activated, caused a sharp spike in the brain's memory centers, forcing the alien to relive traumatic or stressful memories repeatedly.
These devices are just the tip of the iceberg. There are more at The Surgeon’s disposal.
As The Surgeon employed these tools, he also used his Mindreaving abilities to amplify their effects, infiltrating the alien's mental barriers, seeking cracks and fissures in its psyche. The combination of physical and psychological torment was designed to overwhelm the alien's mental defenses, extracting crucial information without causing permanent physical damage.
The Surgeon worked with a chilling detachment, observing the alien's reactions and adjusting his techniques accordingly. His probing was relentless, each method meticulously calculated to extract the maximum amount of information. The alien, despite its physiological differences, was not immune to the onslaught of mental and physical stimuli.
The Surgeon's methods were beginning to yield results. As the alien resisted, The Surgeon's excitement grew. "This is getting tense for you. Your resistance is impeccable, but not unbreakable!" he remarked with a sinister grin. "You're making this all too fun for me!" His laugh, a blend of amusement and malice, echoed off the cold walls of the chamber.
"Now, let's make you more comfortable!" he said with a mocking tone. Approaching the alien, The Surgeon began to methodically strip off its armor and helmet. Each piece was forcibly removed, revealing the creature beneath.
As the armor came off, the alien's true form was exposed. It was a stark contrast to the imposing figure it had presented in its battle attire. The alien's skin was a mottled grey, smooth and seemingly seamless, almost like a layer of natural armor. Its body was slender yet muscular, built for agility. The head was  slightly elongated, with no hair, and its face was a tapestry of unique features: large, dull grey eyes, a slit-like mouth with sharp teeth, and a narrow-tipped nose. The ears were mere indentations on the side of its head. Its fingers were long and ended in what appeared to be delicate, yet potentially lethal hand-like claws.
With the alien now fully exposed, The Surgeon looked over his subject with a mix of curiosity and anticipation. "Now, this is where the real fun begins," he declared with a tone of sinister glee.
He stepped closer, his tools ready, each one more intimidating than the last. The Surgeon was in his element, a master of his craft, about to delve deeper into the psyche and physiology of this extraterrestrial being. The information he could extract now was potentially invaluable, and he was determined to uncover every secret, every piece of knowledge hidden within this alien captive.
In the grim confines of the interrogation chamber, The Surgeon stood poised to begin his most invasive interrogation yet. The alien, a vulnerable subject under his unrelenting gaze.
The Surgeon leaned in close to the alien, his tools arrayed ominously beside him. "Don't worry, I don't need to know your language to understand your thoughts," he whispered, his voice dripping with malevolence. "I just want to know what plans you have. Sound good? Don't worry. The more you resist, the more I will enjoy this."
He started with a device that looked innocuous but was fiendishly effective – the Neural Echo Amplifier. As it whirred to life, the alien's eyes widened in terror. The Surgeon placed the device near the alien's temple, and it emitted a low hum. The alien's body tensed, its skin shimmering with a sheen of sweat as waves of pain coursed through its neural pathways. The creature's mouth opened in a silent scream, its eyes conveying a depth of agony.
The Surgeon watched, fascinated, as the alien's thoughts began to surface, unguarded and raw. Images of distant worlds, star charts, and military formations flickered through the alien's mind. Each snippet of thought was a piece of the puzzle, revealing the alien's origins, their technological advancements, and hints of a larger, more ominous plan.
"Interesting," The Surgeon mused, his fingers deftly adjusting the settings on another tool, the Agony Synthesizer. This device didn't just cause pain; it recreated the sensation of the alien's worst fears and traumas. The alien writhed, its body contorting as it relived its darkest moments. Through its eyes, The Surgeon saw glimpses of a harsh, unforgiving world, brutal training regimens, and a culture that glorified the thrill of battle and the glory of death by a worthy adversary.
He employed the Mind Rend, a tool that fragmented the alien's thoughts, scattering them like leaves in the wind. It was a disorienting, terrifying experience for the alien, whose mental defenses were crumbling under the onslaught. Through the chaos, The Surgeon extracted fragmented thoughts – plans of invasion, strategic targets, and an obsession with a mysterious artifact of immense power.
The alien, now gasping for breath, its body slick with perspiration, was pushed to the brink. The Surgeon's relentless pursuit of information was yielding results, but at the cost of the alien's physical and mental integrity.
"Ah, there it is," The Surgeon exclaimed as a clear thought emerged – a detailed map of a significant strategic location, crucial to the alien's plans. He smiled, a cold, satisfied grin. "You see, as honorable as you may seem, your thoughts betray you, my dear friend."
Throughout the interrogation, The Surgeon's manipulation of pain and fear was masterful, a dark art honed to perfection. The alien, once a figure of imposing strength, was reduced to a broken, trembling form, it’s will eroded by the unyielding torment.
As the interrogation intensified, Captain Orion entered the chamber, his expression a mix of concern and disapproval. "Surgeon, these methods..." he began, but The Surgeon cut him off.
"Captain, you must understand the gravity of this," The Surgeon interjected, his tone firm. "GRID has been monitoring alien activity for months. Star outpost after star outpost, obliterated without a trace. But now, we have a chance for answers."
Orion's gaze shifted between The Surgeon and the writhing alien. The brutal methods conflicted with his sense of honor, but he knew the stakes were high. The alien threat had been a shadow, elusive and deadly. Now, they had an opportunity to uncover the truth.
The Surgeon turned back to the alien, a twisted smile on his lips. "I love answers," he said, almost whimsically. "And you, my friend, are going to provide them."
The Surgeon's interrogation of the alien, methodical and relentless, continued in the stark chamber. The Mind Fracture Device, fastened securely around the creature's head, sent jolts of disorienting energy into its brain. The Surgeon watched intently as the alien's mental barriers began to crumble, revealing fragmented thoughts and glimpses of strategic information.
As the interrogation progressed with the use of the Echo Chamber and Psyche Shredder, the alien's resistance weakened even more. The Surgeon's expertise was evident in his precise application of each tool, balancing the infliction of pain with the need to keep the subject conscious and responsive.
It was the Memory Prodder that unearthed the most critical information. The alien's memories, once obscured by layers of mental fortitude, now lay bare. The Surgeon discovered that the alien was part of an elite military outfit known as the Dra'Kor Vhel'Tan. This revelation shed new light on the situation; the alien was not merely a scout but a highly trained operative.
The Surgeon delved deeper, uncovering that the Dra'Kor Vhel'Tan, along with several other teams, had been dispatched ahead of the main invasion force. Their mission was to disable Trident's automated planetary defenses, paving the way for the imminent invasion. The alien's thoughts revealed that these teams had already completed their objectives and left the planet, indicating that the invasion was not just impending but had been meticulously planned and set in motion.
The alien, under the relentless assault of the interrogation tools and The Surgeon's Mindreaving abilities, also divulged the motivation behind the invasion. The Obsidians, as they are called, driven by a sacred duty, were seeking a sacred artifact that had been activated on Trident. The activation of this relic was considered a grave sacrilege, an unforgivable desecration that demanded retribution. The Obsidians' plan was clear: retrieve the artifact and punish humanity for their perceived heresy.
Captain Orion, who had been observing the interrogation, was struck by the gravity of this information. The alien's identity as a member of the Dra'Kor Vhel'Tan and the revelation of the Obsidians' sacred mission changed the entire scope of the conflict. 
Orion had remembered hearing the words “Dra’Kor Vhel’Tan” over random comms channels before he recalled Fireteam Valor.
 An elite force had already compromised Trident's defenses, and a coalition of alien races was on the brink of launching a full-scale invasion.
The interrogation session had taken a toll on the alien. The once formidable Dra'Kor Vhel'Tan operative now appeared broken and defeated, its body bearing the non-physical scars of The Surgeon's methods. The Surgeon, recognizing the extent of the information extracted, signaled for a pause. The creature was carefully unstrapped and taken away for medical attention, its value as an intelligence source exhausted for now.
Orion and The Surgeon, now alone in the chamber, processed the implications of what they had learned. "We must prepare immediately," Orion said with urgency. "The Obsidian invasion is not just a threat; it's already underway. We need to warn our forces and fortify our manual defenses, especially with our automated systems down. "
As they left the interrogation chamber, the weight of their newfound knowledge was palpable. An elite alien force had infiltrated Trident, and the main Obsidian invasion force was on its way, driven by a deep-seated vendetta against humanity. The race to defend the planet and uncover the secrets of the sacred artifacts was now more critical than ever.
In the war room, members of Fireteam Valor were gathered, the tension palpable as they awaited the debrief. They were a mix of wariness and anticipation, each processing the gravity of their situation in their own way.
Staff Sergeant Rourke leaned against the wall; his arms crossed. "Bet The Surgeon's having the time of his life in there," he said with a grim chuckle. "Wonder if our alien friend is singing yet."
Specialist Harlan snorted. "Singing? More like screaming his damn head off. If those walls could talk."
Lieutenant Argus, sitting at the head of the table, his gaze fixed on the door, replied, "Whatever it takes. We need that intel if we're going to stand a chance against these bastards."
Corporal Vega, her expression hard, added, "I just hope we get something useful out of this mess. We’re sitting ducks here until we know what we’re up against."
Royce shifted uncomfortably in his seat. "This whole thing is screwed up. You saw how much it took to take down those pricks back at Charr.”
Harlan laughed darkly. "Relax, this is our show now, kid. I’m sure we can figure out how to kill them a bit faster."
Throughout the conversation, Wraith remained silent, his demeanor calm and unreadable. He observed his teammates, his expression betraying none of the emotion that seemed to ripple through the others.
Rourke glanced over at Wraith. "What about you, Wraith? You've been quiet. Got any insights with that GRID brain of yours?"
Wraith's gaze shifted from one team member to another. "Emotions won't help us now," he finally said. "Focus on the task at hand. What matters is how we respond to what we learn. The Surgeon will get the information we need; our job is to be ready to take action."
The room fell silent for a moment, each member processing Wraith's words. The reality was stark – they were on the brink of an unprecedented conflict, and the fate of Trident hung in the balance.
As the team continued to wait, their conversation interspersed with moments of silence, the door to the war room finally opened. Captain Orion stepped in; his face etched with the weight of the situation.
"Alright, listen up," Orion began, his voice firm. "We've got our intel. It's time to understand exactly what we're dealing with."
The team straightened up, their attention now fully on Orion. 
Captain Orion stood before Fireteam Valor, his posture embodying the severity of the situation unfolding on Trident. The members of Fireteam Valor, clad in their Praetorian armor, directed their full attention towards him as he began to speak.
"Fireteam Valor, we're at Combat Alert Red. An invasion is already underway," Orion started, his voice steady but laden with urgency. "We have scarce information about the enemy's numbers or the exact timing of their attack. However, we must prepare for the main invasion force as soon as humanly possible."
He paused briefly, ensuring his next words were clearly understood. "Under OE-Exodus-TR Protocol, we're initiating a full-scale evacuation of all civilians to emergency spaceports at Crete City and New Eridanus. There are undisclosed underground bunkers in New Eridanus and reinforced underground tunnels under Crete City that lead to the beachheads. These will hopefully provide a safe haven secondary fall-back point, in the event lift-off is not an option."
Orion's gaze swept across the room. "This evacuation will be executed methodically, with priority given to the most vulnerable - those with medical needs, families with young children, essential personnel, followed by the general populace. It's crucial that this operation is carried out efficiently and without panic."
He continued, detailing the military involvement in the evacuation. "The 82nd OGDF Battalion's Alpha, Bravo, and Delta Companies are tasked with escorting the civilians. They will ensure safe passage and coordinate with local authorities to secure the population.”
The gravity of their defensive position became apparent as Orion shared more troubling news. "Our automated planetary defenses and long-range communication systems are down. We’re running nearly blind and defenseless. But we’re working to manually bring our orbital defense cannons back online. Given the circumstances, it's likely the enemy knows we're aware of their plans. We must be ready to defend this planet, at all costs.”
He shifted his focus to the immediate readiness of Fireteam Valor. "Honor Team is currently stationed in orbit on the Liberation, alongside Admiral Drexel. Should you require backup or additional support, STRIKE Team drop shuttles from the Liberation can reach your location within five minutes of the alert."
Orion then provided an update on the incoming support. "Luckily we were just within range of Admiral Greenwich and his minor fleet. He notified STARCOM Fleet of the situation and is en route with twenty ships. Their arrival time is uncertain, but we are hopeful they'll join us for the defense," he paused, looking up at the view screen, “Unfortunately, the rest of STARCOM Fleet is unable to join us in the defense for at least two weeks. We’re on our own.”
Concluding his briefing, Orion emphasized the need for thorough preparedness. "Ensure you're fully equipped and ready for prolonged engagement. Stock up on ammunition, water, and food supplies. Once you deploy, we cannot predict the duration of your mission. Stay vigilant and prepared for any scenario."
“I will be staying behind, Orion. I need to speak with The Surgeon and coordinate a few tasks, once the interrogation is concluded,” said Wraith.
“Do what you need to do, Wraith. Just keep me updated on whatever the hell you’re planning,” Captain Orion replied.
With Orion's briefing concluded, the members of Fireteam Valor began their preparations, each acutely aware of the critical role they were about to play in the defense of Trident and the safety of its civilians. The atmosphere in the war room was one of focused determination, as they braced for the challenges that lay ahead.
Captain Orion returned to the podium, his gaze sweeping across the room filled with Fireteam Valor members, each one ready for the daunting task ahead. His next words were a stark reminder of the grim reality they faced.
"Remember what I said before, men. Kill them all."
In response, the team stood up in unison, embodying the spirit of unity and readiness. Each member took their right hand, made a fist, and pounded it against their left breastplate, the sound resonating through the room. 
Together, they shouted, "STRIKE!" This was more than just a motto; it was a declaration of their resolve and readiness to use deadly force when necessary. It was a call to action, only used in moments where motivation was paramount, and the prospect of combat was imminent.
Orion, bolstered by their display of solidarity, added, "Praetorians fight to the last, men!"
Before he could continue, the moment was abruptly interrupted. The Surgeon stepped out and addressed the group with a sense of urgency tinged with malice. "My foresight is picking up some oddities within the mind of the Obsidian we've captured," he announced. "While he's being triaged for his 'injuries,' I believe it's time to bring him back to my chair. We need to extract as much information as possible before his life force gives out, and believe me, it will give out. Can’t have an alien running around Gladius, now, can we?"
The room's atmosphere shifted to one of intense focus. The Surgeon's ability to delve into the minds of others was a crucial asset, especially in situations where time was of the essence and the stakes were high. His suggestion to extract information before the Obsidian's potential death was a harsh but necessary strategy given the circumstances.
Orion nodded in hesitant agreement, understanding the importance of the Surgeon's foresight. "Do what you must, Surgeon. We need that intel. Every piece of information could be vital to our defense strategy and could save countless lives."
As the Surgeon moved to carry out his task, the members of Fireteam Valor prepared to support him in any way necessary. The situation was dire, and every second counted. The team knew the Surgeon's interrogation of the Obsidian could unveil critical details about the impending invasion, potentially giving them an edge in the looming conflict. Fireteam Valor was ready to do whatever it took to protect Trident and its inhabitants, especially Lieutenant Argus, with his family living in New Eridanus.
Captain Orion, with a decisive nod to Lieutenant Argus, issued his command, "Valor Six, you know what to do. Get it done."
Lieutenant Argus, his expression resolute and determined, replied sharply, "Yes, sir!"
Together, Orion and The Surgeon made their way down to the medical room. The corridors of the base were dimly lit, casting long shadows that seemed to stretch endlessly. The hum of the base's machinery and distant sounds of preparation for the impending conflict created an undercurrent of tension as they walked. The air was thick with the sense of impending action, each step taking them closer to a crucial and dark part of their mission.
Upon entering the medical room, they were greeted by the sight of the captured Obsidian. It lay there, still mentally exhausted, and physically worn out from the earlier encounter and the Surgeon’s previous methods of interrogation. The alien's breathing was labored, its body bearing the marks of conflict and strain.
The Surgeon approached the alien with a sinister glee in his eyes. Despite his previous role as a GRID operative, his demeanor and methods were unsettling, exuding an air of malevolence. He was a man who found a disturbing kind of pleasure in his work, especially when it involved extracting information by any means necessary.
As he activated his foresight ability, preparing to delve into the alien's mind, the Surgeon leaned in close to the creature. His voice, dripping with evil intent and filled with malicious laughter, broke the tense silence. "It's time to play again. I hope you like my… new methods." he taunted; his tone reminiscent of a classic villain reveling in the anticipation of the torment he was about to inflict.
His words, dark and chilling, showcased the lengths to which GRID and its operatives were willing to go in pursuit of vital intelligence. The Surgeon's methods, though morally ambiguous, were deemed essential in these dire circumstances. His capability to penetrate the minds of others, especially those of a formidable and enigmatic enemy like the Obsidians, was a resource that, albeit unsettling, was critical to the defense of Trident.
As the Surgeon's hands hovered over the alien, his fingers twitched slightly – a subtle sign of his foresight ability activating. The room seemed to grow colder; the air charged with a palpable sense of dread. The alien, weak and vulnerable, was no match for the Surgeon's invasive tactics. Even in its diminished state, it was evident that the Obsidian was a formidable being, its presence alone commanding a sense of awe and fear.
Captain Orion, standing a few steps back, watched the scene unfold with a complex mix of emotions. While he understood the necessity of the Surgeon's methods, it was clear that part of him struggled with the ethical implications. However, the urgency of the situation and the need for crucial intelligence about the impending invasion overrode any personal misgivings.
The Surgeon, now fully immersed in his task, continued his dark work. The Obsidian's mind, a labyrinth of alien thoughts and memories, was slowly being unraveled by the Surgeon's relentless probing. Each piece of extracted information brought them closer to understanding the enemy's plans and potentially gave Fireteam Valor and the GFC a fighting chance against the looming threat.
In this moment, the lines between good and evil, right and wrong, were blurred. The Surgeon, a figure of darkness, became a necessary evil in the fight for survival. His laughter echoed through the medical room, a haunting reminder of the sacrifices and compromises made in the name of protecting humanity.
As The Surgeon disengaged his foresight ability, the atmosphere in the medical room shifted palpably. The procedure he had just performed on the alien was profoundly disturbing. He had ensnared the Obsidian's mind in a relentless torture loop. In this mental construct, every ten seconds, the alien would experience the excruciating pain of its skin being flayed by a floating, rusty dagger – a horrifyingly vivid and torturous illusion.
The alien, physically motionless, was trapped in this cycle of pain, The Surgeon's ruthless efficiency and his enjoyment of such methods was unsettling. The ethical implications of such an act were profound, but in the Surgeon's mind, they were secondary to the need for information.
Turning back to Captain Orion, the Surgeon’s expression shifted slightly, trying to mask the overt malice in his actions with a veneer of professionalism. 
"I need my tools," he said, initially. Then, pausing, he seemed to reconsider his wording, as if trying to make it sound less sinister. "Let's bring my enjoyment," he corrected himself, and then quickly rephrased again, "I mean our possible intel, back to my chair." 
His attempt at correction did little to conceal the ominous nature of his intent. He chuckled darkly, the sound echoing ominously in the room.
Captain Orion, though visibly unsettled by the Surgeon's methods, acknowledged the necessity of their next move. The intel that could be extracted from the Obsidian was too valuable to ignore, especially given the desperate situation on Trident. Despite his discomfort, he understood that in war, especially one of this magnitude, certain moral lines were often blurred in the name of survival.
With a nod from Orion, the Surgeon prepared to transport the alien to his interrogation chamber, a place where many secrets had been unwillingly revealed. The Surgeon's chair, equipped with various instruments and devices, was his domain, a place where he excelled in extracting information from even the most resilient minds.
The alien, physically weakened and still mentally trapped in the Surgeon's torturous loop, was firmly strapped into the interrogation chair. The restraints were tight and unyielding, designed to hold even the most powerful of beings. Captain Orion, standing a few steps away, issued a stern directive to the Surgeon. His voice was firm, underlining the urgency of their situation.
"Get the information as quickly as you can. I understand your... enjoyment in this. Save that for another time. Time is of the essence. Get the intel fast. I don't care how you do it. Just do it."
The Surgeon, momentarily taken aback by Orion's bluntness, quickly refocused on the task at hand. Realizing he had been indulging in his sadistic tendencies at the expense of the mission, he nodded in agreement. 
"Absolutely. In that case, I'll start with the phantom limb inducer to break his will just a tad bit more!" he declared, a hint of eagerness still lingering in his voice despite his attempt to mask it.
The Phantom Limb Inducer, a sinister piece of technology, was one of the Surgeon's more fearsome tools. It was designed to create the sensation of limbs being twisted, contorted, or even severed, all without causing any physical harm. For the victim, the pain felt excruciatingly real, a psychological torment that could break even the strongest wills.
He leaned into the alien, diving into his mind and deactivated the torture loop, “Oh, I bet it feels nice to be free of this.” He softly laughed, just loud enough for the alien to hear. The way his foresight worked, he ensured the alien understood every word and intent of the coming actions.
The Surgeon’s chuckle came to a halt, “I don’t want you to be distracted! You’ll love what comes next!”
The alien looked at The Surgeon, so much fear in his eyes. The Surgeon knew that this was one of the first times in a long time this alien felt fear of any kind. He reveled in this knowledge.
As the Surgeon activated the device, the alien's reactions were immediate and visceral. Despite being physically unharmed, it writhed in the chair, its screams echoing through the chamber. The Obsidian's eyes widened in terror as it experienced the sensation of its limbs being bent at impossible angles, twisted and pulled in ways that defied its physiology.
The Surgeon watched intently; his eyes fixed on the alien as it struggled against the invisible agony. Every scream, every contortion of its body, was a sign that the device was working, pushing the alien's mental endurance to passed it’s limits. Yet, as he observed the alien's suffering, a part of the Surgeon relished in the control he exerted. It was a struggle for him to maintain a facade of professionalism, to suppress the twisted satisfaction he derived from his work.
Throughout this ordeal, no tangible intel was extracted; this phase was solely focused on weakening the alien's mental fortitude, preparing it for the actual extraction of information.
In this dark room, morality was a blurred line, overshadowed by the necessity of survival. The Surgeon, a master of mental manipulation, continued his work, his actions driven by the dire need for information that could turn the tide in the impending battle for Trident.
As the Surgeon prepared to employ the memory prodder on the weakened alien, he adjusted the device meticulously. 
The Surgeon activated the device, sending a series of calibrated pulses into the Obsidian's brain. These pulses were engineered to resonate with the neural frequencies of the alien, causing its memories to become more accessible. As the machine whirred to life, the Obsidian's body tensed, a sign that the prodder was beginning to breach its mental barriers.
On a nearby monitor, the Surgeon observed the alien’s brain activity intensify. He expertly manipulated the controls, delving deeper into the Obsidian's mind. The alien’s eyes flickered rapidly, a physical reaction to the invasion of its innermost thoughts.
Through the memory prodder's interface, the Surgeon began his interrogation. He focused on extracting specific details about the impending invasion. His questions were direct, probing for the size of the invasion force, the identity of the leaders, and the extent of their plans.
Gradually, amidst the alien's mental resistance, crucial details began to emerge. The Surgeon gleaned that the invasion force was formidable: seventy warships and ten transport ships, each capable of holding over one-hundred thousand Obsidian soldiers. Additionally, there is one major flagship and one minor flagship, likely serving as the command centers for the invasion. The scale of the force was massive, a clear indication of the Obsidians' commitment to their cause.
Furthermore, the Surgeon discovered the presence of over ten thousand fighter-like shuttles, designed for air-to-air combat. These shuttles were presumably intended to secure air superiority and support ground operations. The details were alarming – the Obsidian force was not only large but also well-equipped for a full-scale assault.
As the Surgeon continued to probe, the Obsidian's mental fortitude began to wane under the relentless assault of the memory prodder. However, the alien's resilience was noteworthy. Despite the Surgeon's invasive techniques, it managed to withhold certain critical information, including the exact timing of the invasion. This indicated either a remarkable mental strength or a deeply ingrained sense of loyalty to its cause.
The session reached its climax as the Surgeon, having extracted as much information as possible, prepared for the final act – the disposal of the alien using the Mind Spike ability. This brutal technique involved overloading the subject’s brain with a lethal burst of concentrated psychic energy, causing irreversible damage.
As the Surgeon activated this Mindreaving ability, the Obsidian's head quickly collapsed inward, it’s eyes popping out and exploding. The body slumped forward, lifeless, in the chair. The alien’s blood, purple in color, dripping down onto the floor aggressively.
Captain Orion, who had been observing the process with a mixture of fascination and horror, was visibly shaken by the violent end of the alien. The extraction of intelligence had been successful to an extent.
Orion, composing himself, acknowledged the Surgeon's efficiency despite the unsettling methods. "Report this to GRID command immediately," he instructed, his mind already shifting to the strategic implications of the information they had obtained. "We need to prepare for what's coming."
As they left the interrogation chamber, the gravity of the situation weighed heavily on them. They had learned of the vast scale of the threat they faced, but many unknowns remained. With the knowledge of the Obsidian force's size and capabilities, they had to quickly formulate a defense strategy against an enemy that was both enigmatic and formidable. The battle for Trident was approaching, and time was running out.
