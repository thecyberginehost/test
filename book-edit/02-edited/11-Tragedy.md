Chapter 11 – Tragedy
Date: December 16, 2888
Time: 1335 Galactic Standard Time (GTC)
Location: Lieutenant Argus's Home, Outskirts of New Eridanus

The room is still and quiet following the end of the call with her husband, Mark. Julia looks at her children, seeing in their young faces a mix of confusion and fear. 
Junior breaks the silence with a question that reflects the innocence and concern of a child his age, "Mom, is it true? Are the aliens here to fight us?"
Julia kneels down to their level, her expression serious yet gentle. "Yes, they are, Junior," she replies, her voice steady. "And we have to defend ourselves. I know you're both afraid but remember that your dad is fighting right now to get to us."
Her words seem to instill a sense of purpose in the children, though the fear doesn't completely leave their eyes. Julia then guides them to a secure cabinet, explaining the importance of knowing how to handle the weapons safely and correctly.
As she goes through the safety procedures with a Nightstalker pistol, Junior's attention shifts. "Can we get Dad's Battlepulse rifle?" he asks, his tone surprisingly determined. Julia is taken aback by the request, but before she can respond, Junior is already heading to their master bedroom.
He approaches the bookshelf and jumps up to reach the top shelf. He confidently pulls on a very old novel called "Starship Troopers." The shelf gives way slightly, revealing the entrance to a hidden, secure room. Junior's familiarity with this secret spot speaks of the many times he must have watched his father.
Inside the secure room, a vault stands, housing an array of weapons – trophies and tools from Argus's previous deployments. Among them are a long-range KA-2B Sniper Rifle, an M56 Battlepulse Rifle, another set of  P19-HE Nightstalker pistols, and a prototype magnetic railgun that Mark had been building in his spare time when he was home on leave. Junior's eyes are drawn to the Battlepulse Rifle. It's clear he's familiar with it; Julia recalls, with a mix of pride and apprehension, how Argus, against her wishes, had taken Junior to the range just last month to practice with it.
"Mom, I can use this," Junior says, a hint of eagerness in his voice, mixed with the seriousness of the situation. "Dad showed me how."
Julia watches her son, torn between the desire to keep him safe and the understanding that these are dangerous times, calling for dangerous measures. She nods slowly, realizing that their survival might depend on such skills. "Okay, Junior. But we have to be very careful. These are dangerous, and we only use them if we absolutely have to."
As she helps Junior with the Battlepulse Rifle, ensuring he remembers the safety protocols and handling techniques, Julia can't help but feel a swell of maternal pride. Her children, so young, are adapting to a reality far beyond their years, showing courage that belies their age. She silently vows to do everything in her power to keep them safe, to see them through this ordeal, and to reunite them with their father.
Junior, with a determination far beyond his eight years of age, grips the Battlepulse Rifle, its size and weight nearly overwhelming for his small frame. He struggles momentarily, the weapon nearly three-quarters his size, before a realization hits him. "The tripod!" he exclaims. He had almost forgotten. The last time he held the rifle, his father had attached a tripod to allow Junior to prop the weapon up in a much more stable position.
He quickly retrieves the tripod from the weapons rack and expertly affixes it to the rifle. The addition makes the weapon more manageable, and Junior's face lights up with a sense of accomplishment. Julia watches, a mix of surprise and pride washing over her. Her son, still so young, has absorbed more from his father than she had realized.
Kat, on the other hand, is more reluctant. At six years old, the very idea of handling weapons is intimidating. She stands back, her small body tense with apprehension. Julia gently encourages her, understanding her fear but also recognizing the necessity of familiarity with the weapons for their safety.
"It's okay, Kat. I'll protect you," Junior says, turning to his sister with a protective gaze. His words are simple but filled with the deep bond of sibling love.
Hearing this, Julia feels a warmth spread through her heart, a bittersweet mix of love and worry. She wishes more than anything that Mark, their father, were here. He would know exactly what to do, how to make them feel safe. But in his absence, it's up to her, and she draws strength from her children's bravery.
"Alright, let's make sure we're all as prepared as we can be," Julia says, her voice steady. "We're going to stay together, stay hidden, and wait for your dad. He's doing everything he can to come back to us."
Together, they review basic safety protocols once more, Julia ensuring that both Junior and Kat understand the gravity of the situation.
As Julia finalizes the preparations with the children, the eerie calm of their home is shattered by the thunderous sound of explosions. The noise is deafening, a cacophony of destruction that seems to come from all around them. Suddenly, the house begins to shake violently, as if caught in the grip of a powerful earthquake. The constant barrage of explosions sends shockwaves through their home, rattling the windows and causing the walls to tremble.
An urgent, loud emergency alarm then pierces through the chaos, its wail echoing throughout the city. This alarm, a harbinger of imminent danger, sends a chill down Julia’s spine.
Acting on instinct, she rushes outside the secure room to the nearest window, her heart pounding in her chest as her children follow suit. The sight that greets her is one of apocalyptic horror. New Eridanus is under a reign of fire, a devastating barrage from above. She sees houses down the street being obliterated by blue beams of light and explosive ordnance, each impact a violent eruption that tears the structures apart.
Realizing the imminent danger, Julia springs into action, herding Kat and Junior towards the secure room. Junior struggles with the Battlepulse Rifle, it’s weight significant for his young age, but he grips it tightly, determined to carry it himself. Kat's face is pale, her eyes wide with terror, barely comprehending the destruction unfolding around them.
They race to the secure room, each step heavy with the realization of the danger they are in. The secure room, fortified with three feet of concrete on all sides, offers their only sanctuary. Julia ushers her children inside, her movements quick and purposeful. The room's air vent, leading underground before emerging outside, ensures a constant supply of breathable air, while the sophisticated air filter system blocks any toxic substances, including radiation.
Once inside, Julia locks the secure room door, sealing them away from the nightmare outside. The sounds of their house being torn apart by the alien assault penetrate even the thick concrete walls of the secure room. The crashes and explosions are a terrifying symphony, painting a mental picture of the devastation happening just beyond their refuge.
In the secure room, fear grips them all. Kat screams, her small body trembling uncontrollably. Julia and Junior wrap their arms around her, trying to offer comfort amidst the chaos. "It's going to be okay," Julia whispers repeatedly, her voice a shaky, but determined anchor in the storm of fear and uncertainty.
Julia looks at Junior, seeing in him the reflection of his father's bravery and strength. Despite the fear in his eyes, he is resolute, a pillar of support for both his mother and sister. In this moment, Julia sees the best qualities of Mark in their son, a mixture of pride and sorrow filling her heart.
Huddled together, they wait, listening to the sounds of their world falling apart. All they can do is hope, cling to each other, and believe in the promise of safety amidst the terror of the invasion.
The sudden cessation of the barrage leaves an eerie silence in its wake. Julia, her heart pounding, activates the Holocom connected to their home’s security system. Of the fifteen cameras installed around the property, only three flicker to life on the screen. The others, she assumes have fallen victim to the devastating attack. The images from the functioning cameras reveal a scene of utter ruin. Her once peaceful street is now a nightmarish landscape of destruction, with debris and unrecognizable remains scattered amidst the wreckage of what used to be homes. Survivors, their movements frantic and disoriented, scurry amid the ruins, a vivid display of panic and despair.
After a few tense minutes, Julia makes a decision. She needs to see the extent of the damage for herself. Turning to Junior, she instructs firmly, "Stay here and protect Kat. I'll be right back." Despite the fear in her children's eyes, she knows she must assess the situation outside.
Stepping out of the secure room and into what remains of their home, the sight that greets her is overwhelming. The familiar comforts of their house are unrecognizable, torn apart by the alien assault. She moves cautiously towards the door, her every step heavy with apprehension.
Outside, the scale of the catastrophe is even more harrowing. The neighborhood she knew and loved was unrecognizable, transformed into a war zone. The air is thick with smoke and the acrid smell of burning homes. Julia's heart aches at the sight, a deep sense of loss enveloping her.
Suddenly, a massive shadow engulfs the area. Julia looks up, her eyes widening in horror. A colossal alien warship, its design menacing and unfamiliar, flies overhead. Its immense size casts a long, dark shadow, enveloping the neighborhood in an ominous gloom. The ship slows, then hovers in the sky, a silent but threatening presence.
Without warning, a beam of energy erupts from the ship, striking the ground a few kilometers away. The impact sends a shockwave rippling through the area, shaking the ground under Julia's feet. She stumbles, catching herself against the remnants of a wall.
Then she sees them – hundreds of objects descending from the ship, growing larger as they approach the planet's surface. They resemble pods, each one a harbinger of further invasion. Julia's mind races, "No. No, no, no.” She said quickly, “They’re coming to us."
Her thoughts turn desperately to Mark. "Mark, where the hell are you? We need you." She recalls the stories he shared, the information about his missions he was permitted to tell. She understands enough to recognize the vessels as transport ships, likely carrying the alien invaders.
Unknown to Julia, at that very moment, Mark was being transported to New Eridanus, racing against time to join the battle for their planet. But for Julia, standing amidst the ruins of her neighborhood, watching the alien forces descending from the sky, the situation seems hopeless. Fear, determination, and a deep longing for her husband's safe return mingle in her heart as she retreats back to the safety of the secure room to her waiting children.
Inside, she locks the door once more, her face a mask of resolve as she comforts Kat and Junior. But in her eyes, there's a glimmer of fear for what's to come, a fear for their future, and a silent plea for Mark's return.
The systematic booms that now echo outside are a chilling harbinger of the alien presence. Julia, her heart pounding with dread, quickly glances at the Holocom's camera feed. The screen reveals a nightmarish scene unfolding just outside their fortified sanctuary.
An alien transport crashes into the ground with terrifying force, creating a massive crater where her neighbor's yard once was. The door of the transport bursts open, and four alien soldiers emerge. Their appearance is menacing, their movements swift and lethal. They open fire indiscriminately at any survivors in sight, their guttural shouts filling the air with a sound that is both alien and terrifying.
Across the street, another transport strikes the ground, and similarly, four more aliens burst out. They move with deadly efficiency, shooting and shouting, their tactical movements coordinated and precise.
Kat, who had been watching the feed secretly, turns to her mother, her young face etched with fear. "Mommy, they're here now. Are we going to die?" Kat’s voice filled with fear as she struggles to get her words out.
Julia pulls Kat close, wrapping her arms around her daughter in a protective embrace. She gently kisses Kat’s forehead, trying to mask her own fear as she responds with as much calmness as she can muster. "Not a chance," she whispers, her voice steady but her heart racing with fear.
Kat continues to cling to Juilia, her small body trembling uncontrollably. Julia extends her left arm out towards Junior. He embraces his mother and Kat without hesitation. Julioa holds her children tightly, her mind racing for a way out, a plan to keep them safe.
The fear they feel is overwhelming – the fear of the unknown, of the alien soldiers outside, of what might happen next. Julia’s thoughts are a whirlwind of worry for Mark, for their safety, and for the future, “If there is a future,” she thought.
Yet, in the midst of this terror, her resolve to protect her children at all costs only strengthens. She whispers words of comfort to them, trying to believe in her own reassurances, as they wait, hoping for a miracle in the face of seemingly insurmountable odds.
The situation escalates to a terrifying climax when an alien transport lands directly in their yard. The impact is felt within the fortified walls of the secure room, a violent tremor that sends a shockwave of fear through them all. Julia watches the Holocom's feed, her heart sinking as she sees the alien soldiers emerge from the transport. Unlike the others, these aliens are not immediately firing their weapons; instead, they begin a methodical search, moving with a chilling purposefulness. It's clear they are hunting for survivors – for anyone left to kill.
The stark realization that hits Julia is paralyzing. They are trapped, with no escape. The secure room, once a haven of safety, now feels like a tomb waiting to be sealed. She watches the screen, her eyes wide with horror, as the aliens methodically scour the area.
"This is it. If they find us in here, this is it," she thinks to herself, a sense of despair washing over her. The very thought of her children being harmed is unbearable, a fear so profound it eclipses everything else.
Junior and Kat sense their mother's fear, and it amplifies their own. Junior places a grip on the Battlepulse Rifle and tightens it as hard as he can, while Kat buries her face in her mother's side, her small body shaking with fear and faint cries.
Julia holds her children close, her mind racing for any possible solution, any sliver of hope. But the grim reality of their situation is inescapable. The only thing she can do now is wait, pray, and hope that the aliens don't discover their hiding place.
In the silence of the secure room, punctuated only by the distant sounds of the alien search and Kat's quiet sobs, Julia closes her eyes and thinks of Mark. She wonders if he's safe, if he even knows what's happening to them. The helplessness of the moment is overwhelming, but she clings to the faint hope that somehow, they might still make it through this alive.
The distant, alien cacophony creeps closer, an unsettling symphony of guttural commands and the methodical plundering of their home. Amidst this encroaching terror, Junior's resolve shines like a beacon in the dark. With trembling hands far too small for the task, he laboriously sets up the Battlepulse rifle on the table. The weapon, designed for adult hands, challenges his every move, its bulk unwieldy and its mechanisms complex. Yet, determination lights his young eyes, a testament to his courage. With a steadiness belied by his age, Junior positions the rifle, pointing its formidable barrel towards the door, waiting to fire at anything that isn’t Human’.
"Don't worry, Mom and Kat," he declares, his voice a mixture of bravery and innocence, "I'll keep us safe."
Julia watches, her heart swelling with a tumultuous blend of pride and dread. Junior's determination mirrors his father's unyielding spirit, a trait that had always been Mark's beacon in the darkest times. Kat's embrace of her brother, a physical manifestation of their unbreakable bond, brings a surge of warmth to the cold fear enveloping Julia's heart.
"I love you mommy. I love you Junior," Kat whispers, a simple declaration that carries the weight of their world. Tears breach Julia's resolve as the alien noises intensify, a stark reminder of their precarious reality. Her family's courage in the face of such terror only deepens her fear for their safety, 
The tension increases as the alien's efforts to find Human survivors reaches a fever pitch, the noise abruptly halting, casting a shadow of ominous silence.
 Junior, with a wisdom beyond his years, mutters, "I think they found us," his small fingers expertly flipping the Battlepulse rifle's selector from safe to automatic, the sound of the soft click echoing in the room.
Julia, embodying the protector, grips her Nightstalker pistol with a resolve forged in desperation, aiming at the door, her heart racing in anticipation.
Suddenly, a sinister red energy, reminiscent of a sword or handheld weapon, slices through the door, but doesn't bring it down. 
The sight sends Kat scurrying for cover under the table, her brother's calm whisper, "Not yet," her only solace. 
Julia watches,  filled with fear and pride as she marvels at Junior's tactical patience, a skill imparted by Mark, now manifesting in their son's poised readiness.
The aliens, thwarted by the door's resilience, unleash a barrage of frustrated attempts before tearing it down with a device that emits a high-frequency sonic pulse, designed to disintegrate molecular bonds. 
The door crumbles with a high pitched noise, and Junior's voice cuts through the tension, "Now!"
Junior squeezes the trigger and unleashes a loud, unnerving scream as the rounds leave the barrel. His shots are precise, each one finding its mark, toppling the first alien in a spray of unearthly blood. The remaining assailants, caught off-guard, scramble for cover, one limping from a well-placed shot by Junior.
Amidst the chaos, Julia finds herself frozen, the pistol's weight meaningless in her hands as fear paralyzes her. Kat's scream pierces the air, a raw expression of terror. In this moment of turmoil, Julia's instincts as a mother override her warrior's resolve, turning to console her daughter, embodying both the protector and the nurturer in the face of an alien threat.
In the heat of moment, with the alien forces breaching the last bastion of their safety, Julia's instincts kick into overdrive. Julia watched in what felt like slow motion as glowing orb arched through the air, its ominous glow forecasting doom. She lunges at Junior, their bodies colliding with the ground in a protective embrace.  Her actions are swift, driven by a mother's desperation to shield her offspring from the unknown horrors of alien warfare. With a strength fueled by adrenaline, she flips the heavy table upright with ease, creating a makeshift barrier that envelopes all three in a cocoon of relative safety beside them. The orb emits a high-pitched shriek, before unleashing its fury with a deafening boom. 
Julia compared the sound to an explosion happening underwater. The blast doesn't roar but whistles, a sound alien in it’s essence as it devours the Battlepulse rifle in a voracious display of heat. The room swelters, the air crackles, and the table succumbs, splintering under the force.
The aftermath leaves them in disarray. Kat and Junior, dazed and bewildered, struggle to comprehend the chaos. The aliens, undeterred, advance, their weapons spitting deadly energy towards the fragile remnants of their cover. Julia immediately picks up her Nightstalker pistol and returns fire. Her shots, though desperate and inaccurate, force the attackers into a momentary retreat. Seizing this fleeting opportunity, she rallies her children, herding them towards the cache of weapons they had prepared for such an apocalyptic scenario.
Amidst the frenzy, a fortunate explosion rends a hole in the secure room's reinforced walls, offering a sliver of escape into the ravaged skeleton of their home. Junior, emboldened, scavenges an alien weapon from the fallen foe he had valiantly killed. The weapon, unfamiliar to operate, confounds him until desperation guides his fingers to the activation mechanism. He fires the weapon sporadically in the direction he heard the alien sounds. Junior could feel the heat from the energy projectiles blasting out of the weapon.
Emerging from the smoke behind him, one of the aliens latched onto the back of his shirt, grabbing what felt like a fistful of his clothing. Junior was hurled with terrifying force into the crumbling remains of their once-safe haven. The impact steals his consciousness, leaving him vulnerable and exposed.
Julia's heart shatters at the sight, her scream a raw, primal sound that tears through the chaos. "Junior! No! You bastard, I'll kill you!" Her challenge is met with a guttural mockery from the alien, a sound that chills the soul.
Fueled by rage and desperation, Julia reaches for the nearest weapon in their arsenal, the prototype magnetic railgun. She hefted the weapon, the weight surprisingly comfortable in her hands. With a cry that merges grief and fury, she unleashes its potential, the weapon's electromagnetic pulse silently hurling projectiles with lethal precision towards the nearest enemy.
In the aftermath of the magnetic railgun's silent verdict, an alien stumbles and falls, uncomprehending of its sudden end. 
Julia, her resolve as unwavering as the steel of her fallen weapon, faces the last enemy. She threw the railgun and pulled out her pistol, pointing it at the final assailant. The creature acted quickly, dodges her pistol rounds with an unnerving grace, it’s movements a blur of contemptuous agility. It descends upon her, disarming and overpowering her with effortless malice. He knocked the weapon from her hands, while simultaneously wrapping its own hand around her neck. The alien lifted her up off the ground, its hand still tightly wrapped around Julia’s neck. 
It taunted, "Pathetic Humans,” in a low, guttural tone.
Kat, ignited by a raw, untamed rage, launches herself at the monster, her small fists and kicks a flurry of futile defiance. The alien unconcerned with the benign assault. Her attack, relentless and brave, finds a moment of opportunity; a shard of glass, a remnant of their shattered haven, becomes her weapon. With a cry fueled by fear and anger, she drives the shard into the alien's knee, exploiting a small gap in its armor. The alien's response is swift and brutal, a scream of pain followed by an aggressive kick that sends Kat flying through the air. She let out a piercing scream, the back of her head hitting a large piece of concrete debris as her body sprawled to the ground. She fell silent.
Julia started to kick and scream as hard as she could, a fearful and helpless feeling washed over her, knowing she could not save Kat, but the alien just stood there, hand still wrapped around her throat, laughing coldly. Julia managed to strike a blow to the alien’s head, its grip on her throat slightly loosening. Without warning, she saw the alien ignite an energy dagger in its other hand and immediately plunge it into the the side of her abdomen. She was silenced as the weapon pierced her, burning its way through her insides.
From the haze of near-unconsciousness, Junior's gaze locks onto a scene of despair. The sight of his mother, impaled by the alien's energy dagger, and the lifeless body of his sister, ignites a primal scream from his lips, a plea woven with pain and disbelief.
Junior's scream, "Mommy! Kat! No!" pierces the chaos, a son's anguished refusal to accept the unimaginable.
With trembling hands, navigates the ground to find the nearest weapon. He stumbles upon the same weapon they used to penetrate their secure room. His hands finds purchase around the hilt as he stands up, his body trembling with fear and rage. Junior activates the alien weapon, as if it was only his thoughts that had activated the destructive energy blade. The heat it emits is a palpable wave of fury as he charges towards the alien, driving the weapon through the alien's defenses and into its chest.
The creature's scream is a grotesque symphony of shock and agony. In its final moments, it retaliates, a vicious swipe that sends him crashing into a nearby pillar. The structure gives way, burying him beneath its weight.
The alien, now grievously wounded, staggers forward, each step a testament to its faltering strength. It collapses, a mere few paces from where it stood, its blood pooling on the cold ground.
Amidst the ruins, Julia, her life force ebbing away, is a portrait of maternal agony. The world she fought so fiercely to protect, her children, her very essence, fades into the shadow of loss. Her son, her daughter, now silent. Tears, a river of sorrow, trace the contours of her face as she clings to the fleeting images of her beloved family. Her final thoughts as the darkness of death claimed her was a fervent prayer, beseeching Mark to return, to right the wrongs, to avenge the unfathomable sacrifices made.
