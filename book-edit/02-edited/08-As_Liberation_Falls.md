Chapter 8 – As Liberation Falls
Date: December 16, 2888
Time: 0725 Galactic Standard Time (GTC)
Location: Flagship – GFC Eden

Lieutenant Barkley, Shadowhawk Six, and Lieutenant Charles, Ironclad Six, gathered their teams for a swift but thorough briefing. The teams, each consisting of specialized roles, listened intently as their leaders outlined the mission.
"Alright, listen up," began Barkley, "Fireteam Shadowhawk and Fireteam Ironclad, we're tasked with boarding the Liberation, assessing the situation, evacuating survivors, and eliminating all Obsidian forces. Let's go over your individual objectives."
Lieutenant Barkley began assigning objectives to each fireteam, "Corporal Jensen and Corporal Diaz, you're responsible for breaching operations and providing explosive support. Your main objective is to clear paths and create diversions within the station."
"Sergeant O'Neill and Sergeant Kim, you'll provide heavy fire support. Keep those Shredders ready for any close quarters combat and suppression fire."
"Specialist Hawke and Specialist Martinez, you'll cover our entry and exit points. Once aboard, you'll transition to close-quarters combat, but initially, your role is overwatch."
"Private Lopez and Private Williams, you'll be floating between point and rear security."
“Lieutenant Charles and the rest of Fireteam Ironclad will work alongside and adapt. Ironclad Six, brief your men.”
Lieutenant Charles added, "Roger that. Remember, coordination and communication are key. Stick to your objectives and we all make it back alive."
"Any questions?" Barkley asked.
Sergeant O'Neill asked, "What's the protocol if we can't secure the station?"
"Scuttle it," Charles answered firmly. "We'll set charges in the control room and evacuate. The priority is to prevent any data or control systems from falling into enemy hands. Once the charges blow, the Liberation will be falling into the atmosphere soon after. It’ll burn up on its way down."
With the briefing concluded, the teams felt a renewed sense of purpose and clarity. Each member knew their role and the importance of their mission. As they prepared to embark, the weight of their task was evident, but so was their resolve to see it through. Fireteam Shadowhawk and Fireteam Ironclad were ready to face the challenges ahead, determined to make a difference in the battle for Trident.
The Praetorians of Fireteam Shadowhawk and Fireteam Ironclad were clad in their GRID-funded Praetorian armor. The armor was a complete vacuum-sealed, sleek charcoal-colored battle armor with red accents, a fully enclosed helmet with an integrated HUD visor, a reinforced chestplate for maximum protection, and servo-assisted limbs for enhanced strength and agility, offering a perfect blend of tactical functionality and imposing presence on the battlefield. 
They made their way through the corridors of the GFC Eden, ready for their mission. Each member was a picture of focus and determination. These were not ordinary Praetorians; they were the Admiral's personal guard, chosen from the best from each Praetorian STRIKE team in the entire GFC. Their reputation was well-earned, and their motto, "None Better," was a testament to their skill in battle.
Lieutenant Barkley, leading the group, had once commanded Fireteam Honor and now carried the same level of dedication and eagerness to this critical mission. He was determined to clear The Liberation of enemies and then provide additional support on the surface.
As the team strode down the corridors, The Eden's crew members stopped to watch them pass. There was a mix of awe and respect in their eyes. The sight of these elite warriors, each selected for their exceptional skills and bravery, was inspiring. The crew members understood the gravity of the mission these Praetorians were about to undertake, and their silent nods and salutes were filled with admiration and gratitude.
In the launch bays, OGDF forces were boarding corvettes for their own crucial mission. They paused to observe the Praetorians walking past. Among the OGDF, there was a palpable sense of reverence for these elite soldiers as well as rivalry. The Praetorians represented the pinnacle of military excellence; their presence boosted the morale of the OGDF troops.
The Praetorians, aware of the eyes upon them, maintained their stoic demeanor. Their armor, equipped with the latest technology and weaponry, gleamed under the lights of the Eden. Each step they took was purposeful, echoing their resolve and readiness for the challenges ahead.
Reaching their exit point, the team members activated their vacuum seal mechanisms, sealing them with a hiss. They checked their Graviton Arrestors, ensuring they were ready for the zero-gravity push towards The Liberation. With boot clamps at the ready for immediate activation upon landing, they were prepared for the unconventional entry into the station.
Lieutenant Barkley's voice cut through the tense silence of the airless chamber. "Engineer Salvatore, seal the doors behind us. We need zero gravity and a vacuum for the jump."
Salvatore nodded, tapping controls on a nearby panel. "Sealing doors now, Lieutenant. Draining atmosphere."
As the room depressurized, Barkley issued his next command. "Activate boot clamps. Let's get positioned on the wall."
The team members, each clad in their advanced Praetorian armor, engaged their boot clamps with a series of mechanical clicks, adhering them to the designated wall. The hum of the ship's systems was the only sound in the now-silent chamber.
"All set, Lieutenant," Staff Sergeant Flannery reported, his voice steady over the comm system, "Ready for the jump. I’ll take point on the way to the Liberation."
"Follow my lead, and we all make it. None Better!" Flannery added, echoing the team's motto.
Barkley glanced at his team, seeing their resolve reflected in their visors. "Fireteams Ironclad and Shadowhawk, the light is green," he announced.
With a mechanical groan, the doors shuttered open, revealing the vast expanse of space, dotted with stars and the distant, chaotic battlefield. The sudden vacuum tugged at them, but their clamps held fast.
"On my mark," Barkley said. "Three, two, one... Jump!"
The team released their clamps in unison, propelling themselves into the void. "Thrusters on, evasive maneuvers, now!" Barkley ordered as they soared toward the Liberation at approximately one hundred and fifty kilometers per hour, and accelerating at an exponential rate.
They navigated through the perilous space battlefield, dodging enemy and friendly fighters, weaving through maneuvers of larger warships, and avoiding stray projectiles.
"Keep tight, watch those fighters!" Lieutenant Charles, leading Fireteam Ironclad, called out.
As they approached the fleet’s carrier ship, the Winds of Winter, disaster struck. The ship was pummeled by alien energy beams and ordnance. A massive explosion erupted from within, its reactor hit.
Barkley's eyes widened. "Graviton arrestors to full, cut hard right!" he commanded.
The team members, reacting instantly, adjusted their trajectories. The shockwave from the exploding ship sent debris and bodies hurtling through space.
"Watch out! Incoming debris ten o’clock!" Flannery shouted, as the team expertly dodged the debris. A large chunk of metal spun towards them.
Private Lopez, struck by a large piece of debris from the carrier ship, was lost in an instant. His life transponders flatlined, the grim reality flashing across each team member's helmet display.
"Lopez!" Private Williams cried out in anguish. Lopez had been more than a teammate to Williams; he was a lifelong friend, their bond formed in childhood and solidified from their early days in boot camp, through the rigorous trials of OGDF school, and the into their elite status as Praetorians. Yet, in the heat of battle, there was no time for grief.
Staff Sergeant Flannery's voice crackled over the comm system, snapping the team back to focus. "Now you just forget about that one! We can't afford to lose focus now. Eyes on the mission!"
His words were a heavy reminder of their duty, the need to stay sharp despite the pain of loss. "Sir, ETA to The Liberation, twenty-three seconds," he reported to Lieutenant Barkley.
Barkley, his own heart heavy with the loss of Lopez, knew the mission had to go on. "Stay in formation. We're almost there. Prepare for landing maneuvers. Prep graviton arrestors for rough landing on my mark!"
The remaining Praetorians, carrying the weight of their comrade's sacrifice, refocused their efforts, adjusting their thrusters as they approached the besieged Liberation. The ship loomed ahead, a battleground unto itself, marred by the scars of the ongoing conflict.
With only seconds to The Liberation, the team braced themselves for the rough landing. They were about to enter The Liberation, ready to fight, to reclaim, and to honor the memory of their fallen comrade. The journey was fraught with peril, but they were Praetorians, the best of the best, None Better.
The Praetorians, hurtling towards the Liberation with their graviton arrestors at full thrust, prepared for a landing that required precision and timing under immense pressure. Staff Sergeant Flannery's voice rang out in the comm systems, "Five seconds!"
Sergeant Flannery continued to count down with unwavering focus, "Five, four, three, two, one, mark!" 
In perfect synchronization, the team reversed their graviton arrestors. The force was so intense it felt as if they were about to breach the hull of The Liberation itself. The sudden deceleration brought them to a hard but controlled stop on the station's exterior.
Immediately, their boot clamps activated, anchoring each Praetorian firmly to the surface of The Liberation. The thud of their landing echoed through the outer layer of metal, but their boot clamps took the brunt of the landing, ensuring stealth was maintained.
"Accountability check,” Barkley ordered, his voice calm but carrying an urgency.
One by one, the team members checked in. "Ironclad Six, all green," Lieutenant Charles reported.
"Shadowhawk Six, all present, sub one." Barkley responded, ensuring his team was accounted for. A lingering sorrow weighed in his voice for the loss of one of his men.
"Everyone, weapons check," Charles added. 
Almost in unison, they unclipped their weapons from the secure breastplate holsters, ensuring their arms were ready for immediate use. The weapons, which had been tightly secured during their high-speed jump to avoid any hindrance or accidents, were now drawn and checked.
The sound of magazines being slapped into rifles filled the comm channel, each click a testament to the team's readiness. The Praetorians were a unit of precision and efficiency, each action deliberate and purposeful.
"Alright, let's find that entry hatch," Barkley said, scanning the hull. "Eyes sharp. We don't know what we're walking into."
The team moved cautiously along the hull, their movements precise, a dance of deadly efficiency in the zero-gravity environment. They navigated the battered exterior of the Liberation, each step bringing them closer to their point of entry.
"There," Specialist Martinez pointed to a hatch obscured by debris and damage from the battle.
Lieutenant Barkley scanned the exterior of the station, his eyes narrowing as he spotted the emergency hatch. It was partially obscured, the metal around it warped and melted shut from a previous explosion. The intense heat from the blast had effectively sealed the hatch, rendering it impossible to open by conventional means.
"Looks like we're going to need a charge. The hatch is melted shut," Barkley observed, his voice calm but authoritative over the comm system.
"Copy that, Lieutenant," replied Staff Sergeant Flannery. "Diaz, prep the breach."
Corporal Diaz nodded and moved forward, carefully placing a controlled explosive charge on the hatch. The team watched in silence, aware that time was of the essence.
“Charge is set," Diaz reported.
Flannery, standing ready, glanced at Barkley for the green light. Receiving a nod, he issued the command, "Detonate on my count. Three, two, one... Breach!"
The charge detonated with a controlled blast; the sound muffled in the vacuum of space. The hatch gave way under the force, allowing the team access to the interior of the station.
Barkley led the way, rifle at the ready. "Move in, Shadowhawk. Watch your sectors. We need to secure this station and evacuate any survivors. Ironclad secure our entry, then move in directly after.”
Fireteam Shadowhawk entered the emergency hatch of the Liberation with their weapons drawn, immediately establishing front, side and rear security. The hatch led them into a narrow maintenance corridor, dimly lit by emergency lighting that cast long shadows along the metal walls. The area was cramped, with various pipes and conduits running along the ceiling and walls, adding to the claustrophobic feel.
The team moved forward cautiously, still in a zero-gravity environment, their steps muffled in the eerie silence of the corridor. They kept their rifles at the ready, eyes scanning for any sign of the enemy. The tension was palpable, each team member acutely aware of the potential dangers lurking in the confined space.
Barkley noticed the corridor ending at a sealed door. Aware of the risks of opening it due to the potential vacuum of space on the other side, he held his team back. "Hold position. We can't risk opening this until we're sure it's safe," he instructed.
“Area secure, Shadowhawk Six," reported Sergeant O'Neill, his voice steady over the comm system.
Barkley nodded, his visor's HUD displaying the status of his team. "Ironclad Six, this is Shadowhawk Six. The area is secure. Proceed with entry. We’ve stopped at a sealed door and are awaiting your confirmation that the breach is sealed before we rendezvous."
Lieutenant Charles acknowledged, "Copy that, Shadowhawk Six. Fireteam Ironclad, move in."
Fireteam Ironclad entered the hatch, following the same procedure as Fireteam Shadowhawk. Sergeant Kim deployed a portable emergency sealing unit, a compact device designed for quick deployment to replace damaged hatches or doors. 
Sergeant Kim activated the unit, which expanded rapidly, forming a tight seal around the door frame. "Seal is secure, Ironclad Six," he reported.
"Good work. Let's rendezvous with Fireteam Shadowhawk and proceed," Charles instructed.
With the corridor now secured and the risk of exposure to space eliminated, the two teams regrouped. 
Fireteam Shadowhawk and Fireteam Ironclad cautiously entered the storage room, weapons raised, and senses heightened. The room, untouched by the direct impacts of battle, presented a stark contrast to the chaos raging outside the station. It appeared to have been unused for some time, with a layer of dust covering the surfaces.
The space was filled with storage boxes, some toppled over, their contents – papers, tools, and various equipment – strewn across the floor. Shelves along the walls, a few of them knocked askew, hinted at the force of the concussions from the space battle outside. A slight vibration ran under their feet, and the distant, muffled sounds of explosions seeped through the station's hull.
Lieutenant Barkley signaled his team to spread out and clear the room. "Check every corner. We don't need any surprises."
The team moved with practiced efficiency; their footsteps almost silent on the metal floor. They methodically checked behind boxes and shelving units, ensuring no enemy was hiding or any threat was present. The room, while cluttered, provided clear lines of sight, making their task somewhat easier.
Lieutenant Charles observed the room's strategic potential. "This could serve as a good fallback point," he commented, his voice low but clear in the team's comm system.
Barkley nodded in agreement. "Agreed. It's defensible and out of the way of the main corridors. Marking this on your HUDs."
Once satisfied that the room was secure, Barkley ordered, "Let's keep moving. But remember this location. If things go south, we regroup here."
The teams regrouped at the entrance, ready to continue their mission deeper into the station. 
With a final check of their gear, Barkley signaled for the team to move out. "Stay sharp and keep a secure formation. We have a lot of ground to cover."
The Praetorians exited the storage room, leaving the silent space behind as they advanced into the uncertain corridors of The Liberation.
As both teams exited the storage room, they transitioned into a tactical formation, each member efficiently clearing the hallway while simultaneously providing security. Their movements were precise and coordinated, honed by rigorous training and combat experience. Every team member was acutely aware of their assigned sector, their eyes scanning for any signs of danger.
The hallway they entered was a stark contrast to the untouched storage room they had just left. It was a scene of devastation and chaos. Bodies of scientists, OGDF forces, and station personnel were strewn across the floor, a grim testament to the brutal efficiency of the enemy's attack. The Praetorians moved among the fallen with respect, their gazes somber but focused.
As they inspected the bodies, it became evident that they had been struck by some type of advanced energy weapon. Limbs were missing from several bodies, the wounds cauterized, suggesting the weapon that inflicted them simultaneously sealed the wounds. This was not a weapon they had encountered before, indicating a level of technological sophistication that was deeply concerning.
The team continued to move through the corridor, methodically checking each room they passed. The hallway was littered with debris from the station, along with personal belongings and equipment, creating an eerie, abandoned atmosphere.
As they advanced, the distant sounds of gunfire and the distinctive crackle of what seemed to be the aliens' energy weapons echoed through the station. The cacophony of battle was drawing nearer, indicating that they were approaching the heart of the conflict.
Lieutenant Barkley signaled for the team to pause. "Listen up. We're getting close to the action. Stay alert, and if you see something that’s not human, blast it to hell," he instructed.
The team members nodded in acknowledgment; their expressions hidden behind their visors but their resolve clear. Each member checked their weapon one last time, ensuring they were ready for the imminent confrontation.
With a silent gesture from Barkley, the teams resumed their advance, moving towards the sounds of battle. The corridor ahead promised a direct encounter with the enemy, and the Praetorians were prepared to face whatever lay in wait, ready to reclaim The Liberation and turn the tide of the conflict.
Suddenly, three alien soldiers appeared from around a corner. Their armor was a striking charcoal color, shimmering with subtle purple hues that seemed to absorb the dim light around them. The leader, standing slightly ahead of the others, wielded a weapon unlike any the Praetorians had seen: a sword made of pure red energy, pulsating with a menacing glow. The other two aliens carried more indistinct weapons, which at first glance seemed less threatening but quickly proved deadly as they opened fire.
The Praetorians reacted instantly, returning fire while seeking cover. The exchange was intense, with energy blasts illuminating the corridor in brief, violent flashes. Corporal Jensen took a direct hit to the weakest point in his torso armor, the left side of his abdomen. The alien weapon tore through his armor, breaking off a chunk and revealing the severely burnt skin underneath.
"Ah, it burns!" Jensen shouted; pain evident in his voice. His suit's advanced medical systems sprang into action, sealing the wound and administering a cooling sensation to the burned area, providing some relief amidst the chaos.
Barkley, realizing the intensity of the enemy's firepower, called out commands to his team. "Focus fire on the leader! Watch those energy weapons!"
The team concentrated their efforts on the alien wielding the energy sword, who moved with a deadly grace, deflecting incoming fire with swift, precise movements. The battle was a brutal dance of energy and light, with each side pushing against the other's defenses.
In the midst of the battle, the Praetorians' training shone through. They moved fluidly as a unit, covering each other and exploiting any opening in the aliens' formation. The sound of gunfire and energy blasts echoed through the corridor.
Finally, with a well-placed shot in the alien’s left breastplate by Sergeant Flannery, the alien leader faltered, the energy sword dissipating as its wielder crumpled to the ground. Seizing the moment, the Praetorians quickly took down the remaining two aliens, ending the skirmish as abruptly as it had begun.
As the team caught their breath, Barkley checked on Jensen. "You good to keep moving?"
Jensen, wincing from the pain, nodded. "Yeah, I can manage. It’s just a scratch."
The skirmish had seemingly ended, and Lieutenant Barkley, Shadowhawk Six, ordered a kill-check to confirm the aliens were truly neutralized. The Praetorians cautiously approached the downed enemies, ready to execute the standard double-tap procedure.
One of the members from Fireteam Ironclad, Private Miller, approached the alien leader. As he raised his weapon to ensure the kill, the alien, exhibiting a startling burst of life, sprang up in a swift, deadly motion. He grabbed Private Miller's weapon, flinging it across the corridor, and in one fluid movement, kicked Private Miller to the ground.
Before anyone could react, the alien thrust his energy sword directly into Private Miller's left chestplate. The lethal energy blade cut clean through his armor and through his heart. Miller's life signs immediately disappeared from the team's helmet displays, his final moments shockingly brief.
Reacting swiftly to this sudden threat, the rest of the team unleashed a barrage of gunfire at the alien leader. His blue blood splattered as he was thrown backward by the force of the bullets, finally collapsing to the ground, lifeless.
The Praetorians stood in a stunned silence, coming to terms with the loss of Private Miller. Lieutenant Barkley, maintaining composure despite the shock, picked up the red energy sword from the fallen enemy and addressed the team, "Let’s move, now. Ironclad, you've got point.”
Lieutenant Charles acknowledged the order. He took the lead as the group continued deeper into the facility, their determination to complete the mission now mixed with a solemn resolve to honor their fallen comrade.
As the Praetorians from Shadowhawk and Fireteam Ironclad advanced towards the control room, their progress was met with increasing resistance. The sound of the recent firefight had alerted other Obsidians, who now converged on their position, determined to halt their advance.
The Praetorians, quickly adapting to the aliens' tactics, engaged in a fierce firefight. Lieutenant Charles led his team with precision, calling out orders and adjusting their formation to counter the enemy's movements. "Ironclad, flank right! Shadowhawk, suppression fire!" he commanded, orchestrating their response to the alien onslaught.
The teams worked seamlessly together, their disciplined fire ensuring minimal injuries while effectively taking down the Obsidian foes. With each encounter, they grew more adept at anticipating and countering the aliens' maneuvers, slowly but steadily making their way closer to the control room.
Upon reaching the entrance to the control room, they found it heavily sealed, impervious to their initial attempts to breach it. Barkley scanned the area and noticed movement in the room adjacent to the control room. Through the reinforced viewing port, they saw a group of aliens engaged in a fierce firefight with a group of humans led by Commander Drexel. The humans had erected makeshift barriers, providing them with a semblance of a fighting chance against the alien onslaught.
Charles acted swiftly. "We need to breach that door. Diaz, set the charges!" he ordered. 
Corporal Diaz quickly placed explosive charges on the door, but the detonation barely made a dent in the advanced sealant that fortified it. Frustration and urgency mounted among the Praetorians.
Lieutenant Barkley decided to try a different approach. "Stand back. I'm going to try something." The team stepped aside as Barkley pulled out the procured energy sword and activated it. Its red glow casting an ominous light in the dim corridor.
He pressed the sword against the door, slicing through the advanced material with little effort. The process was slow, and tension filled the air as they hoped to make it in time to assist Commander Drexel and his group.
Inside, Commander Drexel and the remains of his crew were fighting valiantly. Their makeshift barricade held, for a while. The humans unleashed everything they had: grenades, plasma charges, and a hail of bullets. However, despite their ferocity, the elite aliens seemed to advance with an almost uncaring determination.
It was clear that the humans were outmatched, and one by one, they fell. Soon, only Commander Drexel remained, his back against the central server console, pistol drawn. As the dust settled, the aliens moved closer, and one of them, bearing more intricate markings on its armor, stepped forward.
Without a word, the lead alien drove its red energy sword into Drexel's leg. The commander roared in pain, "You son of a bitch!" His face was contorted in agony, and yet, in his eyes, there was a fire, a defiance that had not yet been extinguished. The alien merely chuckled, the sound echoing eerily in the chamber.
"Human," it began in English, its voice deep and filled with a cruel amusement, "your efforts are useless. Your cries will be drowned by the billions of others when we eradicate you." As the alien leaned in closer, Drexel mustered all his strength and spat at its face, causing a sizzling sound as it made contact. The alien recoiled slightly; its gaze more intense.
But Drexel's spirit was unbroken. With his free hand, he reached for a hidden pistol strapped to his ankle. Just as his fingers brushed the cool metal, the alien's reflexes proved faster. A secondary energy blade, shorter but just as deadly, emerged, and with a swift motion, severed Drexel's hand. Drexel cried out again, but his defiance was unwavering.
The alien leader, intolerably impatient, withdrew the blade from Drexel's leg. In a swift, cruel motion, it thrust the sword into the commander's chest and simultaneously plunged the dagger up into Drexel's chin. The dim overhead lights of the server room reflected off the red glow of the energy weapons, casting an eerie illumination. As the blade ran through Drexel's chest and the dagger impaled his chin, an intense energy burned through him. The heat from the blades sizzled against his flesh, producing a hissing sound that mixed with his muffled screams of pain.
Lifted off the ground by the sheer force and power of the alien's strike, Drexel's feet dangled, twitching sporadically. His face was contorted with agony. The eyes that once shone with spirit and determination now grew glassy and distant, but they retained a trace of their unyielding fire.
Smoke wafted up from the entry points of the weapons, where flesh met heated energy. The scent of burnt skin filled the room. Even the Praetorians, waiting helplessly outside the room, could smell the stench. The weight of Drexel's body, combined with the searing heat of the energy blades, caused his form to slowly slide down. The energy blades, while lethal, were not designed to hold weight, and as they seared through muscle and bone, they slowly lost their grip.
With a final gruesome thud, Drexel's lifeless body dropped from the blades, collapsing onto the cold metallic floor with a thud. The once-commanding figure lay there, still and defeated, a stark contrast to the towering and triumphant stance of the lead alien, who withdrew his weapons with a satisfied sneer. The room, filled with the scent of battle and loss, stood silent except for the alien's triumphant laughter echoing in the aftermath of their conquest.
The alien menace now looked to the entry to the room, watching as their own energy sword was cutting through the sealed door.

As Barkley finally cut through, the door fell away, revealing the scene inside. It was a moment too late. They witnessed Commander Drexel’s lifeless body convulse as its nerve endings erratically contorted.
The Praetorians, stunned by the sight, quickly sprang into action. A furious firefight ensued as they engaged the aliens in an attempt to avenge the fallen Commander and secure the room. Barkley shouted orders amidst the chaos, "Focus fire! Take them down!"
The room echoed with the sound of gunfire and energy blasts, the Praetorians fighting with a renewed fervor fueled by the loss of Commander Drexel. Despite their efforts, the realization sank in that they were now facing an even more challenging mission.
The intense battle between the Praetorians and the Obsidian forces intensified as the aliens' energy weapons unleashed a barrage of deadly fire. The Praetorians, despite their advanced training and weaponry, found themselves overwhelmed, forced to retreat to a previously established makeshift barrier constructed by the crew of The Liberation.
In the midst of this chaos, Private Williams was struck behind his left knee with an energy blast, burning its way through to his skin. He tried desperately to regain his footing. As he staggered to one knee, trying to rise, an Obsidian warrior charged at him with a glowing energy sword in hand. Williams, in a desperate bid to defend himself, raised his rifle and fired relentlessly at the approaching enemy.
But to his dismay, the rounds seemed to have little effect. The alien's armor was incredibly resilient, causing several of the bullets to ricochet off harmlessly. Those bullets that did not bounce away were expertly deflected by the alien's rapid, almost dance-like movements with the energy sword. Each swing of the sword created a blur of light, deflecting the bullets with a precision that was both terrifying and awe-inspiring.
As Williams's magazine emptied, the alien, seizing the opportunity, hurled the energy sword with lethal accuracy. Williams, caught in the act of reloading, had no time to react. The energy sword pierced through his helmet, penetrating his skull with brutal force. The impact of the throw knocked Williams off his feet, sending him crashing to the ground with a heavy, resonant thud.
The Praetorians, engaged in their own desperate battles, could only witness the tragic demise of their comrade from the corners of their eyes. They were powerless to help, pinned down by the relentless enemy fire.
The Obsidian warrior, after felling Williams, advanced toward his lifeless body with a cold, methodical pace. Stepping onto Williams's chestplate, the alien reached down and grasped the handle of the energy sword still embedded in Williams's helmet. With a violent jerk, the alien pulled the sword free, the action accompanied by a gruesome sound as the blade exited the skull. Blood and fragments of helmet and bone followed the sword as it was drawn out, the hot energy from the blade also sealing the wound with a hissing sound. 
Williams's body lay motionless, a stark reminder of the lethal danger posed by the Obsidian invaders. The Praetorians, filled with a mix of grief and rage, steeled themselves for the ongoing battle, driven by the memory of their fallen brother and the urgent need to fulfill their mission.
Recognizing the dire situation and their inability to fend off the overwhelming Obsidian forces indefinitely, Lieutenant Charles quickly formulated a plan. Amidst the chaos of the firefight, he communicated his idea to Lieutenant Barkley.
"Barkley, we can't hold them off forever. But we can slow them down," Charles shouted over the din of battle. "We blow the ceiling. The Liberation’s schematics show there are depleted heavy fuel cells and old machinery parts stored in the upper level right above us. If we bring that down, it'll block this corridor."
Barkley, understanding the necessity of such a desperate measure, nodded in agreement. "Do it! Ironclad, set the charges. Shadowhawk, cover them!"
The team swiftly set to work. Despite the relentless enemy fire, Fireteam Ironclad managed to place explosive charges at strategic points along the corridor's ceiling. The upper level, known to store old, decommissioned machinery and several large depleted fuel cells, would provide enough debris to effectively block the passage.
As Fireteam Ironclad hurriedly set the charges, Fireteam Shadowhawk laid down a barrage of suppressive fire, keeping the advancing Obsidians at bay. The air was thick with the sounds of gunfire and the ominous hum of alien energy weapons.
With the charges in place, Charles gave the signal. "Fire in the hole!" he yelled, and the team braced for impact.
The explosion was deafening. The ceiling buckled and collapsed, sending a cascade of heavy machinery, twisted metal, and fuel cells crashing down. Dust and debris filled the corridor, creating a dense cloud that obscured vision and choked the air.
As the dust settled, the team saw that their plan had worked. The corridor was now blocked by a massive pile of rubble, machinery and depleted fuel cells, effectively cutting off the enemy's advance. The Obsidians on the other side were either crushed under the debris or forced to find another way around.
"Move! Let's get out of here while we can!" Barkley ordered, leading his team away from the blockade. The Praetorians quickly retreated, using their intimate knowledge of the facility's layout to navigate through alternative routes.
Despite their successful escape, the mood among the team was somber. The loss of their comrades weighed heavily on their minds, but they knew their sacrifice had bought valuable time. Time that could mean the difference in the larger battle for Trident. The Praetorians moved with renewed purpose, ready to regroup and continue their fight against the Obsidians.
As the Praetorians retreated through the maze of corridors on The Liberation, Lieutenant Barkley realized the direness of their situation. They couldn’t reach the control room, let alone set the charges needed for its destruction. He made a decision and reached for his radio.
"GFC Eden, this is Shadowhawk Six. We need immediate air support. Requesting a Dread Wing Fighter to launch a nuke at The Liberation. We're unable to secure the control room. I say again, we are unable to secure the control room, over."
There was a tense pause before Admiral Greenwich's voice crackled through. "Acknowledged, Shadowhawk Six. I'm authorizing the strike. Evacuate your teams immediately. You have five minutes to get the hell out of there. Greenwich out."
Barkley relayed the information to the teams. "We're pulling back to the escape pods. Move out!"
As they neared the pods, a grim reality set in. There wasn’t enough space for everyone. Corporal Jensen, who had been wounded earlier, coughed through the pain. "I’ll stay," he grunted. "I'm not making it through this anyway. Just check my life indicators."
Sergeant Flannery looked at Martinez, then at Barkley. "We can’t leave him here."
Barkley's voice was firm, but his eyes betrayed his emotion. "We have to. Jensen is making the right call."
They quickly loaded into the escape pods. As they did, Barkley made eye contact with each remaining team member. "Remember what we’re fighting for. None Better, right?"
“None Better!” they echoed, a chorus of determination amid despair.
As the door to the escape pod was about to be sealed, they noticed a squad of Obsidians emerging from an adjacent room, their movements swift and purposeful.
Corporal Jensen waved at his team as if to shoo them away, “I'll hold them off,” he declared, gripping his weapon tightly. "Get out of here. I've got this."
His determination was palpable, even as the others protested. 
Sergeant Kim ran up to Jensen, “If you’re going to stay here, take this,” he said as he handed Jensen his Shredder.
“Thanks man, I’ll make it count.” Jensen said as he positioned himself strategically at a choke point between a makeshift barrier and the escape pod, ready to confront the oncoming enemy.
As his team members reluctantly continued to the pods, Jensen opened fire as he saw the first of the Obsidians waves. His weapon roared in the confined space, bullets tearing through the corridor. The first wave of Obsidians was caught off-guard, several falling before they could even react under the Shredder’s heavy fire. Jensen kept firing, his shots precise and deadly. But the enemy was relentless.
An Obsidian managed to close the distance, its energy sword slashing through the air. Jensen dodged the first swing but was hit by the second, the blade cutting through his armor. He grunted in pain but continued fighting, taking down the alien with a well-placed shot to its neck, a weak point of the alien's armor.
Despite his valiant effort, the numbers were against him. Another Obsidian charged, and this time, Jensen couldn’t evade the fatal blow. The energy sword pierced the center of his chestplate and then the center of his chest, delivering a mortal wound. As he fell, his gaze turned to the viewport, watching his team’s escape pods launch.
As the lifeforce dissipated from Corporal Jensen, his final words were mumbled, but clear as day, “None better,” and with that, he perished.
As the escape pods departed from the Liberation, The Praetorian teams, encapsulated in their small vessels, were a mix of tense readiness and reflective silence. The pods, sleek and aerodynamic, propelled away from the besieged station at high speed, distancing themselves from the imminent nuclear detonation.
Lieutenant Barkley, his gaze fixed on the viewport, watched the unfolding scene. He saw the silhouette of a sleek, small ship emerging from the Liberation, unmistakably Obsidian in design. It darted away with alarming speed, heading directly towards Trident. As he and his team watched, the ship suddenly vanished, its cloaking technology activated, rendering it invisible against the backdrop of space. The display of advanced stealth capabilities sent a shiver down Barkley's spine.
Before he could process this further, the viewport was suddenly filled with a blinding light. The nuclear strike on The Liberation had been executed. The explosion was a silent, yet awe-inspiring spectacle from their distance – a brilliant, expanding sphere of destructive energy that engulfed the station. The once formidable Liberation disintegrated under the nuclear fireball's immense power.
The shockwave that followed was like a cosmic sigh, rippling through space. The escape pods shook violently under its force, their stabilization systems working overtime to maintain course. The Praetorians inside braced themselves against the tremors, their helmet visors illuminated by the explosion's fading glow.
The sight of The Liberation's destruction was both a grim victory and a painful reminder of their sacrifice. They had succeeded in their mission to prevent critical data from falling into enemy hands, but at the cost of many lives.
As the last remnants of the explosion dissipated into the darkness of space, Barkley turned away from the viewport, his mind already shifting to the challenges ahead. "We need to warn the ground forces about that Obsidian ship," he said, his voice a mix of determination and urgency. "And prepare for what's coming. I think this fight’s just getting started."
The team members nodded in agreement; their expressions hardened. The battle for Trident was escalating, and they were now headed directly into its epicenter. The journey to the surface would be fraught with peril, but they were ready.
