# THE REFORGED UNIVERSE — Master Lore Canon
### Author: D.K. Shadow
*Universe-level reference distilled from the author's Master Lore Reference (the "Reforged Universe Lore Bible") plus newer verbal canon from editing sessions. This sits ABOVE the per-book Story Bible. Where this conflicts with the latest manuscript/Story Bible, the manuscript wins and this is corrected.*

> **Scope:** A single connected mythology across **three eras / three genres**, spanning ~65 million years, on **one metaphysics**.
> - **Fantasy era** → high fantasy register
> - **Modern era** → cosmic science fiction
> - **Far-future era (2888, *ForgePulse*)** → cyberpunk military
>
> **The one rule under everything:** *Attachment is resistance. Transcendence done wrong is corruption.* Every civilization that reaches for ascension is gambling whether it can gain power without losing what made it worth saving. The Celestian Pact lost that bet and became the Void.

## 1. The Unifying Trinity
- **Unifying force:** **Conduit energy** — "the Force on steroids." Present in everything, dormant until awakened; only proper genetic lineages can channel it. Strongest wielders reshape reality; far-future inheritors get only fragments. ⭐ **Its nature/agency:** normally conduit energy **just lets things be** — but when something **interrupts the natural order** (corruption / the Void), it becomes **relentlessly restorative**: it will **"take out the trash" by any means, over any timescale**, until the order is set right. *This is the quiet agency behind every "coincidence" in the saga* (Argus's engineered death, the Grand Convergence) — not fate or gods, but **the universe's own immune response to the Void.** ⭐ **Possibly the deepest layer of all:** the leaning theory is that **conduit energy is the soul-creator of the Infinites themselves** (§2) — older than the gods, the source of their sentience. *(Origin unconfirmed, even by the author.)*
- **Unifying threat:** **The Void** — a hive-mind corruption-parasite.
- **Unifying theme:** attachment = resistance; transcendence-done-wrong = corruption.

**Author shorthand (tone targets):** Scourge = "a redeemed Arthas." Xylaris = "Doomguy on crack, with the soul of a framed custodian god." The Void = Halo's Flood × WoW Void. The Ancient Ones = WoW Old Gods × Lovecraft's Cthulhu.

## 2. Cosmological Foundation
- **The Infinites — origin (mostly mystery):** they **came to be suddenly** — sentient, but **not physically existent** in this realm — and simply tried to do whatever they were "programmed" to do. **No one knows their exact origin — not even the author.** *Leaning theory:* **conduit energy is their soul-creator**, the thing that gave them sentience — anything the Infinites would ever do, **the conduit had already "thought" of.** *(So conduit energy is the **deepest layer of all**, older than the gods.)*
- ⭐ **The Big Bang was an ACCIDENT:** once the Infinites figured out how to **become physical manifestations**, they **tried to create equals** — and the attempt **detonated the Big Bang**, which **nearly destroyed them** yet **created the entire universe and all its galaxies.** Chastened, they **vowed never to create equals again** (doing so would fundamentally destroy them) and **passed that rule down to every lesser being they made.** Then they built a **hierarchy to create and maintain the universe.**
- ⭐ **Structure (the multiverse — exists but is NOT explored in-story):** every galaxy has its own **Creator + Maintainer pair** (replica *roles*; each galaxy fundamentally different); the Infinites rule over all. *(The author will not explore other galaxies — ours is the only one that matters; the rest are "boring by comparison.")* ⭐ **Elevation:** *very rarely*, *very carefully*, the Infinites **raise a proven-worthy being into their own hierarchy** (see Taryn, §7).
- **Starwalker (Creator)** and **Xylaris (Maintainer)** — **our galaxy's** pair, two brothers made to steward this one galaxy. Xylaris was built a **custodian, not a warrior**; he became a warrior by necessity. (Early-lore variant spelling "Xylorus" → **Xylaris** is canon.) ⭐ **Why our galaxy is *the* galaxy:** it is the **only one in all the Infinites' creation where a Starwalker created beings *equal* to himself** (the **Ancient Ones**) — the single violation of the Infinites' core rule, and the root of every catastrophe that follows (the Void). Every other galaxy's Creator obeyed.
- **The Ancient Ones** — out of loneliness, Starwalker broke the rule and made **five equals**. They came out **evil by design**, not choice. Incomprehensible cosmic entities; no fear, no bravado; judge purely by power. Imprisoned, but never stopped scheming; the prison was never perfect.
- **The Devourer** — one of the five; the **architect of corruption**. Engineered the Void. Perspective is cold and empty — no hatred, no passion, only observation and action.

## 3. The Void
- Born when the Ancient Ones manipulated the **Celestian Pact** (a civilization pursuing ascension) into a **corrupted ascension ritual**.
- ⭐ **It never sheds its Celestian "traditional sense":** because the Void *is* corrupted Celestians, it **remains bound by Celestian customs and rites** — most importantly **Lok'Sha O'Ka** (which *originated* in the Celestian Pact). The Void **cannot refuse** such a rite — which is how **Xylaris forced the Hive Mind into a duel** (see Lok'Sha O'Ka).
- **Inhabits hosts intact** (doesn't merely destroy); a single **central hive mind** controls everything it takes. At peak: 80–90% of the galaxy corrupted.
- **Without a hive-mind host** it only spreads passively and searches; the longer it goes hostless, the **stronger its grip on the next host**.
- **Its one weakness: raw emotional intensity.** Love, hate, grief, rage strong enough to resist the homogenizing pull can hold corruption at bay. A strong-willed host can be corrupted yet never *controlled* → a stalemate where the Void owns the body but cannot drive it. **This is the mechanical heart of the universe.**
- ⭐ **Corruption = oblivion of the soul (the stakes):** to be **fully Void-corrupted is to be *damned*** — the corrupted can **never release their life essence back into the universe** (no rebirth, no return). At death they are **erased into nothingness, as if they never existed.** *(This is why Scourge's freed-but-corrupted soldiers are the **Army of the Damned** — §14.)* **The only being ever to transcend it is Taryn** (her willpower kept her *essence* uncorrupted even as the Hive Mind) — see §6.
- ⭐ **Visual marker — blood color:** **uncorrupted blood runs BLUE**; **Void-corrupted blood runs PURPLE.** So purple blood (and purple-shadow flesh) is a **corruption tell** — e.g., the artifact's "purple, shadowy zombie" creatures. *(Manuscript standardized: normal Obsidians bleed blue in Book 1.)*

## 4. Xylaris, Obscurum & the Betrayal
- Xylaris used conduit energy on his homeworld **Obscurum** to **seal the Ancient Ones** — the **first imprisonment of the Void**. The cost: it destroyed his world and **killed his own wife and child** (his foundational grief).
- **Starwalker then betrayed him** — having seen the power of an equal, he **framed Xylaris** for destroying a world (actually a Void containment op), imprisoned him in the **Thal'kyr**, and diminished him (grayed/cracked skin, **red Sith-like eyes**, most conduit access stripped).
- The **Thal'kyr** began as a stasis chamber; now a full **gravspace-capable ship** built around it. Starwalker releases Xylaris only when a crisis exceeds his own ability, then re-imprisons him. A **Maintainer cannot refuse an emergency** once released.
- Xylaris fights with **kinetic rifles, scatter rifles, and hidden conduit blades**; calls Starwalker **"the Betrayer."** Personal AI: **Aetheris** (true capabilities hidden from Starwalker). ⭐ **Aetheris knows the Voss lineage** and is the AI that "survived alongside Xylaris, **tracking the Vos bloodline across the gap**" (§7). **Currently:** while **Xylaris sleeps in gravspace stasis** (Thal'kyr), **Aetheris is the active watcher** — guarding the **Void prison** and observing the galaxy evolve from the ship in gravspace. Features extensively in the deep-past (TikTok) lore. ✅ **Aetheris is NOT Elena Voss's AI "Athena"** (author-confirmed — separate entities).
- To the Primordial Architects (who never learn the truth): **Starwalker = "the Exalted One," Xylaris = "the Disgraced One."**

## 5. Harbingers & Codexes
- **Harbingers** are guardians, not equals. Xylaris forged the **first five** from the Celestian Pact members who **refused** corruption.
- New Harbingers are created via **Codexes** — sentient ancient tomes that choose guardians by merit. ⭐ **Each Codex chooses FIVE Harbingers** (Amos's five = one Codex's set). With ~**50 Codexes** ("50 relics"), there are **many fives** scattered across worlds and eras — a vast, dispersed order of Harbingers.
- ⭐ **In the far future (ForgePulse), Codexes are not recognized for what they are — they appear as mysterious "artifacts" / "relics"** (Clarke's Third Law again). The artifact **Elena Voss** studies (Ch13) and the **corrupted artifact** the *Pillar of Anguish* excavates under New Eridanus are **Codexes** (the latter a *corrupted* one); the ForgePulse **"50 relics" = Codexes.**
- ⭐ **Who knows about the Voss lineage:** *only* (a) those a **Codex chooses** (i.e., Harbingers) and (b) **Aetheris**, Xylaris's AI. Nobody else — not even most of the Voss-descended — knows what they carry.
- **The corruption of two (expanded):** the Hive Mind (Taryn) corrupted two of the first five — **Orek → Scourge** and **Jarek → Abyss** — granting them **Void power layered on top of Harbinger power.** ⚔️ **The Lok'Sha O'Ka vs. the Hive Mind:** **Xylaris challenged the Hive Mind** to the ancient rite; per its rules the **invoked party may field champions in their stead**, so the Hive Mind sent her two corrupted Harbingers to fight **Xylaris and Gralthar** (the Or'uk). **Xylaris destroyed Abyss (Jarek) with conduit energy** in that duel; **Scourge broke free of the Hive Mind** there — keeping the blended Void+Harbinger power — and **fled the galaxy.** *(⚠️ Supersedes older lore: Scourge's true name is **Orek**, not "Jerrick"; Abyss's is **Jarek**, not "Jared.")*
- **Scourge today (Orek) — active wildcard:** the universe's antihero/redeemed-Arthas. For ~65M years he has **created Void-corrupted-but-*sentient* soldiers** — subjects **cut off from the Hive Mind**, so they **keep their own will yet gain Void power.** He now runs a galaxy-wide operation across the **Reforged galaxy**: **remote sites on ~20% of planets (including Earth)** where he corrupts **unwilling subjects**; **successes are taken to his world to train, failures are destroyed.** ⭐ **This army is being built *for the Grand Convergence*** — Scourge eventually brings his sentient Void-corrupted soldiers to fight alongside **Xylaris** and the assembled **Harbingers** against the real enemy (see THE GRAND CONVERGENCE).
- **Kar'yan replaces Abyss:** after his Reckoning sacrifice, **Kar'yan's life essence was captured and reborn as a Harbinger to fill Abyss's vacated slot** among the five (see §7).
- **Eldrakken's fall — the Betrayer's origin (⭐ deep-past ↔ fantasy bridge):** another of the original five, **Eldrakken**, witnessed Scourge's blended Void+Harbinger power and **tried to replicate it on himself.** He **failed** — corrupting himself and dooming the rest. Corrupted, **Eldrakken slew the remaining Harbingers — all except Kar'yan, who feigned death and escaped.** Eldrakken then **took a Codex** and used it to reach **Amos** in the fantasy era, infiltrating the **new Chosen** as "**Eldrin.**" *(This is exactly the Prologue myth — "one Harbinger… struck down his brethren"; "the betrayer shall be undone.")*
- **Both survivors converge on Amos:** *Dawn of the Chosen* secretly holds **both** survivors of that massacre — **Kar'yan** (as "**Caelum**," who delivers a Codex to Amos) and **Eldrakken** (as "Eldrin," the corrupted Betrayer). The original-Harbinger war's last two, on opposite sides, meeting at the new beginning.

## 6. Primordial Architects Era
- The cosmic engineers of the long age after the betrayal.
- **Supreme Commander Mojah Din'Sha** — lived ~5,000 years.
- **Etherspace** = the **first** FTL medium discovered in this era — and the **most efficient.** **Gravspace** = the **second**, discovered later: beyond FTL travel, it lets a being remain **stagnant, separated from the current dimension** (outside normal space/time). ⭐ **This dimensional-separation property is how Xylaris escaped the Symphony of Annihilation (the Reckoning) AND keeps the Void imprisoned** — he holds the prison from **gravspace stasis aboard the Thal'kyr.** *(The prison isn't perfect — the Void got it cracked slightly, hence the leaks/whispers.)*
- **The Or'uk** — a species the **Void cannot assimilate**; survived the Reforging. **Gralthar** (Or'uk) fought alongside Xylaris on the warship **Bloodforged** — and **stood beside Xylaris in the Lok'Sha O'Ka against the Hive Mind**, facing her two corrupted-Harbinger champions (Scourge & Abyss). **Kuja-Wo'Shuk** shares the lineage that later produces **Vrakar and Drakken**.
- **The fatal mistake:** the Architects found Xylaris's seal on Obscurum, **mistook it for a Celestian artifact, and released the Ancient Ones** — believing they were recovering relics. This corrupted **Taryn Voss**, who became **the Hive Mind**. *(The galaxy burned because an institution lied to about its own history made an honest mistake.)*
- ⭐ **The Pre-Reckoning War Council** *(source: TikTok transcript, `source-transcripts/PA-pre-reckoning-council.md`):* the final council where the Reckoning is planned. **Xylaris** lays out the Void's true nature to **Mojah Din'Sha, Kuja-Wo'Shuk, Kar'yan Voss,** and **Gralthar**, with **Aetheris** advising. *(Confirms the transcript's "Moja Dinsah" = canonical **Mojah Din'Sha**.)*
  - ⭐ **Void-nature refinement (cf. §3):** the Void is **NOT evil — it is *deluded*,** which Xylaris calls *more* dangerous. It does not hunger; **it seeks *union*,** believing that consuming all existence fulfills a **directive written into its essence by the Ancient Ones** ("the hands once mistaken for gods"). The Celestians first consumed were not evil; the evil is the Ancient Ones' directive. *(Thematic rhyme with the Dra'Kor Vhel'Tan — a true-believer enemy convinced its atrocity is right; "honor/righteousness is perception" scales from a saboteur cell to a god-eating Void.)*
  - ⭐ **Assimilation clock:** Aetheris gives **six months to full galactic assimilation, eight at most.**
  - ⭐ **The stakes if they fail:** the Void breaches **the Veil** (the interdimensional boundary holding the Ancient Ones) and **awakens them** — *"what we now call death will be mercy."*

## 7. The Reckoning — 65 Million Years Ago
*(a.k.a. the Symphony of Annihilation)*
- A chain of **supernovas** triggered by the Architects to imprison the Void by destroying its hosts.
- **Kar'yan Voss** (human warrior) stands at its center; his wife **Taryn** was the Hive Mind host — he had to ignite the supernova believing it would erase her.
- ⭐ **The plan / mechanism** *(source: PA council transcript, §6):*
  - **Kar'yan Voss** volunteers to pilot the **Eternal Blade** into the stellar core of **Solaris Magna** and trigger **Ignis Protocol**, igniting the supernova chain — *"the **Cascade**"* (= the Symphony of Annihilation). He flies to the epicenter knowing it kills him. *(Distinct from the two arks Eos/Gaios he launches first.)*
  - ⭐ **Two-pronged deception (Mojah Din'Sha's design):** the Architects stage a **loud, full battle as a spectacle** to mask movement in the stars, **while the true strike lands in shadow** (the Eternal Blade) — so the Void doesn't sense the real play.
  - ⭐ **The escape:** once Ignis fires, **the Void is drawn to the Cascade's gravitational pull**, unable to resist — and in that window **Xylaris's group enters Etherspace (Aetheris guiding), then crosses into Gravspace** (his eventual prison-holding stasis, §6/§4).
  - ⭐ **Aetheris's tolerance:** synchronization must fall within **±3 milliseconds** or the cascade fails.
  - ⭐ **The three Harbingers:** at the council a rift opens and **three Harbingers** (Celestian-Pact refusers Xylaris forged, §5) arrive unbidden to stand with him *"until the last star burns."* ✅ **Count locked (author-confirmed):** of the **original five**, the Hive Mind corrupted two — **Jarek → Abyss** (destroyed by Xylaris in the Lok'Sha O'Ka) and **Orek → Scourge** (connection to the Hive Mind severed; **fled the galaxy still retaining his blended Void+Harbinger power**, §58). So **four Harbingers technically still exist** (Scourge among them) — but **two fewer on Xylaris's fighting front**, leaving the **three loyal** who arrive at the council. No contradiction with "first five."
  - 📝 **Epigraph candidate — Kar'yan's closing line:** *"In the vastness of space, our greatest challenge isn't the distance between stars — but the darkness that lurks between them."*
- ⭐ **Taryn's transcendence & survival (author canon; reader learns eventually):** her **ridiculous willpower** against the Hive Mind meant she was **never corrupted *enough* for her life essence to be corrupted** — making her **the only being ever to escape the Void's oblivion** (cf. §3, corruption = damnation). She did **not** simply die: **the Infinites extracted her.** ✅ **Why (answered):** a **lesser being who fought off a *god-like* entity** (the Hive Mind / the Void) by sheer will **proved worthy of elevation into the Infinites' own hierarchy** — an honor they grant *very* rarely and *very* carefully. **Taryn is now part of the Infinite hierarchy:** not as mighty as an *original* Infinite, but **likely now more powerful than the Creator and Maintainer themselves (Starwalker & Xylaris).** She **fully knows Kar'yan is now a Harbinger** — but **Kar'yan does NOT know she survives anywhere.** *(A devastating buried thread: the foundational couple both alive across the gap — she now a near-Infinite power, watching; he believing her gone. It mirrors, and may pay off through, Junior + Lyrana.)*
- Before detonation he launched two arks out of the galaxy (ship-names = AI-names):
  - **Eos** — all Primordial Architect knowledge (the past preserved).
  - **Gaios** — life essences of every species to be reseeded (the future enabled). ⭐ **The reseed deliberately threaded the Voss lineage through *every* reseeded species** — even though the **original Voss line was human.** So the **Harbinger bloodline survives latent across *all* the Primordial-Architect peoples** (humans *and* every Obsidian race), not just humanity. *This is the mechanism that lets an Obsidian like **Lyrana** carry Taryn's Voss branch — the line was distributed at the reseed, then descended through each species' generations (no direct children of Kar'yan/Taryn required).*
  - **Modern humanity exists because of both.**
- Kar'yan died and **ascended → a new Harbinger of the Ethereal Veil** (his essence captured and **reborn to replace the slain Abyss** among the original five). Xylaris escaped into Gravspace carrying the imprisoned Void → **Thal'kyr stasis** (his AI **Aetheris** tracking the **Vos bloodline** across the gap and guarding the prison). Skorge fled.

## 9. Era I — *Harbingers of the Ethereal Veil* (Fantasy)
*A few million years post-Reforge. Told entirely in a fantasy register because its people cannot perceive the underlying technology (Clarke's-Third-Law epistemology).* Architects appear as gods; the Ethereal Codex as a sentient tome; the enemy are the **Dark Brethren** — ✅ **who ARE the Void itself.** Meira's people have no way to perceive its true nature, so they name the corruption/its thralls "Dark Brethren" for what it *looks* like. (So the fantasy era's "demon cult" is the same hive-mind parasite that powers ForgePulse and the modern era — the reader who knows the cosmology sees it; the characters never can.)
- **Meira** (Amos's world) sits in a **gravitationally slowed temporal frame** — feels like deep past, but runs **concurrent with 2888**. When far-future humans crack Gravspace, the strain leaks Void whispers into Meira's slow-time frame (why the Void "appears in the wrong era").
- **The Five Chosen (true Harbingers):** **Amos** (young blacksmith, first-Marked, Codex bearer); **Torin** (dwarf; apprentice + unspoken father/son bond with Amos); **Lyra** (elf song-weaver); **Nara** (sylph from Aeris, second-Marked, took a blade for Amos); and **Ishkandir** — the sole **Or'uk** (Gralthar's species), a living bridge to the pre-Reforging age. ✅ **Author ruling: Ishkandir is the fifth Chosen.**

Tied to the **Highborne Elves "bound to the Void."** *(He is the false sixth, not a true Harbinger.)*

## 11. Era III — *Project ForgePulse*, 2888 (Cyberpunk Military) — **THE BOOK WE ARE EDITING**
*Planet **Trident**, 2888. Humanity attempts **Neural Ascension** — the same reach that destroyed the Celestian Pact, now via technology instead of ritual. Same rule applies: whatever ascends must stay anchored in attachment or become the next Void.*

### The war
- Opens before the public knows an invasion is coming. **Praetorians** = humanity's elite warrior caste; the saga follows their **systematic attrition** (à la Noble Team, *Halo: Reach*). Two fireteams the reader just met are wiped almost immediately; from there, deaths come one by one. **By the end of the larger arc, every Praetorian deployed to Trident is gone.**
- **Fireteam Valor** are the central characters. **Lieutenant Argus makes the absolute final stand** (the fall of Trident) — the only reason any survivors escape, including **his son and Tessa**.

### Mind Reavers & Neural Ascension
- **Neural Ascension Protocol** **awakens *dormant* Mindreaver (conduit) ability in subjects who currently have none** → conduit users (a *diminished* inheritance: foresight + small telekinesis). Humanity doesn't know it carries this (doesn't know it descends from the reseeded). *(Distinct from Clandestine **nano-augmentation**, which only super-charges baseline physical/cognitive ability and never touches Mindreaver powers.)*
  - *(Terminology: the lore-bible base-tier term "Mind Rivers" is **superseded** — the manuscript's **"Mindreaver"** is canonical for the psychic users. See Story Bible / Discrepancy Report.)*
- Two paths: a high-end **Mindreaver** can undergo Neural Ascension to become far more powerful; or the protocol can try to awaken a no-ability person (extremely dangerous, usually fatal — used anyway under a losing war).
- Run by **GRID**; the more ethical successor to **Project Clandestine** (which produced fractured operators like **Strickland** and **the Surgeon**).
  - **Project Clandestine — details (from Strickland's GRID file):** an older, darker GRID program (sub-ops include **Project Phantom**; field formation **Clandestine Battlegroup Alpha**). ⭐ **GRID operatives are ALL (latent) Mindreavers** — GRID selects the **most powerful**, identified **young** (Mindreaver ability shows as early as **age 2**). It **takes such children** (Strickland at age **4**), strips their identity (designation **CD-MR10-01**), and **nano-augments them physically** (~age 12: speed, reflexes, self-healing + undisclosed) — then codenames them by deeds (Strickland = **"Demon"** after **Operation Blood Drive**, age 15). Operators get **cover identities** for "dirty work" (Demon → "Lt. John Strickland," Death Platoon leader). ✅ **The two augment programs (author-confirmed):** **Nano-augmentation** *(Clandestine)* super-charges **baseline physical/cognitive ability** ("Spartan-II on crack") — it does **not** grant or affect Mindreaver powers; those are **innate.** **Neural Ascension** *(later program)* **awakens *dormant* Mindreaver ability in subjects who have none.** So **Strickland/Demon is a Mindreaver who is *also* nano-augmented in his physical abilities.**
  - ⭐ **GRID designation code (`CD-MR##-##`):** **CD** = Clandestine Division · **MR** = Mindreaver · first number = **Mindreaver power level (1–10; 10 = max)** · second number = **chronological order "tactically acquired"** by GRID. So **Strickland = `CD-MR10-01`** → **maximum power (10) AND the very *first* acquisition** (GRID's foundational, top-tier operative). The **Surgeon = `CD-MR07-17`** → power 7, 17th acquired — his "**Amplified** Mindreaver" status is a base **7 boosted** above its natural ceiling. *(The on-image "CD-MW10-01" was a typo; canon is **MR**.)*
  - ⭐ **Before they earn a codename, they're called only by their acquisition number** (the dehumanizing tell — a kidnapped child is just a number until violence earns him a name): **Strickland was "Zero One"** (01); **the Surgeon was "One Seven"** (17). The codename (Demon, Surgeon) comes later, by deeds.

### The Obsidians (true nature)
- **Terminology:** the polity is the **Obsidian Empire** (a.k.a. the Obsidian dominion); its military is the **Obsidian Armada**. ("Obsidians" = the people/coalition.) **Armada core values: honor, strength, unity.**
- **Not a single species — a pact of FIVE alien races**, each from its own star system, **forged into one people by Vrakar in the War of the Five Suns** (each "sun" = one species' home system, fought and united). **They are the Primordial-Architect-descended reseeded peoples — *all of them except humans.*** *(Reconciliation: cleanest reading — the principal reseeded lineages are the **five Obsidian races + humans**; humans are the one left out. The master lore-bible's older "~80–90% of those reseeded" phrasing is refined by this. Five-vs-"all" exact composition still flagged TBD.)*

> **⭐⭐ THE HIDDEN THROUGHLINE — the Void is separating humanity (author canon, reader does NOT know):** The Obsidians are **every Primordial-Architect-descended reseeded race *except humans.*** In ForgePulse, the Void's *real* objective — concealed beneath the holy-crusade and artifact pretexts — is to **isolate humanity from that coalition.** It corrupted **Vrakar** to **burn the intended human–Obsidian alliance** and manufacture the war *specifically to cut humans off from the rest of the reseeded peoples.* **Likely why:** the **Voss/Harbinger bloodline** — the Void's natural counter — was deliberately reseeded **latent across *all* species**, but **humans are the original line and the ones actively awakening it** (Neural Ascension; Junior becomes a Harbinger). By **isolating humans** from the Obsidian-carried branches, the Void keeps the lineage **fragmented and weak** — preventing the branches from **re-converging** into restored Harbinger strength. So the crusade and the artifact are **covers**; the war is a **separation gambit.** ⭐ This is exactly what **Lyrana & Junior's secret peace works against** — re-uniting human and Obsidian is the one outcome the Void exists to prevent. *(Visible in the author's 65-My TikTok lore: the Primordial-Architect species shown there are the very races who become the Obsidians.)*
