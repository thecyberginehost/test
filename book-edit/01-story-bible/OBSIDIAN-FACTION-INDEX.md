# THE OBSIDIANS — Faction One-Pager (Index)
*At-a-glance roster for the Obsidian Empire. Full prose, reconciliations, and source notes live in `REFORGED-UNIVERSE-LORE.md` §11 + the Obsidian deep-lore block; manuscript conflicts in `DISCREPANCY-REPORT.md`. Art in `assets/`.*

**What they are:** the **Obsidian Empire** — a pact of **five alien races** (minus humans), forged into one people by **Highlord Vrakar** in the **War of the Five Suns** (each "sun" = a species' home star system). Military = the **Obsidian Armada**. Core values: **honor, strength, unity.** Unknowingly carry **Celestian ritual vocabulary** (Vor'kara, Lok'Sha O'Ka). **The real power behind them is the Void** — they are corrupted instruments, not the true threat.

## History (Obsidian timeline)
1. **War of the Five Suns** (~50 yrs ago) — Vrakar unites the five races. Climax: Kyr + Lyrana's sabotage + the Umbral Hand's ion storm → Vrakar wins the **Lok'Sha O'Ka** duel vs. the Seleron general.
2. **Kresh Rebellion** (~50 yrs ago) — the Kresh rise, are crushed (Kyr's Titan Vanguard / Drakken's Dra'Kor Vhel'Tan — *lead TBD*), then bound by the **Iron Accord**.
3. **The Dark Purge** — Drakken invokes Lok'Sha O'Ka to seize the Blackguard from a High Commander too deferential to Vrakar.
4. **Sacred Retribution** (2888) — the holy war on humanity (Trident); the corrupted crusade. Drakken's Blackguard + Kyr's Titan Vanguard + the Kresh secure New Eridanus; **Charles's nuke wipes a Titan Vanguard division** → epilogue.

## Customs & Terms
- **Lok'Sha O'Ka** — ancient challenge to a **duel to the death**; used vs. enemies *and* as an in-ranks leadership challenge (merit over birth). Highborn Guard exempt. Recognized even by the Seleron.
- **Vor'kara / Val'kora** — greeting-of-respect pair: **Vor'kara** = initial; **Val'kora** = the reciprocation (if honored back). (Junior's Ch01 radio intercept = Val'kora.)
- **Iron Accord** — Kyr's code binding the Kresh. **Krath'Zaroth** — flagship ("Sacred Retribution"). **Pillar of Anguish** — the New Eridanus warship (survives, excavating).

## ⭐ The Cross-Saga Bridge
**Junior ↔ Lyrana.** The human envoy in Lyrana's secret peace talks is **Junior** (Argus's son, the Harbinger). Human line (Argus→Junior) meets Obsidian royal line (Vrakar→Lyrana): the two heirs secretly seek the peace their fathers were meant to build — while Junior is on a collision course with the corrupted Vrakar.
