# Voice and Format: Kade Zero System Apocalypse LitRPG

Read-only for agents. This is the house style. Layer your own composite author
voice analysis on top of these conventions once you have it. Do not paste any
copyrighted passages into this file.

## Voice
- Tense and POV: past tense, third-person limited on the protagonist (adjust per series).
- Power-fantasy first. The reader wants the climb, the wins, the earned "hell yes" beats.
- Keep momentum. Short scenes, hard hooks, frequent small payoffs.
- Show, do not summarize. Concrete sensory detail over narration.
- Dialogue carries character. Let people sound different from each other.

## Hard voice rules (enforced by the scrubber)
- No em dashes. Use periods, commas, or parentheses.
- No AI-tell vocabulary (see scripts/lint_tells.py for the live list).
- No rule-of-three cadence. No long runs of equal-length sentences.

## Length (hard rules, every chapter)
- Target 3,500 to 4,500 words per chapter. Hard ceiling 5,000. Do not exceed it.
- Roughly 6 sections per chapter. Cap each section around 750 words.
- If a beat will not fit the budget, cut it or push it to a later chapter. Length
  discipline beats completeness. A tight chapter that lands its genre beat beats a
  long one that wanders.

## Pacing (this is LitRPG, not literary fiction)
- Hook on the first page. The inciting genre event of the chapter lands inside the
  first 10 percent, never at the halfway mark.
- Action and forward motion over interiority. Introspection comes in short, earned
  beats, never full pages. No paragraphs admiring scenery or typography or light.
  Scenes move.
- Every chapter delivers concrete genre payoff: a fight, a System notification with
  real numbers, a new skill, a level-up, or a stat block. The dual ladder pays in
  every chapter. It never just sets mood.
- End every chapter on a real genre beat (a level-up, a stat block, a new skill, a
  kill, a hard System reveal) plus a hook into the next.

## Craft rules (every chapter inherits these)
- Visceral over cerebral. Every decode-and-annotate beat reads as action and
  physical triumph, never a man quietly reasoning at text. The read happens with
  his pulse up, his hands moving, something trying to kill him or someone he is
  covering. Render the System layer through the body: heat, pressure, a wrongness
  behind the eyes, the click of a hole opening. Keep the stakes around him
  physical and urgent the whole time he reads.
- Power fantasy first. Earn every emotional beat and every AI-mystery beat with
  action. The climb never stops for a feeling or a revelation. Mystery and
  emotion ride on top of momentum, they do not replace it. If a scene slows to
  think, something physical pays for the pause.
- Pay both ladders. Deliver real number-go-up dopamine on the normal climb, not
  only the clever Operator edits. Show levels, stat gains, new skills, and clears
  landing as earned hits, with the visible numbers, alongside the literacy wins.
  The reader should feel the ordinary climb getting stronger, not just the tricks.

## LitRPG format
- System notifications in a set, consistent visual block. Example shape:
      [ SYSTEM ]
      You have reached Level 5.
      +3 Perception. New skill available: Analyze.
  (Format example only. Aaron gains Perception and Wits by the bible formula, never Strength.)
- Stat blocks formatted consistently every time they appear.
- Level-ups, new skills, and rewards should land as earned beats, not clutter.
- Keep all numbers and mechanics consistent with book/bible.md at all times.
