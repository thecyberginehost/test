"Three patches ... and not one had reached for the decode tax" (L35) and "looked

- (b) Drop the middle item and rewrite the count as two patch events (PATCH 0007,
  PATCH 0011), adjusting "three times" -> "twice" on L35 and L37.
Either fixes the contradiction; (a) is the writer's apparent intent but currently has
no bible backing.
