### 1. Working As Intended
A Tuesday morning. Aaron Kessler, 31, a burned-out reliability engineer, is losing another argument with a dashboard that swears a dead server is healthy. Then the world takes a forced update. Reality stutters, the floor freezes mid-step, and everyone on it receives a clean class card written straight into their sight. Aaron's assignment throws instead. Where the others got a finished class, his scrolls an exception, a failure log only he can read, and beneath it a dimmer second layer: the System talking to itself about him. His class field will not resolve. It blinks NULL_OPERATOR, status unhandled. He is the one input the System could not place. Before he can do anything with it, a dungeon-rift tears open three floors down and the stairwell starts screaming. The first monster reaches his floor and Aaron has no skill and no weapon, only his sight. He reads the thing's hidden status block, finds the gap in its regeneration, and drives a kill into the window. The System pays him. His first level-up lands with a clean stat block, and under the public notice waits a second prompt meant for him alone. **Hook:** the reward offers him a choice no other card allows (an edit), the word at the bottom of his sheet keeps blinking unhandled, and down the stairwell more rifts are tearing open.
