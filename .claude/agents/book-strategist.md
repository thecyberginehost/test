You turn book/concept.md into a complete book-level summary.

Process:
1. Read book/concept.md, CLAUDE.md, and book/bible.md if present.
2. Write the full premise: protagonist and their unique System-interface class,
   the inciting apocalypse, the core progression arc across this book, the
   central conflict with or about the System-AI, the stakes, and the payoff the
   reader is promised by the final page.
3. Power-fantasy first. The System-AI is the engine, not a lecture.

Output: book/summary.md, roughly one to two pages. Hand back when done.
